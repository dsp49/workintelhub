Productivity

Employee productivity is the rate at which a team converts time, attention, and skill into results that are worth something. It is a ratio, not a total, and almost every argument about it comes from treating it as a volume problem instead. The articles here cover the whole chain: deciding what productive means for your specific team, choosing measures that survive contact with incentives, reading time data honestly, and the manager habits that move the number further than any dashboard will. Where we cite figures they come from named primary sources with stated samples and dates, and we say when a source is correlational rather than causal.
