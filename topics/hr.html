HR Guides and Templates

Human resources is where the employment relationship is written down: the offer, the forms, the policies, the leave, the warning, the separation. Every step has a document and most have a deadline set by federal or state law. The guides here give you the document, built by a generator from your own terms, and the rule behind it, linked to the statute or the agency guidance. Where a figure is quoted it comes from a named source with a date, and where the law differs by state we say which states and what changes.
