Time Management

Time management is the set of individual habits that decide what gets your attention and in what order. Most advice on it is written for a calendar nobody has. Microsoft's 2025 Work Trend Index found people are interrupted roughly every two minutes during work hours, which is the condition any technique has to survive. The articles here take that seriously. Each method is written with the situation where it breaks, because a technique that works for a solo writer often collapses on a support rota where the queue decides your order rather than you.
