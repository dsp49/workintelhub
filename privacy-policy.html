Privacy Policy

Last updated August 6, 2026

This policy describes what actually happens when you visit workintelhub.com. It is written against how the site is currently built rather than from a template, and it will be updated when that changes. Questions go to info@workintelhub.com.

What we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect names, postal addresses, or payment details, because there is nothing to buy here and nothing to log into. We do not sell or rent data about visitors to anyone.

About the subscribe box

The subscribe form on this site is not currently connected to anything. It does not send, store, or transmit the address you type, and no mailing list exists yet. We are saying so explicitly because a form that looks functional but is not could otherwise leave you believing you had signed up for something. If we connect it to a mail provider we will update this policy and name that provider before it goes live.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which articles people read and how they arrive. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes information including your IP address, an approximate location derived from it, your browser and device, the pages you view, and the site or search that referred you. We use this in aggregate to decide what to write next. We do not attempt to identify individual visitors and we do not combine analytics data with anything else.

Google acts as our processor for this and handles the data under its own terms. You can opt out across all sites using Google's browser add-on, or block analytics cookies in your browser or with an extension. This site works normally without them.

Other third parties involved in loading this site

  • Google Fonts. Typography is loaded from Google's font servers, which means Google receives a request from your browser, including your IP address, when a page loads. No cookie is set by this.
  • Cloudflare. The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes requests in order to deliver pages and protect the site.
  • Google Search. The search box in the header does not search this site directly. It opens a Google search restricted to workintelhub.com in a new tab, which means your search term is sent to Google and is covered by Google's privacy policy rather than this one.

Links inside our articles point to outside sites such as research publishers and government pages. Once you follow one, that site's privacy policy applies, not ours.

Cookies

The only cookies this site sets are those used by Google Analytics, described above. We set no advertising cookies, and there are no advertising or social media tracking pixels anywhere on this site. You can delete or block cookies in your browser at any time without losing access to anything here.

If you email us

When you write to info@workintelhub.com we keep the message so we can reply and, where it is a correction, so there is a record of what changed and why. We do not add correspondents to any mailing list.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you, and to object to its processing. Because we hold almost nothing, in practice this usually concerns analytics data held by Google or an email you have sent us. Write to info@workintelhub.com and we will explain what exists and act on the request.

Children

This site is written for people managing teams at work. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we add anything that collects data, such as a working newsletter, comments, or embedded media, we will update this page and change the date at the top before that feature goes live.
