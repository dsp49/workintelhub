Clockify Pricing (2026): Plans, Kiosk Seats and Free Limits

P Pavan
November 9, 2026 · 10 min read

Clockify costs $3.99, $5.49, $7.99 or $11.99 per seat per month on annual billing for Basic, Standard, Pro and Enterprise, or $4.99, $6.99, $9.99 and $14.99 month to month, with a Free plan for up to five users. Kiosk-only seats for hourly staff who clock in at a shared device cost a fifth of those prices, from $0.79. The Free plan was cut back in April 2026 to five users, 31-day reports and PDF export, which is the change most people searching for Clockify pricing are trying to understand.

This page sets out the November 2026 prices for every tier and the kiosk seats, what the Free plan still includes, a feature-by-feature table, the billing rules that change what you pay, what teams of 5, 15 and 50 actually spend, the price history, the common complaints and when a competitor is cheaper. Clockify is the lowest-priced full-featured tracker in the category; the question is which tier you actually need.

The kiosk-only seat is the detail most buyers miss. A "limited user" has no email login and can only clock in and out at a shared kiosk device, and is billed at a fifth of the full-seat price on the same plan. A restaurant with three managers and 30 hourly staff on Standard pays for three full seats ($16.47) and 30 limited seats ($35.70), or $52.17 a month on annual billing, rather than $181.17 for 33 full seats.

What the Free plan still includes

On April 21, 2026 CAKE.com announced changes to the Free plan that took effect in late April and May. Free is now capped at five active users, including the owner, and lost the kiosk, billable rates and project amounts, limited users, shared report links, project time estimates and CSV and Excel export, all of which moved to Basic. Reports on Free can cover at most 31 days at a time and export only to PDF; the API is limited to 30 requests an hour per workspace and three webhooks. Existing paid customers were not affected and paid prices did not change.

What remains is still a working tracker for a team of up to five: unlimited time entries, projects, clients and tags; the timer, timesheet view, auto tracker and calendar; the Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android and browser-extension apps; Pomodoro, idle detection and reminders; every integration except QuickBooks; and team activity. Anyone who needs a kiosk, billable amounts, a report older than a month in Excel, or a sixth user now pays $3.99 per seat. The change is why Clockify's Trustpilot score fell to 2.9 of 5 in October 2026 and why several thousand small teams went looking for Clockify alternatives.

Two tier boundaries decide most purchases. Approvals, invoicing, time off and attendance are all on Standard, so $5.49 is the realistic price for a managed team; Basic is for teams that need billable rates or a kiosk and nothing else. Scheduling and labor cost are on Pro, which is where agencies and field teams end up. The help center lists "screenshots" under Pro while the pricing page says "activity monitoring"; in practice the Pro monitor records apps and sites when a user opts in and does not capture images, which is why Clockify appears on our lists of tools without surveillance.

Billing rules that change the price

  • Seats, not usage. The bill is for every active or invited user, whether or not they tracked time that month. Deactivate leavers; deactivated users keep their data and free the seat.
  • Once paid, everyone is paid. The five free users do not carry over to a paid plan; a six-person team on Basic pays for six seats, not one.
  • Proration. Seats added mid-cycle are charged pro rata for the rest of the period. Annual plans are prepaid for a fixed seat count; seats removed from an annual plan are not refunded and reduce the next renewal. Upgrades take effect immediately with a prorated charge; a switch from annual to monthly waits until the annual term ends.
  • Trial. Every new workspace gets seven days of Pro features without a card. When it ends the workspace drops to Free, data is kept, and only the owner and the first four invitees stay active; later invitees are deactivated until you pay.
  • Cancellation. From Billing, Subscription, at any time; paid features run to the end of the period, then the workspace becomes Free with all data intact. No refunds except for billing errors.
  • Payment. Card for all plans; wire transfer or purchase order through sales for larger contracts. Currency follows location and can be switched; US buyers pay in dollars.
  • No discounts beyond the 20 percent annual saving. Clockify publishes no nonprofit, education or startup pricing, which is unusual in this category (Toggl and Harvest both do).
  • Dedicated support is by seat count rather than price: a success representative at 120 seats on Basic, 90 on Standard, 60 on Pro, 40 on Enterprise and 30 on the Bundle.

Price history

Clockify launched in 2017 as a free product with no paid tier, which is still how many people think of it. Paid plans arrived in 2018 and 2019 (the January 2019 changelog introduced a trial of what were then called "Premium features"), prepaid Enterprise seats in July 2020, and the current four-tier structure of Basic, Standard, Pro and Enterprise in 2021. The CAKE.com Bundle with Pumble and Plaky was added in April 2024, and paid third-party add-ons with per-seat pricing followed in August 2024 and November 2026. The list prices of $3.99, $5.49, $7.99 and $11.99 on annual billing have been stable through this period; Clockify documents no price increase, and its April 2026 Free-plan announcement states that paid prices did not change. The economically significant change was therefore not a price rise but the April 2026 narrowing of the Free plan, which moved kiosk, billable rates and exports to Basic and capped Free at five users.

What buyers complain about

The Free plan cut

Teams that built workflows on free kiosk clock-ins, billable reporting or Excel exports lost them in May 2026 with about a month's in-app notice. The complaints are about the change rather than the price, which at $3.99 is the lowest paid tier in the category.

Fixed by: budgeting $3.99 per full seat and $0.79 per kiosk seat, or moving to Jibble or TimeCamp if free is the constraint

Seat billing for people who do not track

Owners, admins and occasional users all cost a seat. A consultancy with four partners who never track and 10 staff who do pays for 14.

Fixed by: deactivating anyone who does not need to log in; deactivated users keep their history and cost nothing

Feature spread across four tiers

Approvals on one tier, scheduling on the next, SSO on the top one. Teams that need one feature from a higher tier pay the higher price for every seat.

Fixed by: pricing the whole team at the tier that includes every feature you need before comparing with competitors, where Standard usually still wins

Annual seats are not refundable

A company that prepays 50 annual seats and shrinks to 35 keeps paying for 50 until renewal.

Fixed by: buying annual seats for the headcount you are sure of and adding monthly seats on top, which Clockify prorates

If Clockify is not the right price

If you want it cheaper: Jibble is free for unlimited users with a kiosk and GPS, and TimeCamp's free plan has no user cap. If you want approvals without the tier structure: Harvest includes invoicing, approvals and expenses in one $11 plan. If adoption is the problem rather than price: Toggl Track's Starter at $9 is the simplest timer in the category, and our Toggl vs Clockify comparison sets the two against each other. If you want monitoring with the timesheets: Hubstaff Grow at $7.50 adds screenshots and activity levels, compared in Hubstaff vs Clockify. The full list is in Clockify alternatives.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Clockify cost per user?

$3.99 (Basic), $5.49 (Standard), $7.99 (Pro) or $11.99 (Enterprise) per seat per month on annual billing, or $4.99, $6.99, $9.99 and $14.99 month to month. Kiosk-only seats for staff who only clock in at a shared device cost $0.79 to $2.39. A CAKE.com bundle with Pumble and Plaky is $12.99 annual or $15.99 monthly.

Is Clockify still free?

Yes, for up to five users including the owner, with unlimited time entries and projects, all the apps and most integrations. Since April 2026 the Free plan limits reports to a 31-day range and PDF export, and no longer includes the kiosk, billable rates, limited users, shared reports or CSV and Excel export. Those moved to Basic at $3.99 per seat.

What changed in Clockify's free plan in 2026?

CAKE.com announced on April 21, 2026 that Free would be capped at five users and would lose the kiosk, billable hours, limited users, shared report links, project time estimates and CSV and Excel export, with reports limited to one month and the API to 30 requests an hour. The change took effect in late April and May 2026. Paid plans and prices did not change, and existing paid customers were unaffected.

Which Clockify plan has approvals and invoicing?

Standard, at $5.49 per seat on annual billing. Basic adds billable rates, the kiosk and exports but not approvals; Standard adds approvals, lock time, invoicing, time off, attendance and overtime, the manager role and the QuickBooks integration.

Does Clockify charge for every user?

Every active or invited user on the Team page uses a paid seat, including owners and administrators, whether or not they track time. Deactivated users do not. Kiosk-only limited users are billed at the reduced kiosk-seat rate rather than the full seat price.

Does Clockify offer a discount for nonprofits or schools?

No. The pricing page states that the 20 percent annual discount is the only one offered, and no nonprofit, education or startup pricing is published. Toggl Track and Harvest both offer nonprofit and education discounts if that matters.

Can I cancel Clockify and keep my data?

Yes. Cancel from Billing, Subscription at any time; paid features continue to the end of the billing period, after which the workspace drops to Free with all data kept. If the workspace has more than five users, the extra users are deactivated until you upgrade again. There are no refunds except for billing errors.

Is Clockify cheaper than Toggl Track?

Yes, at every tier. Clockify Standard with approvals and invoicing is $5.49 per seat; Toggl's approvals are on Premium at $16 per user. Clockify Basic at $3.99 compares with Toggl Starter at $9 for billable rates. Toggl's advantages are its simpler timer and its written policy against monitoring, not price.
