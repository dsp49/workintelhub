Top Workforce Intelligence Platforms: 8 Reviewed (2026)

P Pavan
October 6, 2026 · 14 min read

"Workforce intelligence" is the label vendors use when they want analytics to sound like a decision rather than a dashboard. Underneath it, every platform reads one of four kinds of data, and that decides what it can tell you. HR-record platforms (Visier, One Model, Workday) know who people are, what they are paid and when they leave. Work-pattern platforms (ActivTrak, Time Champ) know how time is spent on devices. Collaboration platforms (Viva Insights) know how people meet and message. Operational platforms (Legion, UKG) know demand, schedules and labour cost. None sees the whole picture; this page organises eight by the data they read so you buy for the question you have.

Pricing was checked in October 2026 and labelled as an estimate where quoted. The list is in data-source order rather than ranked. The workforce analytics review ranks a wider field, and the workplace analytics guide explains the collaboration-data category in depth.

The tools in detail

Visier People

Governed people data with predictive attrition and planning
Runs on Web Pricing (October 2026) Quote only. Priced by headcount and connected data sources on annual contracts; independent reports place typical mid-market deals above $50,000 a year and enterprise deployments with several modules well above that

What it is

The reference people analytics platform: governed data model, hundreds of metrics, predictive attrition and planning; quote only, 1,000-plus employees.

Why it is on this list

HR-record intelligence at enterprise scale: Visier connects every HR system into one governed model and surfaces turnover, hiring, pay, mobility and attrition risk with a natural-language assistant and planning. Quote only, sized for a thousand employees and up.

Where it is strong

  • Hundreds of pre-built metrics and questions
  • Governed people data model across sources
  • Predictive attrition and planning
  • Natural-language assistant

Where it falls short

  • Quote only; enterprise price
  • Sized for 1,000-plus employees
  • Implementation needs clean data

ActivTrak

Capacity, focus and burnout risk from activity
Runs on Windows, macOS, Chrome OS Pricing (October 2026) Free for 3 users; Essentials $10, Essentials Plus $15, Professional $17, Professional Plus $21 per user per month on annual billing

What it is

Activity-side analytics: capacity, utilisation, focus and burnout risk; free for three, from $10.

Why it is on this list

Work-pattern intelligence for desk-based teams: ActivTrak reads activity on work devices into capacity, utilisation, focus and collaboration time, after-hours work and burnout risk by team, with coaching dashboards. From $10 a user; it is monitoring.

The intelligence most organisations want, why good people leave, lives at the join between HR records and work patterns, and no single platform holds both well. The practical approach is an HR-record platform for the population view and a work-pattern or collaboration tool for the team view, with the join done by a human in a conversation; the retention strategies and burnout in the data guides show what that looks like.

Privacy, monitoring and AI rules

People analytics runs on personal data and the rules follow it: GDPR and the UK and EU works council regimes for European employees, the California Consumer Privacy Act's employee provisions, Illinois's biometric and AI-in-hiring laws, New York City's automated employment decision tool audit requirement, and the California automated decision rules for any model that affects employment decisions. Activity-side tools (ActivTrak, Time Champ, Viva Insights) are monitoring and need the monitoring policy and the notice several states require; the privacy line guide says what stays out of scope. Aggregate first, with minimum group sizes, and treat predictive attrition scores as a prompt for a conversation, never as a decision.

Reading a quote

Most of this category is quote-priced. Where I give a figure for a quote-only vendor it is the range independent buyer reports and procurement advisers describe, labelled as an estimate, not a price list; your quote will depend on headcount, data sources, modules and contract length. Published prices are stated as published.

How these tools were evaluated

I evaluated each tool against the same checklist: what it captures and how configurable that is, which platforms it runs on, how it treats the employee (visible or silent agent, access to their own data, screenshot blurring and private time), what the published pricing is and what the minimum commitment really costs, how long data is kept, where it can be deployed, and what the vendor's own documentation and verified user reviews say about reliability and support. Pricing was taken from each vendor's pricing page in October 2026; where a vendor publishes no price, the page says so rather than guessing. Free plans and trials were used where they exist. I did not run a formal benchmark, and the page does not claim one.

Key takeaways

  • Every workforce intelligence platform reads one kind of data: HR records, work patterns, collaboration or operations; buy for the question you have.
  • Visier, One Model and Workday for HR records; ActivTrak and Time Champ for work patterns; Viva Insights for collaboration; Legion and UKG for operations.
  • The question most organisations want answered sits at the join between HR records and work patterns, and a human does the join.
  • Activity and collaboration tools are monitoring; aggregate above minimum group sizes and publish the policy.

Frequently asked questions

What is a workforce intelligence platform?

Software that analyses data about a workforce to support decisions. The term is marketing rather than a category; underneath, each platform reads one kind of data: HR records (Visier, One Model, Workday), device work patterns (ActivTrak, Time Champ), collaboration metadata (Viva Insights) or operational demand and labour data (Legion, UKG). What it can tell you follows from which.

What are the top workforce intelligence platforms?

Visier for HR-record intelligence at enterprise scale, One Model for data teams, Workday People Analytics for Workday customers; ActivTrak and Time Champ for work-pattern intelligence on desk-based teams; Viva Insights for collaboration intelligence in Microsoft 365; Legion and UKG for operational intelligence on hourly workforces.

Can workforce intelligence software predict who will quit?

HR-record platforms (Visier, One Model, Workday) produce attrition-risk scores from tenure, pay, promotion history, manager changes and similar factors, and at population level they are useful for targeting retention effort. At individual level they are probabilities, not predictions, with legal and ethical limits on how they can be used; treat a score as a prompt for a conversation, not a decision.

Is workforce intelligence the same as employee monitoring?

Only for the work-pattern and collaboration tools (ActivTrak, Time Champ, Viva Insights), which read activity on devices or in Microsoft 365 and are monitoring under the laws of several states, with notice and policy obligations. HR-record and operational platforms analyse data the organisation already holds for payroll, HR and scheduling.

How much do workforce intelligence platforms cost?

Published: ActivTrak $10 to $21 per user, Time Champ $3.90 to $13.90, Viva Insights $6 add-on. Quoted: Visier (independent reports place mid-market deals above $50,000 a year), One Model (roughly $30,000 to $150,000), Legion (enterprise), and the Workday and UKG modules within their HCM contracts.

Which platform combines HR data with how work is done?

None does both well. Viva Insights joins Microsoft 365 collaboration metadata with HR attributes at aggregate level; UKG and Dayforce join HR with time and scheduling data; ActivTrak and Time Champ can import team and role attributes to segment activity data. For the full join, most organisations run an HR-record platform and a work-pattern tool and connect the findings in management conversations.
