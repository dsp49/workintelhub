Teramind Pricing (2026): Starter, UAM and DLP Explained

P Pavan
November 9, 2026 · 10 min read

Teramind costs $14 per seat per month for Starter, $28 for UAM and $32 for DLP on annual billing, or $15, $30 and $35 month to month, with a five-seat minimum on every plan and Enterprise by quote. All three tiers record screens continuously; what the higher prices buy is keystroke, email and file capture on UAM and content-aware data loss prevention on DLP. Cloud retention is six months for recordings and 18 months for activity data on every tier, and there is no mobile agent.

This page sets out the November 2026 prices and the five-seat floor, a feature table that shows where keystrokes, clipboard and OCR actually sit (two of them higher than most buyers assume), the add-ons and deployment options, the billing and licensing rules that decide the invoice, what teams of 3, 25, 100 and 500 pay, what is known of the price history, the recurring complaints and when another tool fits. Teramind is priced as security software, and it is the right buy only if you need what security software does.

Teramind's pricing page renders its prices with a script and shows "Pay yearly (Save 8%)" and a seat selector starting at five; the dollar figures above are the ones the page displays when rendered, as captured by a pricing tracker on November 9, 2026 and reported by independent reviewers the same month, and they are consistent with the stated 8 percent annual discount. Teramind's knowledge base confirms that Starter, UAM and DLP are "available on a monthly or annual subscription with a 5-seat minimum", that annual payments are made upfront and are non-refundable, and that monthly plans can be canceled at any time. Prices are the same for the cloud service whether you later deploy on Teramind's cloud, a private cloud or on-premises; the private and on-premises options are quoted through sales.

Five seats is the floor, so the smallest Teramind bill is $70 a month on Starter annual ($840 a year), $140 on UAM and $160 on DLP. A "seat" is a unique monitored user: a domain account used across several computers consumes one license, while local accounts on different machines each count separately.

Two features sit higher than buyers expect. OCR, which lets you search recordings for text that appeared on screen, is Enterprise only, not DLP. And clipboard monitoring is a DLP feature, not a UAM one. The practical boundary for most buyers is between Starter and UAM: Starter records screens and measures productivity, which is more monitoring than most time trackers offer, but it is UAM at twice the price that adds the keystroke and email capture that makes Teramind an investigation and insider-risk tool. Every Teramind tier records screens continuously by default, which an administrator can turn off; that is the opposite of ActivTrak, where screenshots are an alarm-triggered add-on.

Standard cloud retention is six months for screen recordings and audio and 18 months for telemetry (activity logs and metadata) on every tier, which Teramind's privacy policy also states. Organizations with longer regulatory retention requirements either buy the Compliance Bundle in six-month steps or deploy on-premises and keep what they like. There are no published nonprofit or education prices.

Billing rules that change the price

  • Five-seat minimum on every self-serve plan. A three-person team pays for five.
  • Monthly or annual. Monthly is billed on the sign-up date each month and can be canceled at any time; annual is paid upfront, renews on the anniversary and is non-refundable. The annual saving is about 8 percent, less than most competitors offer.
  • Seats are unique monitored users, not devices and not concurrent sessions. The Revealed Agent identifies a user by email with one concurrent login; the Hidden Agent identifies by the computer login. Shared local accounts on several machines each consume a seat; a domain account on several machines consumes one.
  • Trial. Seven days of the full DLP package on the cloud for up to five users, no card; extensions on request. On-premises and private cloud evaluations typically get a 14-day license.
  • Payment. Visa, Mastercard and American Express; bank transfer; checks for annual US contracts.
  • Platforms and their limits. Windows 10 and later including ARM, Windows Server 2016 and later, macOS Monterey 12.7.6 and later, and Linux (Ubuntu 24, Debian 13, RHEL 9) with reduced features on Mac and Linux; Citrix, VMware Horizon and terminal servers (about 30 users per terminal server). No ChromeOS, no mobile.
  • Hosting. The cloud service runs in Oracle Cloud data centers; Teramind states ISO 27001:2022 and SOC 2 Type II compliance.

Price history

Teramind has not published price changes and pricing trackers record none, so the history has to be assembled from third-party listings, which are undated and inconsistent: figures of roughly $11 to $12.50 per seat for Starter, $22.50 to $23 for UAM and $26 to $27 for DLP appear in older directory entries, against $14, $28 and $32 today, implying rises of about 20 percent at some point since the Starter tier was introduced. The company was founded in 2014, was based in Aventura, Florida, and now describes itself as fully remote with a registered address in Cheyenne, Wyoming; it launched an AI governance platform in March 2026 and signed a distribution agreement with Ingram Micro in April 2026. Treat the older figures as indicative only.

What buyers complain about

The five-seat minimum

Small teams and sole proprietors who want to monitor one or two machines pay for five.

Fixed by: accepting Starter at $70 a month as the floor, or using Hubstaff, Insightful or ActivTrak, which have no minimum or a free tier

Starter is not what the marketing implies

Buyers expect keystrokes and email capture because that is what Teramind is known for; they are on UAM at twice the price.

Fixed by: pricing UAM from the start if you need investigation-grade capture, and Starter only if screen recording and productivity analysis are enough

Short cloud retention for a security tool

Six months of recordings is less than many regulators and litigation holds require.

Fixed by: buying the Compliance Bundle in six-month steps or deploying on-premises, where retention is yours to set

Prices hidden behind a script

The pricing page shows dashes until the calculator renders, and quotes vary by deployment.

Fixed by: getting the quote in writing with the seat definition, deployment and retention spelled out

Mac and Linux gaps

Several rules and capture types work only on Windows.

Fixed by: checking the per-platform feature matrix in Teramind's knowledge base before licensing a mixed fleet

If Teramind is not the right price

If you need productivity analytics without recording, ActivTrak starts at $10 with no screen capture by default and no minimum; ActivTrak vs Teramind sets them side by side and ActivTrak pricing has its tiers. If you want recording and activity data at a lower price for a small team, Insightful from about $8 and Controlio from about $6.67 record screens with monthly billing and no five-seat floor. If you need DLP at enterprise scale, Veriato, DTEX, Forcepoint and Microsoft Purview are the comparison set, all by quote. If you want on-premises without Enterprise pricing, Kickidler and CurrentWare ($12 to $20) offer it. The full list is in Teramind alternatives.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Teramind cost per user?

$14 (Starter), $28 (UAM) or $32 (DLP) per seat per month on annual billing, or $15, $30 and $35 month to month, with a minimum of five seats on every plan. Enterprise and Government pricing is quoted. Private cloud and on-premises deployments are also quoted. The annual discount is about 8 percent.

What is the minimum number of Teramind users?

Five. Teramind's knowledge base states that Starter, UAM and DLP are available monthly or annually with a 5-seat minimum, so a team of two or three pays for five: $70 a month on Starter annual, $140 on UAM, $160 on DLP.

What is the difference between Teramind Starter, UAM and DLP?

Starter records screens, offers live view and remote control, monitors websites and applications and analyzes productivity, with limited behavior rules. UAM adds keystroke logging, email and file transfer monitoring, printing, dynamic risk scoring, unlimited activity-based rules and AI conversation controls. DLP adds content-based rules, clipboard monitoring, rules for text entered into AI tools, sensitive data discovery, fingerprinting and compliance templates. OCR of recordings is Enterprise only.

Does Teramind log keystrokes?

On UAM, DLP and Enterprise, yes. Starter does not include keystroke logging, email monitoring or file transfer monitoring. All tiers record the screen, which an administrator can turn off.

How long does Teramind keep recordings?

Six months for screen recordings and audio and 18 months for telemetry such as activity logs on the cloud service, on every tier. The Compliance Bundle add-on extends both by six months per increment at a price available through your success manager. On-premises and private cloud customers set their own retention.

Does Teramind have a free plan or trial?

No free plan. The cloud trial is seven days of the full DLP package for up to five users with no credit card, extendable on request. On-premises and private cloud evaluations typically receive a 14-day license.

Can Teramind monitor phones or Chromebooks?

No. Teramind states that it monitors Windows, Windows Server and macOS desktops and laptops, with a Linux agent of reduced functionality, and does not support Android or iOS devices or ChromeOS. The management dashboard can be viewed in a mobile browser.

Is Teramind more expensive than ActivTrak?

At the entry level, yes: Teramind Starter is $14 per seat with a five-seat minimum against ActivTrak at $10 with no minimum. They are different products: Teramind records screens continuously and sells keystroke logging and data loss prevention on its upper tiers, while ActivTrak does no keystroke logging and captures screenshots only through an alarm-triggered add-on. Teramind offers monthly billing; ActivTrak is annual only.
