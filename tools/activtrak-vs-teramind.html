ActivTrak vs Teramind (2026): Analytics vs Surveillance

P Pavan
November 9, 2026 · 11 min read

ActivTrak and Teramind are the two names that come up first when a company searches for employee monitoring software, and they are built for different buyers. ActivTrak is productivity analytics: application and website time, schedule adherence, workload and coaching, with no screenshots by default and no keystroke logging, from $10 per worker on an annual plan with a free tier for three people. Teramind is user activity monitoring and data loss prevention: continuous screen recording on every tier, keystrokes and email from $28, content inspection from $32, on-premises deployment, and a five-seat minimum.

This comparison sets out the November 2026 prices and what teams of 5, 25 and 100 pay, a line-by-line feature table, the difference between measuring and watching and what it means for notice and trust, a chooser that scores the two against your situation, detailed cards on each, and how to move between them. The short version: ActivTrak if you want to understand how time is spent, Teramind if you need to record and inspect what people do.

The verdict

ActivTrak for productivity analytics: who is overloaded, which teams are in meetings all day, whether the hybrid schedule is being kept, how much time goes to each application. It takes no screenshots by default, logs no keystrokes, starts at $10 per worker with a free tier for three, and is sold to managers and HR rather than to security teams.

Teramind for investigation and data protection: continuous screen recording you can play back, keystrokes and email on UAM, content-aware data loss prevention on DLP, and on-premises deployment for regulated environments. It starts at $14 with a five-seat minimum and is sold to security and compliance teams.

They overlap only at the bottom. ActivTrak with the Screen Details add-on and Teramind Starter both do productivity measurement with some screen capture at $10 to $15. Above that the products diverge: nothing in ActivTrak does what Teramind UAM and DLP do, and nothing in Teramind does ActivTrak's coaching and workload analysis as well. Decide which problem you have before comparing prices.

The difference, stated plainly

ActivTrak measures. Its agent records which applications and websites were active, for how long, against a schedule, and turns that into productivity classifications, workload and focus-time reports, habit coaching and, since 2026, measurement of how much staff are using AI tools. By default it keeps no image of the screen and it has never logged keystrokes; its security page says "ActivTrak does not take or retain screenshots" except through the optional Screen Details add-on under configured alarm conditions. That design is why it can be deployed with a straightforward notice and why HR and operations teams buy it: the data is about time and applications, not content.

Teramind watches. Every tier records the screen as video and offers a live view and remote control; UAM adds every keystroke, email and file movement, and DLP adds inspection of content, including what is pasted into an AI chat. Rules fire on behavior and content and can block, warn, lock the session or alert a security analyst, and the recordings are evidence for an investigation or a regulator. That design is why security and compliance teams buy it, why it offers on-premises deployment and government cloud, and why it carries the legal and cultural weight that our keystroke logging and monitoring lawsuit guides describe. Deploying Teramind UAM on knowledge workers to improve productivity is a mismatch of tool to problem, and so is deploying ActivTrak to catch data theft.

What it is

ActivTrak records application and website activity, classifies it as productive or not by role, and reports on focus time, meetings, workload, schedule adherence and, with the AI Insights add-on, AI tool usage and impact. The two lower tiers carry compliance alarms and can take the Screen Details add-on; the $17 tier drops alarms in favor of coaching features and a conversational Work Advisor. Plans were renamed in July 2026 from Essentials, Essentials Plus and Professional. The agent takes no screenshots by default and does no keystroke logging.

Where it wins

  • No screenshots by default, no keystrokes, which simplifies notice and trust
  • Free for 3 and $10 entry with no seat minimum
  • Workload, habits, meeting and AI-usage analytics that Teramind lacks
  • HRIS, Teams, Slack, Viva and BI integrations

Where it loses

  • Annual billing only
  • No continuous recording, no DLP, no on-premises option
  • 6-month default retention on the lower tiers
  • Paid support mandatory above 50 licenses; no Linux agent

Choose it if

You want to understand how work time is spent and coach around it, you are not investigating anyone, and you can accept an annual contract.

Teramind

Insider risk, investigation and DLP
Runs on Windows, Windows Server, macOS, Linux (reduced) agents; Citrix and terminal servers; web console Pricing (November 2026) Starter $14, UAM $28, DLP $32 per seat per month on annual billing ($15, $30, $35 monthly); 5-seat minimum; Enterprise, private cloud and on-premises by quote; 7-day trial. Full tiers on our Teramind pricing page

What it is

Teramind records user sessions as video with live view and remote control on every tier, adds keystroke, email, file and printing capture with dynamic risk scoring on UAM, and content-aware data loss prevention with clipboard monitoring, fingerprinting and rules for AI prompts on DLP. Behavior rules can warn, block or lock sessions. Enterprise adds OCR search of recordings, scriptable rules, GovCloud and professional services. Cloud retention is 6 months of video and 18 months of telemetry; on-premises customers set their own.

Where it wins

  • Continuous recording with playback on every tier
  • Keystrokes, email, files and content inspection for investigations and DLP
  • On-premises, private cloud and government cloud deployment
  • Monthly billing and Linux support

Where it loses

  • 5-seat minimum and $14 entry, 40 percent above ActivTrak
  • Starter lacks the keystroke and email capture buyers expect; UAM is $28
  • Weaker workload and coaching analytics; no Slack or Teams
  • 6-month cloud video retention unless extended; no ChromeOS or mobile

Choose it if

You have a security or compliance requirement to record and inspect user activity, you may need to investigate incidents with evidence, or you must deploy on your own infrastructure.

Moving between them

  1. ActivTrak to Teramind: there is no data migration path; ActivTrak exports reports (to 10,000 or 50,000 rows by tier, or via ActivConnect) for your records, and Teramind starts collecting on deployment. Rebuild productivity classifications as Teramind categories. Because Teramind records screens, update the monitoring notice and obtain fresh acknowledgments before the agent goes out; New York, Connecticut and Delaware require it and our policy template has the clauses.
  2. Teramind to ActivTrak: export or archive recordings you must keep before the subscription ends, since cloud retention stops with the account; ActivTrak has no import. Expect less data and a simpler notice. ActivTrak's annual-only billing means timing the switch to Teramind's renewal.
  3. Run both for one pay period only if you have a written reason; double monitoring is hard to explain to staff and doubles the cost.
  4. Either way, document what was collected, for how long and why, in the retention schedule described in our record retention guide.

How this comparison was made

Both products were checked on the criteria used across our monitoring reviews: what the agent captures by default and by option, tiers and prices, minimums and billing terms, retention, deployment options, platforms, integrations, compliance claims and company facts. ActivTrak data was read from activtrak.com/pricing, its security, support and integrations pages on November 9, 2026, with the July 2026 package update confirmed through a third-party capture because ActivTrak's help center blocked automated reading. Teramind data was read from teramind.co/services/price, its knowledge base and privacy policy the same day; because the pricing page renders prices with a script, dollar figures come from a rendered capture and independent November 2026 reviews and match the page's stated 8 percent annual discount. Neither vendor was contacted; WorkIntel Hub has no affiliate relationship with either.

Key takeaways

  • ActivTrak starts at $10 per worker per month on annual billing with a free tier for 3; Teramind starts at $14 annual or $15 monthly with a 5-seat minimum, rising to $28 for UAM and $32 for DLP.
  • ActivTrak takes no screenshots by default and does no keystroke logging; screenshots are a quoted add-on on its two lower tiers, triggered by alarms. Teramind records screens continuously on every tier and adds keystrokes, email and files on UAM and content inspection on DLP.
  • ActivTrak is cloud only and annual only; Teramind offers monthly billing and cloud, private cloud, on-premises and government cloud deployment.
  • At 25 people, ActivTrak costs $250 to $560 a month by tier and Teramind $350 to $800; at 100 people, $1,000 to $2,240 against $1,400 to $3,200.
  • Retention: ActivTrak keeps 6 months on its lower tiers and 1 year on the $17 tier, extendable to 3 years at $1 per worker per year; Teramind keeps 6 months of video and 18 months of telemetry, extended by a quoted Compliance Bundle or set freely on-premises.
  • ActivTrak's strengths are workload, habit and AI-usage analytics with Slack, Teams and HRIS integrations; Teramind's are investigation evidence, DLP, SIEM integrations and Linux support.
  • Choose ActivTrak to understand and coach how time is spent; choose Teramind when you must record, inspect or block user activity for security or compliance.

Frequently asked questions

Is ActivTrak or Teramind cheaper?

ActivTrak at the entry level: $10 per worker per month against Teramind Starter at $14 on annual billing, and ActivTrak has no seat minimum where Teramind requires five. Teramind's upper tiers at $28 and $32 cost more than any ActivTrak plan, including the bundle at about $22.40, but they include recording, keystroke logging and data loss prevention that ActivTrak does not offer. Teramind has monthly billing; ActivTrak does not.

Does ActivTrak record screens like Teramind?

No. ActivTrak takes no screenshots by default and does not record video. Its Screen Details add-on, available on the two lower tiers at a quoted price, captures screenshots when a configured alarm fires. Teramind records the screen continuously on every tier with live view and playback, and an administrator can turn recording off.

Which logs keystrokes?

Teramind, on its UAM, DLP and Enterprise tiers, not on Starter. ActivTrak states that it does no keystroke logging, video or camera monitoring on any plan.

Which is better for productivity management?

ActivTrak. Its product is built around productivity classification, focus time, meeting load, schedule adherence, workload balance, habit coaching, a conversational Work Advisor and AI-usage measurement. Teramind reports productivity on every tier but its depth is in security features, and its recording-first design is harder to justify to staff for a productivity purpose.

Which is better for preventing data theft?

Teramind. Its DLP tier applies content-based rules, monitors the clipboard, fingerprints sensitive files, discovers and classifies data and can block or lock sessions, with recordings as evidence; UAM adds keystroke, email and file capture. ActivTrak has no data loss prevention beyond website blocking through its Screen Details add-on.

Can either be installed on-premises?

Teramind can, on VMware, Hyper-V or Nutanix, in a private AWS or Azure cloud, or in AWS GovCloud and Azure Government on the Enterprise tier, with retention set by the customer. ActivTrak is cloud only, hosted on Google Cloud.

Do I need to notify employees for either?

Yes for both in New York, Connecticut and Delaware, which require written notice of electronic monitoring, and for both in practice everywhere. Teramind's recording and keystroke capture carry heavier obligations: California's privacy law gives employees access to the collected data, several states' wiretap laws require consent for content capture, and our monitoring law guide covers the rest. ActivTrak's default collection of application time is easier to notice and to defend.

Which has longer data retention?

ActivTrak keeps 30 days on Free, 6 months on its $10 and $15 tiers and 1 year on the $17 tier, extendable to 3 years at $1 per worker per month per extra year. Teramind keeps 6 months of recordings and 18 months of telemetry on all cloud tiers, extendable in 6-month steps through a quoted Compliance Bundle, or without limit on-premises.
