Best PTO Tracking Software: 12 Tools Compared (2026)

P Pavan
November 9, 2026 · 18 min read

A PTO tracker has one job a spreadsheet cannot do reliably: apply accrual, carryover and approval rules to every person every day and produce a balance nobody disputes. The twelve tools below do that at prices from free to $25 per employee per month, and they split into three kinds: dedicated trackers (Vacation Tracker, Day Off, Timetastic, Calamari, LeaveBoard, actiPLANS, Flamingo), workforce apps with a time-off module (Connecteam), and HR or payroll systems in which time off is one feature (Zoho People, BambooHR, Gusto, Rippling). Which kind you need depends on whether leave is the only HR process you want software for.

This guide compares all twelve on the rules that matter (accrual method, carryover caps, negative balances, approval levels, blackout dates), on where requests happen (Slack, Teams, mobile, inside payroll), on payroll connections and on November 2026 pricing, including the four with free plans. A chooser scores them against your situation, each has a detailed card, and the closing sections cover the legal rules a tracker has to get right and the numbers on paid leave in the US. For the laws themselves, see paid sick leave laws by state and PTO rollover and payout.

The tools in detail

Vacation Tracker

Best dedicated tracker for Slack and Teams teams
Runs on Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, web, iOS, Android Pricing (November 2026) Core $2 and Complete $4 per user per month on monthly billing, with minimum fees of $50 and $100 that cover up to 25 users; about 10 percent off on annual billing; 14-day trial with all features; no free plan (one launched in November 2025 was retired)

What it is

Vacation Tracker lives where people already are: requests, approvals and balances inside Slack, Teams or Google Workspace, with an AI assistant that takes plain-language requests since mid-2026. Core gives one approver, a shared calendar, on-demand reports and iCal sync. Complete adds accrual (weekly, biweekly, semimonthly or monthly) with caps, multi-level approval with substitute approvers, direct Google and Outlook calendar sync, scheduled reports and hourly requests down to 15-minute increments. Policies support capped or reset carryover with expiry dates, negative balances and time off in lieu. An automation add-on handles seniority entitlements, blackout periods, probation and maximum staff away. The ADP Workforce Now integration imports users and pushes approved leave with code mapping.

Where it is strong

  • Requests and approvals without leaving Slack or Teams
  • Accruals, caps, negative balances and multi-level approval at $4
  • ADP Workforce Now sync; open API and MCP server
  • Frequent, dated releases through 2026

Where it falls short

  • Minimum fee makes it $50 or $100 a month for teams under 25
  • No Gusto, QuickBooks or Paychex integration
  • Accruals and multi-level approval need Complete
  • No state sick-leave templates

Who it suits

Teams of 10 to 200 that run on Slack or Teams and want real accrual rules without an HR suite.

The rules a PTO tracker has to get right

A spreadsheet fails at the same five points every time, and the tools above differ mainly in how many of them they handle automatically.

  1. Accrual method. A lump sum each January is simple but over-grants to people who leave in February. Per-pay-period accrual with a cap matches cost to service. Per-hour-worked accrual is what most state paid-sick-leave laws require (commonly one hour per 30 or 40 worked), and only Connecteam, Gusto and the HR suites on this list do it natively; see paid sick leave laws by state.
  2. Carryover and payout. Use-it-or-lose-it is illegal in California, Montana and Nebraska; capped carryover is the lawful substitute. Several states require payout of accrued vacation at separation, so the tool must report an exact balance on the last day; our PTO rollover and payout guide has the state table and a payout calculator.
  3. Negative balances. Allowing a few days into the red is common; recovering it from a final paycheck is restricted in many states. A tool that caps negative balances (Connecteam, Vacation Tracker) or resets them (BambooHR) avoids the argument.
  4. Approvals and coverage. One approver per team is enough for most companies; multi-level approval matters where a department head and HR both need to sign, and blackout dates and maximum-staff-away rules matter in retail and support. Vacation Tracker Complete, Calamari, Day Off and Zoho handle these.
  5. The record. Leave balances are wage records in the payout states and must be kept for the full retention period, six years in New York and New Jersey; see record retention by state. An audit log of who changed what, which Timetastic, Calamari and actiPLANS provide, is the difference between a dispute and a lookup.

The context in numbers

  • 80 percent of private-industry workers had access to paid vacation in March 2026, and 81 percent to paid sick leave and paid holidays (Bureau of Labor Statistics, Employee Benefits in the United States, released September 25, 2026). Access runs from 71 percent at establishments with fewer than 50 workers to 91 percent at 500 or more.
  • After one year of service, 32 percent of private-industry workers received 10 to 14 days of paid vacation; after ten years, 30 percent received 15 to 19 days (BLS paid vacations fact sheet, September 2026).
  • 67 percent of Americans left some paid time off unused in 2025 (Solitaired survey, January 2026); 23 percent of workers took no vacation days in the past year (FlexJobs, October 2025, 3,000 respondents). Our unused vacation statistics page collects the series.
  • Eighteen states plus the District of Columbia mandate paid sick leave as of 2026, counting Virginia's law for home health workers; add Illinois, Maine and Nevada, whose laws grant paid leave for any reason, and the count is 21 jurisdictions. Alaska's law took effect July 1, 2025 and Nebraska's October 1, 2025; Missouri's was repealed effective August 28, 2025.

  • Native payroll sync is rare: Connecteam exports to Gusto, QuickBooks, Paychex, Xero and ADP; Vacation Tracker syncs with ADP Workforce Now; Gusto, Rippling and BambooHR are the payroll.
  • HR suites price time off as part of the system: BambooHR $10 to $25 per employee, Gusto $6 to $22 per person plus $49 to $180 base, Rippling from $8 plus base fee, Zoho People about $1.25 to $4.50.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free PTO tracker?

Day Off, which is free for up to 10 users with customizable leave types, a shared calendar, approvals and basic reports, and adds accruals, capped carryover and blackout dates for $2 a seat. Connecteam's Small Business plan is also free for 10 users with every feature, including per-hour accrual and payroll export, and suits hourly teams. LeaveBoard is free for nine employees and Zoho People for five.

Which PTO software works in Slack and Microsoft Teams?

Vacation Tracker (requests, approvals, balances and an AI assistant in Slack, Teams and Google Workspace), Day Off (Slack and Teams apps), Calamari (Slack, Teams, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365) and Timetastic (Slack on Business, Teams on Pro). Flamingo runs entirely inside Slack. BambooHR has a Slack app; Rippling integrates with both.

Which PTO tracker handles state paid sick leave accrual?

The ones that accrue per hour worked: Connecteam (fixed or per hour, with caps, tenure rules and negative-balance limits), Gusto (per hour, with waiting periods and a state and city reference table), and the HR suites Rippling and BambooHR. Most dedicated trackers accrue per pay period or per year, which satisfies vacation policies but not laws written as one hour per 30 or 40 hours worked.

How much does PTO tracking software cost?

Dedicated trackers run $1.20 to $4 per user per month in November 2026: actiPLANS from $1.20, LeaveBoard $1.35, Timetastic $1.50 to $2.50, Day Off $2, Calamari $2 to $2.50 and Vacation Tracker $2 to $4 with minimum fees of $50 or $100. HR systems that include time off cost more: Zoho People about $1.25 to $4.50, Rippling from $8 plus a base fee, BambooHR $10 to $25 per employee, Gusto $6 to $22 per person plus a $49 to $180 base.

Can PTO software sync with payroll?

Some can. Connecteam exports hours and PTO to Gusto, QuickBooks Online, Paychex, Xero and ADP Workforce Now; Vacation Tracker syncs approved leave to ADP Workforce Now; Timetastic's top tier connects to Sage and QuickBooks. Gusto, Rippling and BambooHR (with its payroll add-on) are the payroll system, so no sync is needed. Day Off, Calamari, LeaveBoard, actiPLANS and Flamingo provide reports and exports rather than live connections.

Do I need multi-level approval?

Only if two people must sign off, for example a team lead and HR, or a manager and a department head. Vacation Tracker Complete (with substitute approvers), Calamari (multi-stage flows by type, team or department), Zoho People (Essential HR and above) and BambooHR (up to five approvers per policy) support it. Timetastic, LeaveBoard, Flamingo and Connecteam use a single approver.

Is use-it-or-lose-it PTO legal?

Not in California, Montana or Nebraska, where accrued vacation is treated as earned wages that cannot be forfeited; employers there use accrual caps instead. In most other states it is legal if the policy is written and communicated, though several states require payout of any accrued balance at separation. A tracker with capped carryover and expiry dates (Vacation Tracker, Day Off, Calamari, BambooHR) implements the lawful alternative.

Should I buy a dedicated tracker or use my HR or payroll system?

If you already pay for Gusto, Rippling, BambooHR or Zoho People, use their time-off module first; it is included and it is where compliance and payout calculations belong. Buy a dedicated tracker when your HR system has no leave module, when requests need to happen in Slack or Teams, or when you need rules (blackout dates, multi-level approvals, hourly requests) your system lacks. Dedicated trackers cost $1 to $4 a user; the suites cost $8 to $25.
