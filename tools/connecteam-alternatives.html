Connecteam Alternatives (2026): 12 Apps for Deskless Teams Compared

P Pavan
October 28, 2026 · 15 min read

Connecteam is the most recommended app for small deskless teams because its free plan gives ten users everything: scheduling, a GPS time clock, chat, forms, training and time off. People start looking for alternatives at user eleven, when the free plan ends and the paid structure appears: three hubs, Operations, Communications and HR and Skills, each priced separately at $29 to $99 a month for the first 30 users, so that a team using all three on paid plans buys three subscriptions. The second trigger is payroll, which Connecteam exports but does not run.

This guide sorts the alternatives by those reasons with dated quotes, gives October 2026 pricing with minimums and whether it is per user or per location, and marks where a vendor's page could not be read. Sling and ZoomShift extend the free tier; Deputy, When I Work and Homebase put the whole feature set in one price; Homebase, Rippling and QuickBooks Time add payroll; 7shifts is the restaurant answer; Jibble and Clockify replace the clock alone.

The short answer

If you outgrew the free plan at user eleven, Sling is free for 30 users and $1.70 after, and ZoomShift is free for 20. Neither has Connecteam's forms and training, but both schedule and clock.

If the per-hub pricing is the problem, Deputy ($5 to $9 per user, $30 minimum), When I Work ($2.50 to $8) and Homebase ($30 to $120 per location) put scheduling, time clock and messaging in one price.

If you need payroll in the same product, Homebase (payroll $49 plus $6 per employee), Gusto and Rippling do it natively; Connecteam exports to them.

What Connecteam costs and does in October 2026

Connecteam

The incumbent
Runs on Web, iOS, Android; kiosk app Pricing (October 2026) Small Business Plan free for up to 10 users with every hub; paid plans priced per hub: Operations, Communications and HR and Skills each at Basic $29, Advanced $49 or Expert $99 a month on annual billing ($35, $59, $119 monthly) for the first 30 users, plus a per-user fee beyond 30 ($0.50 to $5 depending on hub and tier); Enterprise by quote; 14-day trial without a card Monitoring GPS at punch and geofences

What it is

Connecteam is an all-in-one app for deskless teams, sold as three hubs. Operations covers scheduling, a time clock with GPS and geofences, a kiosk app, task management and forms. Communications covers chat, updates, a directory, surveys and a knowledge base. HR and Skills covers onboarding, training courses, documents, time off and recognition. Each hub has its own tier ladder, and a team that wants scheduling, chat and training on paid plans buys all three. The free plan includes everything for ten users, which is why it is the most recommended tool for very small crews and why the bill at user eleven surprises people.

Where it beats Connecteam

  • The widest feature set for frontline teams in one app, including forms, training and recognition
  • Free for ten users with no feature cuts
  • Flat base pricing for 30 users is cheap per head for a team of 25 to 30

Where Connecteam is still better

  • Three hubs mean three subscriptions; Expert across all three is $297 a month on annual billing before extra users
  • Reviewers report quoted prices differing from the website and thin support
  • Payroll is by export only; no native payroll or tax filing

Choose it if

Deskless teams of 25 to 300 that will use the forms, training and communication features as well as the schedule, and that have checked the three-hub total against a one-price competitor. Our attendance tracking software review places it among its peers.

Prices were read from connecteam.com/pricing on October 28, 2026. The page does not state what happens at the eleventh user beyond the free plan ending, and we did not locate a help center article that does; expect to choose a paid hub.

Why people leave Connecteam

Connecteam's Trustpilot profile is strong (4.7 of 5 across 1,914 reviews) and the negative reviews are few. The reasons below are the ones with evidence or a clear product basis, each with a fix.

The bill at user eleven

The free plan covers ten users and every feature. The eleventh user requires a paid hub at $29 or more a month per hub, and a team that used scheduling, chat and training on the free plan now needs three.

Fixed by: larger free tiers: Sling (30 users), ZoomShift (20 users), Homebase (one location), Jibble (unlimited users for the clock).

Hubs priced separately

A 40-person team on Advanced for Operations, Communications and HR pays $147 a month plus 30 extra users across three hubs, roughly $200 a month on annual billing; the same team on Deputy Core pays $260 and gets no training module, on When I Work Pro $200 with no forms.

Fixed by: one-price tools where the whole feature set is in the tier: Deputy, When I Work, Homebase, 7shifts for restaurants.

Quoted price differs from the website

An October 2026 reviewer says the prices offered on a sales call did not match the pricing page and described the call as hostile.

the prices advertised on their website did not reflect what was actually offered.Trustpilot, one star, October 9, 2026

Fixed by: self-serve pricing with no sales call: Sling, When I Work, Clockify, Homebase.

Support and payroll export

A two-star review from October 2026 describes weeks without a working export of hours to ADP and no real support.

It's been weeks and we still haven't been able to export our hours to ADP.Trustpilot, two stars, October 14, 2026

Fixed by: native payroll or maintained payroll integrations: Homebase (own payroll), Deputy (payroll add-on and ADP, Gusto, QuickBooks exports), QuickBooks Time, Rippling.

Task management limits

A reviewer found the task hub too limited for assignment at scale and called the product botched together, a view at odds with most reviews but consistent with Connecteam's breadth over depth.

Connecteam is THE most botched together software I have ever used.Trustpilot, one star, October 22, 2026

Fixed by: purpose-built tools for the job that mattered: 7shifts for restaurant tasks and logs, Blink for frontline communication, Deputy for scheduling depth.

No native payroll

Hours leave Connecteam by export or integration. A small business that wants one bill for scheduling, time and payroll cannot get it here.

Fixed by: Homebase payroll ($49 plus $6 per employee), Rippling, Gusto, QuickBooks Time with QuickBooks Payroll.

The alternatives in detail

Deputy

One price for scheduling, clock and compliance
Runs on Web, iOS, Android; kiosk Pricing (October 2026) Lite $5, Core $6.50, Pro $9 per user per month (annual saves 10%); minimum monthly spend of $30 per invoice on monthly plans since September 1, 2025; payroll add-on $8 per user plus $49 base a month on annual US plans; HR $2, Messaging+ $1.95, Analytics+ $1.50 per user; no free plan

What it is

Deputy schedules by demand, enforces break, overtime and fatigue rules, clocks in with GPS or a facial-recognition kiosk, approves timesheets, and exports to ADP, Gusto, QuickBooks and Xero, with messaging and news feed included. Auto-scheduling arrives on Core. It is the scheduling-first alternative with the deepest compliance rules.

Where it beats Connecteam

  • Scheduling, clock, compliance and messaging in one tier price
  • Break and overtime rules for California, New York and fair workweek cities
  • Established payroll exports and a payroll add-on

Where Connecteam is still better

  • No free plan and a $30 monthly minimum
  • No forms, training or onboarding modules
  • Pro is contact-sales

Choose it if

You left over hub pricing and the schedule is the product you actually use.

Switching

Deputy imports employees from CSV and from payroll systems; rebuild shift templates, then overlap one roster.

Sling

Free for 30, cheap after
Runs on Web, iOS, Android Pricing (October 2026) Free for up to 30 users; Premium $1.70 and Business $3.40 per user per month billed annually ($2 and $4 monthly); 15-day trial; billed on active users; no minimum

What it is

Sling schedules shifts with availability, swaps and open shifts, runs a time clock with geofencing on Premium, adds labor cost tracking and PTO on Business, and includes messaging and a newsfeed on the free plan. It is the lowest-cost route out of Connecteam's free tier for a team of 11 to 30.

Where it beats Connecteam

  • Free for three times as many users
  • Under $2 a user for the clock
  • Billed on active users only

Where Connecteam is still better

  • No forms, training, tasks or kiosk app
  • Lighter reporting
  • Owned by Toast; restaurant features are the roadmap

Choose it if

You have 11 to 30 people and used Connecteam for scheduling and chat.

Switching

CSV import of employees; recreate positions and locations.

When I Work

Scheduling with auto-build
Runs on Web, iOS, Android Pricing (October 2026) Essentials $2.50, Pro $5, Premium $8 per user per month, shown monthly; annual pricing not displayed; 14-day trial without a card; no minimum; the pricing page and its FAQ disagree on whether the time clock is included on every plan, so confirm before buying

What it is

When I Work schedules with auto-scheduling on every tier, handles availability, swaps and shift confirmations, runs a time clock with GPS, and includes team messaging. Premium adds API access, webhooks and SAML single sign-on. It exports to Gusto, ADP, QuickBooks and Paychex.

Where it beats Connecteam

  • Auto-scheduling from $2.50
  • One per-user price with no hubs
  • SSO and API on Premium for larger operators

Where Connecteam is still better

  • Its own pricing page is inconsistent about the time clock
  • No forms, training or onboarding
  • Old support complaints on a tiny review sample

Choose it if

You want scheduling automation at a low per-user price and will check the clock question on the page.

Switching

CSV import of employees and positions; schedules rebuild from templates. See our When I Work alternatives guide for its own trade-offs.

How to leave Connecteam cleanly

  1. Export per hub. Timesheets, schedules, forms submissions, course completions and documents export separately from each hub; the documents and training records are the ones you will miss.
  2. Download the employee directory with roles, locations and phone numbers; every alternative imports from CSV.
  3. Close the pay period in Connecteam and start the new tool at the boundary so no shift is paid twice or not at all.
  4. Re-enroll kiosks and geofences in the new tool before the first shift; a crew that cannot clock in on Monday morning is the migration failure that gets noticed.
  5. Issue the monitoring notice if the new tool records GPS or uses facial recognition at the kiosk; Connecticut, Delaware and New York require written notice, and Illinois requires a biometric release before any face scan. Our biometric privacy laws guide covers the kiosk question.

Key takeaways

  • Connecteam in October 2026: free for 10 users with every feature; paid plans per hub (Operations, Communications, HR and Skills) at $29, $49 or $99 a month on annual billing for the first 30 users plus a per-user fee beyond, so three hubs on Expert is $297 a month before extra users.
  • Its Trustpilot score is 4.7 of 5 across 1,914 reviews; the few negatives concern quoted prices differing from the website, support and a stalled ADP export.
  • Larger free tiers: Sling (30 users), Zoho Shifts (25), ZoomShift (20), Homebase (one location, unlimited users), Jibble (unlimited users for the clock).
  • One price for scheduling, clock and messaging: Deputy ($5 to $9 per user, $30 minimum), When I Work ($2.50 to $8), Homebase ($30 to $120 per location).
  • Native payroll: Homebase ($49 plus $6 per employee), Rippling, QuickBooks Time with QuickBooks Payroll, 7shifts for restaurants.
  • Deputy's $30 monthly minimum (since September 1, 2025) and Homebase's per-location pricing are the two structures that catch small or multi-site teams.
  • On leaving: export each hub separately, keep the training records and documents, switch at a pay-period boundary, re-enroll kiosks first, and issue notices where GPS or facial recognition is involved.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to Connecteam?

Deputy for teams that mainly need scheduling, a time clock and compliance rules in one per-user price; Sling for teams of 11 to 30 that outgrew the free plan and need scheduling and chat cheaply; Homebase for one to three shops or restaurants that want payroll in the same product. Restaurants should look at 7shifts first.

Is there a free alternative to Connecteam?

Sling is free for 30 users, Zoho Shifts for 25, ZoomShift for 20 at one location, Homebase for one location with no user limit, Jibble for unlimited users as a time clock, and Clockify for 5. Connecteam's own free plan covers 10 users with every feature.

How much does Connecteam cost after the free plan?

Paid plans are priced per hub. Operations, Communications and HR and Skills each cost $29 (Basic), $49 (Advanced) or $99 (Expert) a month on annual billing, or $35, $59 and $119 monthly, for the first 30 users, plus a per-user fee beyond 30 that ranges from about $0.50 to $5 depending on hub and tier. Enterprise is quoted. Prices were read on October 28, 2026.

What happens when I pass 10 users on Connecteam's free plan?

The Small Business Plan ends at 10 users and you choose a paid hub for the features you use. Connecteam's pricing page does not describe the mechanics beyond that, and we could not locate a help center article that does, so ask before adding the eleventh user.

Which Connecteam alternatives include payroll?

Homebase runs its own payroll at $49 a month plus $6 per employee with tax filing in all 50 states; Rippling is a payroll and HR platform with time modules; QuickBooks Time feeds QuickBooks Payroll; 7shifts and Deputy offer payroll add-ons. Connecteam, Sling, When I Work, Jibble and Clockify export to payroll providers.

Is Deputy cheaper than Connecteam?

It depends on team size. Deputy charges $5 to $9 per user with a $30 monthly minimum, so 40 users on Core cost about $260 a month; Connecteam's Operations Advanced hub for 40 users costs about $74 on annual billing. Deputy is one price for everything it does, while a team using three Connecteam hubs would pay about $200. Compare the hubs you actually use.

Which alternative is best for restaurants?

7shifts, which schedules against POS sales forecasts, manages tips and tip pooling, runs manager logbooks and integrates with Toast, Square and other restaurant POS systems, with a free tier for one location. Homebase and Sling (owned by Toast) are the other restaurant-oriented options.

Can I export my data from Connecteam?

Yes, by hub. Timesheets, schedules, form submissions, training completions and documents export separately, and the employee directory exports to CSV. Export the training records and documents before the account closes; they are the data no alternative imports automatically.
