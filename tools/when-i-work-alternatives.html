When I Work Alternatives (2026): 11 Scheduling Apps Compared

P Pavan
October 28, 2026 · 14 min read

When I Work is the cheapest way to get auto-scheduling for an hourly team: $2.50 a user on Essentials, no base fee, no minimum, no per-location pricing. People leave it for what it does not do rather than for what it does badly. It has no break or overtime rules at the point of scheduling, no payroll, no forms or training, and in October 2026 its own pricing page cannot agree with itself about whether the time clock is included on every plan.

This guide sorts the alternatives by those gaps, gives October 2026 pricing with minimums and whether it is per user or per location, and marks where a vendor's page could not be read. Sling and ZoomShift cost less; Deputy adds compliance; Homebase, Rippling and QuickBooks Time add payroll; Connecteam adds forms and training; 7shifts is the restaurant answer; Jibble and Clockify replace the clock.

The short answer

If you want the same scheduling for less or free, Sling is free for 30 users and $1.70 after, and ZoomShift is free for 20. Both schedule and clock; neither auto-builds a schedule.

If you need compliance rules, deeper reporting or payroll, Deputy ($5 to $9, $30 minimum) enforces break and fatigue rules and sells a payroll add-on; Homebase ($30 to $120 per location) runs its own payroll; 7shifts schedules restaurants against sales.

If the team needs more than a schedule, Connecteam adds forms, training and chat free for ten users, and QuickBooks Time or Rippling tie the clock to payroll you already run.

What When I Work costs and does in October 2026

When I Work

The incumbent
Runs on Web, iOS, Android Pricing (October 2026) Essentials $2.50, Pro $5, Premium $8 per user per month, displayed as monthly prices; an annual option is mentioned but no annual prices are shown; 14-day trial without a card; no minimum; the pricing page's FAQ also describes $2.50 for single-location teams and $5 for multi-location businesses, which does not match the three tiers Monitoring GPS at punch

What it is

When I Work schedules hourly teams with templates, availability, shift swaps, open shifts and shift confirmations, and includes auto-scheduling on every tier, which is rare at $2.50. The time clock records punches by app, web or shared device with GPS, and timesheets export to Gusto, ADP, QuickBooks, Paychex and others. Team messaging is included. Premium adds API keys, webhooks and SAML single sign-on. Each plan on the pricing page shows a time tracking and attendance toggle, while the FAQ says every plan includes it, so whether the clock is included or an add-on on Essentials needs a direct answer from the vendor before you buy.

Where it beats When I Work

  • Auto-scheduling from $2.50 a user, the cheapest in this group
  • No base fee, no minimum, no per-location pricing
  • Payroll exports to the major providers and SSO on Premium

Where When I Work is still better

  • Its own pricing page is inconsistent about the time clock and about what the tiers are
  • No forms, training, onboarding or native payroll
  • A tiny Trustpilot sample (4.1 of 5 across 7 reviews) with support complaints from 2024

Choose it if

Hourly teams of 10 to 200 that want scheduling automation at a low per-user price and will confirm the time clock question in writing. Our Deputy vs When I Work comparison goes deeper.

Why people leave When I Work

When I Work has almost no public negative review record (seven Trustpilot reviews, the critical ones from 2024), so the reasons below lean on the product and its pricing page rather than on a pattern of complaints. Each has a fix.

Uncertainty about what the clock costs

The pricing page shows a time tracking toggle per plan and the FAQ says every plan includes it; earlier versions sold attendance as an add-on. Buyers who assumed the clock was in Essentials have reported a different bill.

Fixed by: tools whose clock is unambiguous: Sling (clock on Premium, $1.70), Deputy (included), Homebase (included on every plan including free), Jibble (the clock is the product).

Support

The two critical Trustpilot reviews, both from 2024, describe a decline in customer service and repeated in-app prompts to rate or raise tickets.

Appalling customer service. They used to care about their customers, now they don't.Trustpilot, one star, August 5, 2024

Fixed by: vendors with phone support: QuickBooks Time, Deputy (on Core and above), Homebase (on paid plans).

No compliance rules

When I Work does not enforce meal break, overtime or fatigue rules at the point of scheduling, so a California or New York manager can publish a schedule that creates premium pay.

Fixed by: Deputy (break, overtime and fatigue rules, fair workweek), 7shifts (labor compliance for restaurants), Homebase (break and overtime alerts on Plus). Our break laws by state guide sets out the rules.

No payroll

Hours leave by export. A small business that wants scheduling, time and payroll on one bill cannot get it here.

Fixed by: Homebase ($49 plus $6 per employee), Rippling, QuickBooks Time with QuickBooks Payroll, Deputy payroll add-on.

Nothing beyond the schedule

No forms, checklists, training or document storage. Teams that need an onboarding flow or a daily checklist add a second app.

Fixed by: Connecteam (forms, training, chat; free for 10), 7shifts (manager logbook, task lists), Homebase (hiring and onboarding).

Per-user pricing at scale

At $5 a user, 150 staff on Pro is $750 a month; per-location products cost less for a busy single site and flat-base products cost less at 30 users.

Fixed by: Homebase (per location), Connecteam (flat base for 30 users), Sling ($1.70).

How to leave When I Work cleanly

  1. Export timesheets and schedules for the retention period; When I Work exports to CSV and to your payroll format.
  2. Download the employee list with positions, locations and wage rates.
  3. Switch at a pay-period boundary and publish the first new-tool schedule a full cycle ahead so staff see it in both apps once.
  4. Move the clock last. Keep punches in When I Work until the new kiosk or app is enrolled on every site; a crew that cannot clock in is the failure everyone notices.
  5. Issue the notice if the new tool uses GPS or a face-recognition kiosk: Connecticut, Delaware and New York require written notice and Illinois a biometric release. Our biometric privacy laws guide covers the kiosk question.

Key takeaways

  • When I Work in October 2026: Essentials $2.50, Pro $5, Premium $8 per user per month, with auto-scheduling on every tier, no minimum and no free plan; the pricing page is inconsistent about whether the time clock is included, so confirm before buying.
  • Cheaper: Sling (free for 30, then $1.70), ZoomShift (free for 20), Jibble (free clock for unlimited users), Zoho Shifts (free for 25).
  • Compliance rules at scheduling: Deputy ($5 to $9, $30 monthly minimum), 7shifts for restaurants, Homebase alerts on Plus.
  • Native payroll: Homebase ($49 plus $6 per employee), Rippling, QuickBooks Time with QuickBooks Payroll.
  • Forms, training and chat: Connecteam, free for ten users, then per hub.
  • When I Work's public review record is tiny (seven Trustpilot reviews); the reasons to leave are product gaps, not a pattern of complaints.
  • Switch at a pay-period boundary, move the clock last, and issue notices where GPS or facial recognition is involved.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to When I Work?

Sling if the goal is to pay less: free for 30 users and $1.70 a user for the clock, without auto-scheduling. Deputy if you need break, overtime and fatigue rules enforced at scheduling. Homebase if you run one to three sites and want payroll in the same product. 7shifts for restaurants.

Is there a free alternative to When I Work?

Sling is free for 30 users, Zoho Shifts for 25, ZoomShift for 20 at one location, Homebase for one location with no user cap, Connecteam for 10 users with every feature, Jibble for unlimited users as a time clock, and Clockify for 5. When I Work has no free plan.

How much does When I Work cost?

Essentials $2.50, Pro $5 and Premium $8 per user per month, shown as monthly prices on October 28, 2026, with a 14-day trial and no minimum. Annual prices are not displayed. The page's FAQ separately describes $2.50 for single-location and $5 for multi-location teams, and the time clock is shown as a per-plan toggle while the FAQ says every plan includes it; confirm with the vendor.

Does When I Work include a time clock?

The pricing page's FAQ says every plan includes time and attendance, but each plan card shows a time tracking toggle, and the clock was historically sold as an add-on. We could not resolve the contradiction from the page. Ask When I Work in writing whether Essentials includes the clock at $2.50.

Which When I Work alternatives have auto-scheduling?

Deputy (on Core and above), 7shifts (against sales forecasts), Homebase (AI scheduling assistant on Plus), Connecteam (auto-assignment on Operations Advanced) and Rippling. Sling, ZoomShift, Jibble, Clockify and Zoho Shifts schedule manually from templates.

Is Deputy better than When I Work?

Deputy enforces break, overtime and fatigue rules at scheduling, offers a facial-recognition kiosk and a payroll add-on, and costs $5 to $9 per user with a $30 monthly minimum. When I Work costs $2.50 to $8 with auto-scheduling on every tier and no minimum. Deputy is better where compliance costs money; When I Work is better for a small team on a budget. Our Deputy vs When I Work comparison goes through it.

Which alternative includes payroll?

Homebase runs payroll at $49 a month plus $6 per employee with tax filing in all 50 states; Rippling is a payroll platform; QuickBooks Time feeds QuickBooks Payroll; Deputy and 7shifts sell payroll add-ons. When I Work exports to Gusto, ADP, QuickBooks and Paychex.

Can I export my data from When I Work?

Yes. Timesheets export to CSV and to payroll formats, schedules export to CSV, and the employee list exports with positions and locations. Export the timesheets for your retention period before closing the account; the alternatives import employees from CSV but not historical schedules.
