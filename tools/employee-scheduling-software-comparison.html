Employee Scheduling Software Comparison: 12 Tools (2026)

P Pavan
October 6, 2026 · 12 min read

This page is the data behind the scheduling and attendance reviews in this series: twelve tools on fourteen criteria, with the pricing model spelled out, because in this category a per-user figure hides per-location plans (Homebase, 7shifts), headcount tiers, base fees (Buddy Punch, QuickBooks Time) and minimums (Deputy). A filter removes anything that lacks a feature you need or costs more than you will pay. It does not rank; the scheduling, shift scheduling and attendance reviews rank for their situations.

Filter the field

Set the platform, the most you will pay per user at entry, and which features are required or unwanted. Per-location and quote-priced tools show as high entry prices.

Entry price is the cheapest published per-user plan in October 2026; Homebase and 7shifts are per location and Humanity is quoted, so they are shown at $29 to $30 and 999 respectively. The pricing model column in the matrix explains each.

The 12 tools, alphabetically

One line each; the matrix carries the detail.

  • Connecteam: gPS clock inside an all-in-one app, free for ten.
  • Deputy: attendance with compliance rules built in.
  • Homebase: time clock on the POS, free for one shop.
  • Humanity (TCP Software): enterprise demand-driven scheduling.

  • Sling: mobile clock-in from $1.70.
  • Time Champ: attendance recorded automatically, no clock-in.
  • When I Work: attendance as a $1.50 to $2 add-on to cheap scheduling.

The tools in detail

7shifts

Schedules against POS sales forecasts
Runs on Web, iOS, Android, POS integrations Pricing (October 2026) Comp free for one location and 30 employees; Entrée $29.99 and The Works $69.99 per location per month on annual billing ($34.99 and $76.99 monthly); Gourmet by quote

What it is

Restaurant scheduling against POS sales forecasts with tip pooling and compliance; free for one location.

In one line

7shifts: the restaurant column; per location, POS forecasts, tips, free for one location.

Where it falls short

  • Restaurant-specific; little use elsewhere
  • Per-location pricing
  • Payroll only in some states

Buddy Punch

GPS, geofencing and photo punches, simply
Runs on Web, iOS, Android, kiosk Pricing (October 2026) Starter $4.99, Pro $6.99, Advanced $12.99 per user per month on annual billing ($5.99, $7.99, $13.99 monthly), each plus a $19 monthly base fee; payroll $8 per user plus $49

What it is

A straightforward time clock with GPS, geofencing, photos and PTO, plus a $19 base fee.

In one line

Buddy Punch: the plain-clock column; GPS and geofencing, scheduling on Pro, $19 base fee.

Where it falls short

  • $19 base fee on every plan
  • No forecasting or auto-scheduling
  • Scheduling only from Pro

Clockify

Attendance, overtime and time off at $5.49
Runs on Web, Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, kiosk Pricing (October 2026) Free for 5 users; Standard $5.49 (attendance, overtime, time off) and Pro $7.99 (scheduling, GPS) per seat per month on annual billing ($6.99 and $9.99 monthly)

What it is

Attendance and overtime at $5.49, scheduling and GPS at $7.99, on a free-for-five time tracker.

In one line

Clockify: the project-plus-shifts column; scheduling on Pro at $7.99, free for five.

Where it falls short

  • Scheduling only on Pro
  • No payroll
  • Dense interface

Connecteam

GPS clock inside an all-in-one app, free for ten
Runs on Web, iOS, Android, kiosk Pricing (October 2026) Small Business plan free for up to 10 users; Operations hub Basic $29, Advanced $49, Expert $99 per month for the first 30 users, then $0.80, $2.50 and $4.20 per additional user

What it is

An all-in-one deskless app with scheduling, GPS clock, forms, chat and training; free for ten users.

In one line

Connecteam: the deskless column; everything in one app, free for ten, priced for 30.

Where it falls short

  • Hubs are priced separately; buying all three adds up
  • Per-user charges above 30 users on each hub
  • Breadth over depth in scheduling

Deputy

Attendance with compliance rules built in
Runs on Web, iOS, Android, kiosk Pricing (October 2026) Lite $5, Core $6.50, Pro $9 per user per month (10% less on annual billing); $30 monthly minimum; payroll add-on $8 per user plus $49; HR $2

What it is

Demand-driven auto-scheduling with a compliant time clock, labour law rules by jurisdiction, and payroll and HR add-ons; $30 monthly minimum.

In one line

Deputy: the compliance column; auto-scheduling, forecasting, rules by jurisdiction, $30 minimum.

Where it falls short

  • $30 monthly minimum hurts very small teams
  • Payroll and HR are paid add-ons
  • More than a simple shop needs

Homebase

Time clock on the POS, free for one shop
Runs on Web, iOS, Android, POS kiosk Pricing (October 2026) Basic free for one location and 10 employees; Essentials $30, Plus $70, All-in-One $120 per location per month; payroll $49 plus $6 per employee

What it is

Scheduling, time clock, hiring and payroll for small hourly businesses, priced per location, free for one shop.

In one line

Homebase: the single-site column; per location, clock on the POS, payroll add-on.

Where it falls short

  • Per-location pricing gets expensive with many sites
  • Payroll is $49 plus $6 a head
  • Not for salaried or office teams

Humanity (TCP Software)

Enterprise demand-driven scheduling
Runs on Web, iOS, Android Pricing (October 2026) Quote only; enterprise demand-driven scheduling sold by TCP Software, typically for organisations of several hundred staff and up

What it is

Enterprise demand-driven scheduling from TCP Software for healthcare and large hourly operations; quote only.

In one line

Humanity: the enterprise column; demand- and credential-driven, quote only.

Where it falls short

  • Quote only; enterprise sales cycle

  • Implementation effort

Free attendance for unlimited users with a face-recognition kiosk, GPS and overtime rules.

How these tools were evaluated

I evaluated each tool against the same checklist: what it captures and how configurable that is, which platforms it runs on, how it treats the employee (visible or silent agent, access to their own data, screenshot blurring and private time), what the published pricing is and what the minimum commitment really costs, how long data is kept, where it can be deployed, and what the vendor's own documentation and verified user reviews say about reliability and support. Pricing was taken from each vendor's pricing page in October 2026; where a vendor publishes no price, the page says so rather than guessing. Free plans and trials were used where they exist. I did not run a formal benchmark, and the page does not claim one.

Disclosure. I have done work for Time Champ. It appears here because it belongs in this category, it is held to the same criteria as every other product, its drawbacks are listed like everyone else's, and it bought no placement. There are no affiliate or vendor links on this page.

Key takeaways

  • Twelve tools, fourteen criteria, no ranking; read the pricing model before the price.
  • Per-location tools (Homebase, 7shifts) are cheapest for one busy site and most expensive for many small ones; base-fee tools are expensive under ten people.
  • Free plans are real: Sling for 30, Jibble unlimited, Connecteam for ten, Homebase and 7shifts for one location.
  • Filter by the features you need absent as well as present.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compare employee scheduling software?

Start with the pricing model, because per-user, per-location, base-fee, tiered and minimum-spend models change the order at every headcount; then rule out tools missing a feature you need (swaps, clock, GPS, compliance, payroll); then compare what is left for your sector. The matrix and filter on this page follow that order.

Which scheduling software is free?

Sling for up to 30 users (scheduling, availability, swaps, messaging), Jibble for unlimited users (attendance with light scheduling), Connecteam for ten users (its whole app), Homebase for one location with ten employees, and 7shifts for one location with 30. Free tiers generally lack forecasting and auto-scheduling.

Which scheduling tools include a time clock?

Deputy, Homebase, 7shifts, Connecteam, Clockify (Pro), QuickBooks Time, Sling (Premium), Buddy Punch, Jibble and Humanity include one. When I Work sells it as a $1.50 to $2 add-on. Time Champ records attendance automatically instead of a clock-in.

Which scheduling software handles break and overtime compliance?

Deputy encodes break, overtime and predictive-scheduling rules by jurisdiction; Homebase, 7shifts, Connecteam, Buddy Punch, Clockify, QuickBooks Time and Humanity apply break and overtime rules you configure; Sling and When I Work track overtime but not break rules. Check daily overtime for California and fair workweek rules for covered cities.

Which scheduling tools work with payroll?

QuickBooks Time feeds QuickBooks payroll natively; Deputy and Homebase sell payroll add-ons; 7shifts offers payroll in some states; When I Work, Connecteam, Buddy Punch, Jibble and Humanity integrate or export to payroll providers; Sling, Clockify and Time Champ export hours.

What does the entry price leave out?

Per-location pricing (Homebase, 7shifts), base fees (Buddy Punch $19, QuickBooks Time $20 or $40), Deputy's $30 minimum, When I Work's clock add-on, Connecteam's per-user charges above 30 and separate hubs, and the plan that actually carries the feature you need, which is often a tier up.
