QuickBooks Time Alternatives (2026): 10 Clocks, No Base Fee

P Pavan
October 21, 2026 · 15 min read

A QuickBooks Time alternative is a time clock for hourly staff that exports to payroll without QuickBooks Time's price structure: a $20 or $40 monthly base fee plus $10 or $12 per user, up $2 per user since July 1, 2026. People leave for four reasons. The base fee, which makes a three-person crew pay $16.67 a head: OnTheClock charges $5 plus $4, and Timeero, Clockify and Jibble have no base fee. Geofencing on Elite only: Timeero, OnTheClock, Connecteam and Workyard include it lower down. Dependence on a QuickBooks account: OnTheClock, Workyard, Homebase and Deputy work with other payrolls or run their own. And overtime alerts that suggest rather than enforce: Workyard and Timeero apply state break rules.

The pages that rank for this search are written by Toggl, Connecteam, Workyard and Time Doctor, each ranking itself first, and several still quote the per-user prices Intuit raised in July. This guide gives October 2026 prices read from each vendor's page, a cost table at 5, 15 and 50 employees, GPS and payroll by plan, and a cancellation sequence that keeps payroll running. There are no vendor links and nothing paid for its place.

The short answer

If the base fee plus per user is the problem, OnTheClock charges $5 base plus $4 per employee with GPS, geofencing and a kiosk, and Timeero and Clockify have no base fee at all. A ten-person crew pays $120 a month on QuickBooks Time Premium, $45 on OnTheClock and $50 on Timeero Basic.

If GPS and geofencing are the job, Timeero puts GPS and mileage on its cheapest plan and geofencing on the middle one; QuickBooks Time keeps geofencing for Elite at $40 plus $12.

If you want to stay in QuickBooks payroll, every tool below exports to QuickBooks Online; OnTheClock also names ADP, Gusto and Paychex. Only QuickBooks Time requires a QuickBooks account to run.

What QuickBooks Time costs and does in October 2026

QuickBooks Time

The incumbent
Runs on Web; iOS and Android through the QuickBooks Workforce app; tablet or desktop kiosk (not mobile) Pricing (October 2026) Time Premium $20 base plus $10 per user per month; Time Elite $40 base plus $12 per user; the base fee is halved for the first three months on new subscriptions; one admin included; monthly billing only; 30-day trial of the base fee, per-user fees still charged; per-user fees rose $2 on July 1, 2026; also bundled in QuickBooks Workforce Premium (plus $13 per employee) and Elite ($134 base plus $17) Monitoring GPS and photo capture; no desktop monitoring

What it is

QuickBooks Time, the former TSheets that Intuit bought in January 2018, is a time clock and scheduler that feeds QuickBooks Online, Payroll and Desktop. Both plans include timesheets with approval workflows, scheduling, time off, overtime alerts, a who's-working dashboard, a tablet kiosk with optional facial recognition and photo attachments, and phone and chat support. Elite adds GPS geofencing, timesheet signatures, project tracking with estimates against actuals, an activity feed and mileage. Intuit's product page says the integration requires an active QuickBooks Online, Payroll or Desktop account.

Better than QuickBooks Time

  • Hours flow straight into QuickBooks payroll with no mapping each run
  • Scheduling, kiosk with facial recognition and approvals on every plan
  • Large installed base and phone support

Where QuickBooks Time is still better

  • Base fee plus per user: $120 a month for ten people on Premium before the promo ends
  • Per-user fees up $2 on July 1, 2026, from $8 and $10 to $10 and $12
  • Geofencing, signatures and project tracking only on Elite
  • Tied to a QuickBooks account

Who should switch to it

Hourly crews already running QuickBooks Online Payroll who value the native sync over price. Our attendance tracking software review places it among its peers.

Prices are from quickbooks.intuit.com on October 21, 2026. Intuit's own product page still shows a placeholder per-worker fee, and several competitor articles still quote the pre-July rates, so compare against the pricing page, not the product page or a third party.

Why people leave QuickBooks Time

QuickBooks Time's review-site scores are good. People leave over price structure and dependence on QuickBooks, not over the app failing. The reasons below are the documented ones.

Base fee plus per user

A $20 or $40 monthly base before anyone clocks in makes small crews pay the most per head. Three employees on Premium cost $50 a month, $16.67 each. The same three on OnTheClock cost $17.

Fixed by: OnTheClock ($5 base), Timeero, Clockify and Jibble (no base fee).

The July 2026 increase

Per-user fees rose from $8 to $10 on Premium and $10 to $12 on Elite on July 1, 2026, according to the customer notice reported by competitors and confirmed by the current pricing page. A 25-person Elite account went from $290 to $340 a month.

Fixed by: flat per-user tools with published annual discounts: Timeero, Clockify, Deputy.

Geofencing costs the Elite tier

Restricting clock-ins to the job site is the feature field employers want most, and it sits on Elite at $40 plus $12. Premium gets GPS stamps at best.

Fixed by: Timeero Pro ($7.50), OnTheClock ($4 plus $5), Connecteam Advanced, Workyard Pro.

Locked to QuickBooks

The integration requires an active QuickBooks Online, Payroll or Desktop account. A business that moves payroll to Gusto, ADP or Paychex loses the reason it bought the product.

Fixed by: OnTheClock (QuickBooks, Gusto, ADP, Paychex, Paylocity), Workyard (QuickBooks, ADP, Gusto), Homebase and Deputy with their own payroll.

Battery drain and GPS accuracy

Reviews describe background GPS draining phones on long shifts and occasional unexpected clock-outs. Any GPS clock has this problem; the tools that handle it best let you choose GPS stamps on punches rather than continuous tracking.

Fixed by: punch-stamp GPS by default: OnTheClock, Jibble, Clockify.

Overtime alerts are suggestions

QuickBooks Time alerts managers to approaching overtime; it does not enforce break or overtime rules by state. Employers in California, Washington and Oregon need meal-break enforcement, not reminders.

Fixed by: Workyard (state, federal or custom break and overtime rules on Starter), Timeero (California break policy on Pro), Jibble (overtime and paid-break calculation).

The alternatives in detail

OnTheClock

The direct price comparison
Runs on Web, iOS, Android, kiosk Pricing (October 2026) $4 per employee per month plus a $5 monthly base fee, one plan, with GPS, geofencing, IP restriction, kiosk, scheduling, PTO and overtime calculation; payroll add-on $6 per paid employee plus $40 base with a one-time $250 onboarding fee; 30-day trial without a card; no contract

What it is

OnTheClock is the tool to put beside QuickBooks Time on a spreadsheet: the same base-plus-per-user shape at a quarter of the base and under half the per-user rate, with the geofencing and IP restriction that QuickBooks Time keeps for Elite. It names QuickBooks, Gusto, ADP, Paychex, Paylocity, Sage and SurePayroll as payroll integrations and offers its own payroll.

Better than QuickBooks Time

  • $5 base plus $4 against $20 plus $10
  • Geofencing, kiosk and IP restriction on the one plan
  • The widest named payroll integration list here

Where QuickBooks Time is still better

  • Smaller vendor; no facial recognition except on its enterprise hardware clock
  • Its own pricing calculator showed a total that did not match its listed rates on the day we checked; confirm
  • Scheduling is simpler than Deputy's or Homebase's

Who should switch to it

Small hourly crews that want the QuickBooks Time feature set without the invoice.

Switching

Export approved timesheets from QuickBooks Time by pay period; OnTheClock maps employees to QuickBooks once and syncs from the next run.

How to leave QuickBooks Time without breaking payroll

  1. Export before you cancel. Intuit's own cancellation article says to export first; reports stop when the subscription ends, though timesheets are preserved and reachable by renewing.
  2. Time the switch to a pay period, and run the new tool in parallel for one cycle so approved hours match before the new tool feeds payroll.
  3. Rebuild the payroll mapping once. Every alternative here exports to QuickBooks Online; map employees, customers and service items in the new tool before the first sync.
  4. Cancel in the right place. If QuickBooks Time was added through QuickBooks Online or Payroll, cancel the add-on there; standalone accounts cancel under Billing and Subscription. Payment details are deleted on cancellation; data is not.
  5. Check break and GPS settings against state law. Meal-break rules in California, Illinois, New York, Washington, Oregon and Colorado and the biometric consent rules for face-recognition kiosks in Illinois are covered in our clock in clock out app review.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to QuickBooks Time?

OnTheClock for most small crews: $5 base plus $4 per employee with GPS, geofencing, kiosk and named integrations with QuickBooks, Gusto, ADP and Paychex. Timeero for crews that drive. Homebase for single-site shift businesses. Workyard for construction crews that need state break rules enforced. Jibble if free matters most. The chooser above ranks them by crew type.

How much does QuickBooks Time cost in 2026?

Time Premium is $20 a month plus $10 per user and Time Elite is $40 plus $12 per user, billed monthly, with the base fee halved for the first three months on new subscriptions. Per-user fees rose from $8 and $10 on July 1, 2026. It is also bundled in QuickBooks Workforce Premium and Elite.

Is QuickBooks Time the same as TSheets?

Yes. Intuit announced the TSheets acquisition on December 5, 2017 and closed it in January 2018, then renamed the product QuickBooks Time. The mobile app is now called QuickBooks Workforce.

Does QuickBooks Time require QuickBooks Online?

Intuit's product page states that the integration requires an active QuickBooks Online, Payroll or Desktop account, and the pricing page says Time plans require QuickBooks Online. If you are leaving QuickBooks payroll, OnTheClock, Workyard, Homebase and Deputy connect to other providers or run their own.

Which alternative has GPS and geofencing on a cheap plan?

OnTheClock includes both on its single $4-plus-$5 plan. Timeero has GPS and mileage on Basic at $5 and geofencing on Pro at $7.50. Workyard has GPS on every plan and geofencing on Pro. QuickBooks Time keeps geofencing for Elite at $40 plus $12.

Is there a free alternative to QuickBooks Time?

Jibble is free for unlimited users with GPS stamps, a facial recognition kiosk, timesheets and QuickBooks export. Homebase is free for up to 10 employees at one location, Connecteam for up to 10 users, and Clockify for 5 users. None charges a base fee.

Can I cancel QuickBooks Time without losing my data?

Yes. Intuit's help article says timesheets are preserved after cancellation and can be reached again by renewing, while payment details are deleted immediately. Export reports before you cancel, because reporting stops with the subscription. If it was added through QuickBooks Online or Payroll, cancel the add-on there.

Which alternatives enforce break and overtime rules?

Workyard applies state, federal or custom break and overtime rules on its Starter plan and automatic meal-break rules on Autopilot. Timeero has a California break policy on Pro. Deputy lists labor law compliance on Lite. Jibble calculates overtime and paid breaks. QuickBooks Time sends overtime alerts that managers can decline.
