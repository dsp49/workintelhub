Toggl Track Pricing (2026): New Plans, Legacy and Add-ons

P Pavan
November 9, 2026 · 10 min read

Toggl costs $9 per user per month for Starter and $16 for Premium on annual billing, or $12 and $24 month to month, with a Free plan for up to three members and Enterprise by quote, plus two paid add-ons: Time Off Management at $2 per seat and Capacity Management at $5 to $6 per member. Those are the November 2026 prices for the product Toggl relaunched in September 2026, which merged Toggl Track and Toggl Plan. Existing Toggl Track customers are still on the older list, where Premium is $18 annual and $20 monthly, which is why two different Premium prices circulate.

This page sets out both price lists, what each plan includes, the billing rules that decide what you pay (licenses are billed whether assigned or vacant), what teams of 3, 10, 25 and 100 actually spend, the price history from 2024 through the relaunch, the standard complaints and when a competitor is the better buy. Toggl is the simplest tracker in the category and the one with the firmest no-monitoring policy; it is also the most expensive way to buy timesheet approvals.

The table shows what Toggl is and is not. It is a project time tracker with strong reporting and a deliberate refusal to monitor: no screenshots, no activity scores, no GPS, no kiosk, and no invoicing. Teams that need approvals pay $16, which is the single most common objection to Toggl's pricing; teams that need attendance or a clock need a different product. Capacity and time-off features that used to be in Toggl Plan are now partly in Starter and partly in two paid add-ons.

Billing rules that change the price

  • Licenses, not active users. You pay for the number of licenses on the subscription, "assigned or vacant". Deactivating a member frees the license but it stays on the bill until an administrator removes it. Do both when someone leaves.
  • Proration. Added licenses are billed pro rata to the renewal date. On monthly plans a lower license count applies from the next invoice; on annual plans a reduction waits for renewal and is not refunded. You cannot reduce licenses below the current member count.
  • Trial. Thirty days of Premium features without a payment method; it ends automatically and drops the organization to Free. The two add-ons have their own 30-day trials, and the Capacity Management trial converts to paid unless auto-subscribe is turned off.
  • Add-ons are billed differently from each other. Time Off Management is $2 per assigned seat with no annual discount; Capacity Management is charged for every active member of the organization and cannot be limited to a subset.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period under a stated no-refunds policy. Data is kept and features are locked; an organization with more members than Free allows is suspended until the extra members are removed. Free-plan data is deleted after six months of inactivity.
  • Upgrades and downgrades. Starter to Premium is immediate with a prorated charge; Premium to Starter waits for the end of the prepaid term.
  • Discounts. Registered nonprofits, educational institutions and students qualify for a discount on request with an institutional email; students can get Starter free for up to six months. Discounts run six months on monthly or twelve on annual billing and must be renewed by request. The percentage is not published.
  • Minimums. None stated for any plan; Enterprise has no published seat minimum, though legacy onboarding help was offered from 40 users.

Price history

Toggl Track's prices were stable for several years at $9 a user for Starter and $18 for Premium on annual billing, with $10 and $20 month to month, which is what Toggl's own community post of November 2024 lists. Between then and July 2026 the Starter monthly price rose to $12, with the annual price unchanged. In September 2026 Toggl relaunched as a single product combining Track and Plan, with a new price list: Free cut to three members, Starter still $9 annual but $12 monthly, Premium reduced to $16 annual and raised to $24 monthly, and two paid add-ons for time off and capacity planning that replaced features of Toggl Plan (whose legacy plans were $8 and $13.35 a user on annual billing). The net effect is that annual Premium is cheaper than before and everything month to month is dearer, which is a push toward annual contracts. Toggl was acquired by Bending Spoons in 2024 along with Harvest, and its documentation now alludes to renewal price changes without giving figures.

What buyers complain about

Approvals cost $16

The feature most managed teams need sits on Premium, and Starter has no equivalent. A ten-person team pays $160 a month for approvals that Clockify sells for $54.90.

Fixed by: deciding whether Toggl's adoption advantage is worth about $100 a month at that size, or using Clockify or Harvest for approvals

Vacant licenses keep billing

Deactivating a leaver does not remove the license; the seat is billed until an admin deletes it, and annual reductions wait for renewal.

Fixed by: removing the license in the same step as deactivating the user, and buying annual licenses only for stable headcount

The free plan shrank to three

New organizations get three members; small teams that relied on five now pay $27 a month on Starter.

Fixed by: Clockify's five-user Free, or Jibble and TimeCamp with no cap, if free is the requirement

Capacity Management bills everyone

The $5 add-on cannot be bought for a planning team only; it is charged for every active member.

Fixed by: using Starter's built-in timeline and PTO basics unless the whole team is planned

No invoicing

Toggl reports billable amounts but will not produce an invoice; agencies export to QuickBooks or FreshBooks or move to Harvest.

Fixed by: Harvest if invoices are the output, or Clockify Standard, which invoices for $5.49

If Toggl is not the right price

If approvals or invoicing are the reason you are shopping, Clockify Standard at $5.49 does both, and our Toggl vs Clockify comparison covers the trade-off against Toggl's simpler timer; Clockify pricing has every tier. If invoices are the output, Harvest at $11 (or $13.75 monthly) includes them with approvals and expenses. If you want capacity planning without the per-member add-on, Everhour's Team plan includes it. If your team is three people, Toggl's Free plan remains one of the best, and if you want free for more than three, Jibble and TimeCamp have no user cap. The full list is in Toggl Track alternatives.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Toggl Track cost per user?

For new customers in November 2026: Starter $9 per user per month on annual billing or $12 monthly, Premium $16 annual or $24 monthly, Free for up to three members, Enterprise by quote. Existing Toggl Track customers on legacy plans pay $9 or $12 for Starter and $18 or $20 for Premium. Time Off Management adds $2 per seat and Capacity Management $5 to $6 per member.

Is Toggl Track free?

Yes, for up to three members on new accounts, with timers, projects, tags, calendar integrations, a desktop timeline and personal reports. Older workspaces were allowed five. The Free plan has no billable rates, team reports or exports beyond a PDF summary, and its API is limited to 30 report requests a month.

Why do I see Toggl Premium at both $16 and $18?

Toggl relaunched in September 2026 as a single product with a new price list, where Premium is $16 on annual billing and $24 monthly. Existing Toggl Track customers remain on the legacy list, where Premium is $18 annual and $20 monthly, and Toggl's support articles still show those figures. Which applies depends on when you signed up.

Which Toggl plan includes timesheet approvals?

Premium only, at $16 per user on annual billing for new customers. Starter has billable rates, team reports and exports but no approval workflow. This is the most common reason teams compare Toggl with Clockify, where approvals are on the $5.49 Standard plan.

Does Toggl Track have invoicing?

No. Toggl reports billable amounts by client and project and exports them, but does not generate invoices on any plan. Harvest, Clockify Standard and Hubstaff Grow all include invoicing.

Does Toggl charge for inactive users?

It charges for licenses, not activity. Every license on the subscription is billed whether assigned or vacant, so a deactivated member's license keeps billing until an administrator removes it. On annual plans a reduced license count takes effect at renewal.

What are Toggl's add-ons?

Time Off Management, $2 per assigned seat per month with no annual discount, for leave requests and balances; and Capacity Management, $6 per member monthly or $5 on annual billing, charged for every active member, for workload and availability planning. Both require a paid plan and come with a 30-day trial.

Does Toggl offer discounts for nonprofits or students?

Yes. Registered nonprofits, educational institutions and students can request a discount with an institutional email; students can receive Starter free for up to six months. The discount lasts six months on monthly or twelve on annual billing and must be requested again before it expires. Toggl does not publish the percentage.
