Best Workforce Productivity Software: 8 Platforms (2026)

P Pavan
October 6, 2026 · 14 min read

Workforce productivity software answers organisation-level questions rather than individual ones: do we have the right number of people in the right teams, is the meeting load sustainable, which teams are at capacity, what is after-hours work doing to attrition, and is a hybrid policy or a reorganisation working. The same vendors sell to managers and to leadership, but the leadership product is the aggregated, trended, de-identified view, and two of the strongest tools here never install anything on a device at all.

I reviewed eight platforms for that use, with pricing checked in October 2026. The first three represent the three routes to a workforce view: a device agent with organisation analytics (ActivTrak), Microsoft 365 signals (Viva Insights) and cloud application data (Prodoscore). The rest fit particular organisations. The productivity monitoring review covers the same products from the manager's side and the workforce analytics page covers the HR data platforms that sit next to them.

The tools in detail

ActivTrak

The most complete workforce productivity analytics
Runs on Windows, macOS, Chromebook Pricing (October 2026) Free for 3 users; Work Activity Tracking $10, Workforce Management $15, Productivity Optimization $17, Workflow $21 per user per month, annual

What it is

Productivity analytics from app and website use with utilisation, focus and coaching reports; optional screenshots, no keystrokes.

Why it is on this list

ActivTrak leads at the organisation level because its Productivity Optimization and Workflow plans are built for it: organisation-wide productivity trends, utilisation and workload balance by team, capacity and headcount planning, technology usage and cost, and AI-identified workflow improvements, with a year of history and more on request. For a leadership team asking where the organisation's time goes and whether it has the right number of people, it is the most complete answer here.

Where it is strong

  • Utilisation, focus and workload reports managers act on
  • No keystrokes; screenshots optional
  • Free for three people
  • Coaching prompts and capacity planning on the upper plans

Where it falls short

  • No Linux
  • The reports that justify it are on the $15 and $17 plans
  • Retention 6 to 12 months unless extended

Microsoft Viva Insights

Collaboration and wellbeing patterns with nothing installed
Runs on Microsoft 365: Teams, Outlook, web Pricing (October 2026) Personal insights included in Microsoft 365 E3 and E5; organisational insights $6 per user per month; the full Viva Suite $12 per user per month

What it is

Focus time, meeting load and after-hours patterns from Microsoft 365 signals, de-identified for managers and leaders.

Why it is on this list

Microsoft Viva Insights is second for any organisation already on Microsoft 365 because it answers the workforce-level questions, meeting load, after-hours work, focus time, manager one-on-one coverage and collaboration patterns, from signals the organisation already holds, de-identified and aggregated with minimum group sizes, with nothing installed on anyone's device. Personal insights are free with E3 and E5; the organisational layer is $6 a user. It knows nothing about applications outside Microsoft.

Where it is strong

  • Nothing to install; uses Microsoft 365 signals
  • De-identified, aggregated by design
  • Personal insights free with E3 and E5
  • Shows meeting load, after-hours work and focus time

Where it falls short

  • Microsoft 365 only
  • No application-level data outside Microsoft tools
  • Organisational insights cost $6 on top of E3 or E5

Prodoscore

An agentless daily score across the workforce
Runs on Agentless: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, CRM and phone connectors; optional desktop agent Pricing (October 2026) Basic $19.99 per user per month with a 15-seat minimum ($300 a month); Premium and Enterprise by quote; trial for up to 25 users

What it is

An agentless daily productivity score derived from Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and CRM activity.

Why it is on this list

Prodoscore is third as the agentless alternative for organisations that live in Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 and CRM and phone systems: a daily productivity score per person and team, trends across the workforce, and no software on the device. Its design suits sales and service organisations; its $19.99 price and fifteen-seat minimum make it a mid-market purchase.

Where it is strong

  • No agent and no screenshots
  • Works from Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and CRM data
  • A single daily score leaders understand
  • Strong fit for sales and service teams

Where it falls short

  • $19.99 with a 15-seat minimum
  • Needs the team to live in cloud tools
  • A score is a blunt instrument

Utilisation and location trends with two years of history

Agent or agentless

The device agent sees applications, websites and activity on any stack, which is why ActivTrak, Insightful and Time Champ can answer capacity and attendance questions that Viva Insights cannot. The agentless route sees only what the cloud platform holds, email, meetings, chat, documents, CRM, but sees it for the whole organisation at once, with nothing to deploy, no screenshots possible, and aggregation and minimum group sizes built in. For leadership questions about collaboration and wellbeing the agentless route is often enough and far less contentious; for capacity and attendance it is not. Many organisations run Viva Insights for the leadership view and an agent only where attendance or utilisation genuinely requires it. The workplace analytics guide covers the agentless category in more depth.

Governance for organisation-level data

  • Aggregate by default. Leadership dashboards should show teams, not names; Viva enforces minimum group sizes and ActivTrak can be configured to.
  • Separate the manager view from the leadership view, with different access and different retention.
  • Publish what is collected. A workforce analytics programme that employees learn about from a news story is over; the policy template and the ethical monitoring playbook set out the disclosure.
  • Do not automate decisions. Headcount, pay and performance decisions informed by the data are a leadership responsibility, not a dashboard output; regulators in several states are moving on automated employment decisions.
  • Retain for the question, not forever. Trend analysis needs a year or two; it does not need individual-level data for that long.

How these tools were evaluated

I evaluated each tool against the same checklist: what it captures and how configurable that is, which platforms it runs on, how it treats the employee (visible or silent agent, access to their own data, screenshot blurring and private time), what the published pricing is and what the minimum commitment really costs, how long data is kept, where it can be deployed, and what the vendor's own documentation and verified user reviews say about reliability and support. Pricing was taken from each vendor's pricing page in October 2026; where a vendor publishes no price, the page says so rather than guessing. Free plans and trials were used where they exist. I did not run a formal benchmark, and the page does not claim one.

Disclosure. I have done work for Time Champ. It appears here because it belongs in this category, it is held to the same criteria as every other product, its drawbacks are listed like everyone else's, and it bought no placement. There are no affiliate or vendor links on this page.

Key takeaways

  • Workforce productivity software answers organisation-level questions: capacity, meeting load, after-hours work, hybrid policy effects, tool usage and attendance across sites.
  • Three routes: a device agent with organisation analytics (ActivTrak, Insightful, Time Champ), Microsoft 365 signals (Viva Insights), cloud application data (Prodoscore).
  • Agentless answers collaboration and wellbeing questions with nothing installed; capacity and attendance need an agent.
  • Aggregate by default, separate manager and leadership views, publish what is collected, never automate decisions, retain for the question.

Frequently asked questions

What is workforce productivity software?

Software that reports productivity patterns at the organisation level rather than the individual: utilisation and capacity by team, meeting load, after-hours work, focus time, application usage and attendance across sites, trended over months. It uses either a device agent (ActivTrak, Insightful, Time Champ, WorkTime, DeskTime, Time Doctor) or cloud platform signals (Microsoft Viva Insights, Prodoscore).

What is the best workforce productivity software?

ActivTrak for the most complete organisation analytics from a device agent, including capacity and headcount planning. Microsoft Viva Insights for Microsoft 365 organisations that want meeting, after-hours and collaboration patterns with nothing installed. Prodoscore for an agentless score across sales and service teams in cloud tools. Insightful suits hybrid capacity questions; Time Champ suits multi-site attendance and productivity at low cost.

Is Microsoft Viva Insights free?

Personal insights are included in Microsoft 365 E3 and E5. The organisational insights that managers and leaders use cost $6 per user per month as an add-on, and the full Viva Suite, which bundles Insights with Connections, Engage, Learning, Goals and the other modules, is $12 per user per month.

Can workforce productivity software work without installing anything?

Yes. Microsoft Viva Insights uses the signals Microsoft 365 already holds (email, meetings, chat, documents) and Prodoscore connects to Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, CRMs and phone systems. Neither can see applications outside those platforms, so capacity and attendance questions usually still need a device agent.

How is workforce productivity data kept private?

Through aggregation and minimum group sizes (Viva Insights enforces them; ActivTrak and others can be configured to), separate access for manager and leadership views, published disclosure of what is collected, no automated employment decisions from the data, and retention limited to what trend analysis needs. The ethical monitoring playbook on this site sets out the design.

How much does workforce productivity software cost?

In October 2026 on annual billing: Time Champ $3.90 (Enterprise $13.90 for the organisation views), WorkTime $5.83, Viva Insights organisational $6 on top of Microsoft 365, DeskTime $6.42, Time Doctor $6.70 (Standard $11.70), Insightful $8, ActivTrak $10 (Productivity Optimization $17 for organisation analytics), Prodoscore $19.99 with a fifteen-seat minimum, per user per month.
