Workforce Management Platforms Compared: 12 Platforms (2026)

P Pavan
October 6, 2026 · 12 min read

This page is the reference table behind the workforce management reviews in this series: twelve platforms on twelve criteria, with the pricing model and the size each one fits spelled out, because in this category the honest comparison is between a $5 scheduler and a quoted suite that includes payroll, and the matrix has to say so. A filter removes platforms that lack a capability you need or exceed what you will pay at entry. It does not rank; the workforce management review and the by-size guide do that.

Most of the platforms on this page are quote-priced. Where I give a figure for a quote-only vendor it is the range independent buyer surveys and procurement advisers report, labelled as an estimate, not a price list; your quote will depend on headcount, modules, contract length and how late in the quarter you sign. Published prices are stated as published.

The tools in detail

ADP Workforce Now

Payroll and compliance everywhere, time on Premium
Runs on Web, iOS, Android, ADP clocks Pricing (October 2026) Quote only. Select covers payroll and HR, Plus adds benefits, Premium adds time and attendance; independent estimates run $23 to $30 per employee per month for the platform, more with outsourced services, plus setup fees

What it is

Payroll-first HCM with time and attendance on the Premium tier and the largest marketplace; quote only.

In one line

ADP: the payroll column; time and attendance on Premium, quote only.

Where it falls short

  • Quote only; negotiated pricing
  • Scheduling is adequate, not specialist
  • Service quality varies by account team

Connecteam

Everything in one app, free for ten
Runs on Web, iOS, Android, kiosk Pricing (October 2026) Free for up to 10 users; Operations hub Basic $29, Advanced $49, Expert $99 per month for the first 30 users then $0.80, $2.50 and $4.20 per extra user; HR and Communications hubs priced separately

What it is

Deskless workforce management in one app; free for ten, priced for the first 30 users.

In one line

Connecteam: the deskless column; free for ten, no payroll.

Where it falls short

  • Light compliance depth

One engine for HR, pay, time and schedules

Size fit

The size column is the one to read first. Under fifty people, Connecteam, When I Work, Deputy and Time Champ fit and the suites do not; from fifty to five hundred, Deputy, Rippling, Paycor and UKG Ready; from five hundred to five thousand, UKG, Dayforce, ADP and Paylocity; above that, UKG Pro, Workday and Dayforce, with Legion for large hourly retail and hospitality. A platform outside its band costs more than it should or does less than it must. The growing businesses review covers the transitions between bands.

How these tools were evaluated

I evaluated each tool against the same checklist: what it captures and how configurable that is, which platforms it runs on, how it treats the employee (visible or silent agent, access to their own data, screenshot blurring and private time), what the published pricing is and what the minimum commitment really costs, how long data is kept, where it can be deployed, and what the vendor's own documentation and verified user reviews say about reliability and support. Pricing was taken from each vendor's pricing page in October 2026; where a vendor publishes no price, the page says so rather than guessing. Free plans and trials were used where they exist. I did not run a formal benchmark, and the page does not claim one.

Disclosure. I have done work for Time Champ. It appears here because it belongs in this category, it is held to the same criteria as every other product, its drawbacks are listed like everyone else's, and it bought no placement. There are no affiliate or vendor links on this page.

Key takeaways

  • Twelve platforms, twelve criteria, no ranking; read the pricing model and size fit before anything else.
  • Published-price specialists (Deputy, When I Work, Connecteam, Time Champ) and quoted suites (UKG, ADP, Dayforce, Workday, Paycor, Paylocity, Legion) are different purchases; Rippling publishes an entry price.
  • Quote-only figures on the cards are labelled estimates from buyer surveys.
  • Filter by the capabilities you need absent as well as present.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compare workforce management platforms?

Start with size fit and pricing model, because a $5 per-user scheduler and a quoted per-employee suite are different purchases; then rule out platforms lacking a capability you need (native payroll, demand forecasting, compliance engine); then compare the rest for your workforce type. The matrix and filter on this page follow that order.

Which workforce management platforms publish pricing?

Deputy ($5 to $9 per user), When I Work ($2.50 to $8 plus a clock add-on), Connecteam ($29 to $99 for 30 users), Time Champ ($3.90 to $13.90) and Rippling ($8 per employee plus $35 entry) publish prices. UKG, ADP Workforce Now, Dayforce, Workday, Paylocity, Legion and Paycor are quote-only.

Which workforce management platforms include payroll?

UKG, ADP Workforce Now, Dayforce, Workday (in some countries), Rippling, Paycor and Paylocity run payroll natively; Deputy sells it as an add-on; When I Work, Connecteam, Legion and Time Champ integrate with or export to payroll providers.

Which platforms have a real compliance engine?

UKG (Ready and Pro WFM) has the deepest rules engine for overtime, breaks, predictive scheduling and union rules; Dayforce and Deputy encode multi-jurisdiction rules; ADP, Paycor, Paylocity, Rippling, Legion and Workday apply rules you configure, with payroll compliance strong in the payroll-first suites. When I Work, Connecteam and Time Champ track overtime but leave most rules to you.

What is the difference between UKG Ready and UKG Pro Workforce Management?

UKG Ready is an all-in-one HR, payroll, time and scheduling suite for organisations from about fifty to a few thousand people. UKG Pro Workforce Management (formerly Kronos Workforce Dimensions) is the enterprise time, scheduling, forecasting and compliance system, typically paired with UKG Pro HCM or another HR system of record, for thousands of employees. Both are quote-priced.

Is Workday a workforce management platform?

Workday is an enterprise HCM system of record with workforce management modules: time tracking, absence, scheduling for frontline workers, and planning and analytics on the same data. It is priced per employee on payroll on multi-year terms and suits organisations above about a thousand people; below that it is the wrong purchase.
