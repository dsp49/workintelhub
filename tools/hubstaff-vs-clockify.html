Hubstaff vs Clockify (2026): Price, Monitoring, Who Suits

P Pavan
November 9, 2026 · 10 min read

Hubstaff and Clockify are both time trackers with projects, timesheets, approvals and invoicing, and they are bought by the same kinds of teams, which is why the comparison comes up. They differ on one axis that matters more than any feature list: Hubstaff takes screenshots and scores activity on every paid plan, and Clockify takes no images on any plan. Price follows: Clockify Standard with approvals and invoicing is $5.49 per seat on annual billing; Hubstaff Grow with the same plus screenshots is $7.50, and Hubstaff Team with payroll, scheduling and GPS job sites is $10.

This comparison sets out the November 2026 prices and what teams of 5, 15 and 50 pay, a feature-by-feature table, the monitoring difference and what it means legally and culturally, a chooser that scores the two against your situation, detailed cards on each, and how to move between them. The short version: Clockify if you will not monitor, Hubstaff if proof of work or payroll from tracked hours is the point.

The verdict

Clockify if you want timesheets, approvals, invoicing and a kiosk at the lowest price in the category, and you do not want to watch anyone: Standard at $5.49 per seat does what Hubstaff charges $7.50 to $10 for, with kiosk seats from $0.79 and no screenshots on any plan.

Hubstaff if proof of work is part of the job: screenshots, activity levels, idle detection and app and URL logs on every paid plan, plus payroll, GPS job sites and scheduling on Team. It costs more and it is monitoring software, with everything that implies for the people being tracked.

The dividing question is not price or features but whether you need to see what people were doing. If the answer is no, Clockify is cheaper and simpler. If the answer is yes, Clockify's opt-in activity monitor on Pro will not satisfy you and Hubstaff will.

The monitoring difference, stated plainly

Hubstaff is built around evidence of activity. Every paid plan captures screenshots (limited on Starter, up to three every ten minutes on Team), records an activity level from keyboard and mouse input, logs applications and URLs from Grow, and flags idle time. Administrators can blur screenshots or turn them off per user, and employees can delete a screenshot at the cost of the tracked time, but the product's purpose is to let a manager see what a tracked hour contained. Clockify records that time was tracked against a project and, on Pro, which applications and sites were open while the timer ran if the user has enabled the monitor; it takes no images on any plan.

The practical consequences are legal and cultural as well as technical. Screenshot monitoring requires written notice in New York, Connecticut and Delaware and a disclosure under California's privacy law, needs a policy employees have acknowledged, and in our monitoring statistics correlates with lower trust and higher turnover intent when it is imposed rather than agreed. It is also the only way some clients and some employers will accept remote hours as billable. Hubstaff suits the second case; Clockify suits teams that have decided not to monitor. The line of what never to track applies to Hubstaff users; Clockify users mostly do not need it.

Each tool in detail

Hubstaff

Proof of work plus payroll
Runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, Chrome extension Pricing (November 2026) Starter $4.99, Grow $7.50, Team $10 per seat per month billed annually ($7, $9, $12 monthly); Enterprise $25 annual only; 2-seat minimum; 14-day trial without a card; no free plan

What it is

Hubstaff is a time tracker with monitoring built in. Starter records time, limited screenshots and activity levels; Grow adds projects, budgets, invoicing, approvals, idle timeout, expenses and the integration library; Team adds payroll and payments, scheduling, attendance, time off, overtime rules, unlimited screenshots and GPS job sites that clock mobile workers in and out automatically. Enterprise adds SSO, HIPAA terms and longer retention. Its Trustpilot profile is better than most monitoring vendors and its documentation is honest about what the agent collects.

Where it wins

  • Screenshots, activity levels and idle detection on every paid plan
  • Payroll and contractor payments from tracked hours
  • GPS job sites with automatic clock-in
  • Scheduling, attendance and overtime rules on Team

Where it loses

  • $7.50 for approvals against Clockify's $5.49
  • No kiosk and no reduced seat for hourly staff
  • No free plan and a 2-seat minimum
  • It is monitoring software; notice laws and trust costs apply

Choose it if

You pay contractors from tracked time, clients require proof of hours, or you run field crews who should clock in when they arrive at a site.

Clockify

The most features for the least money, without surveillance
Runs on Web, Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, kiosk, browser extensions Pricing (November 2026) Free for 5 users; Basic $3.99, Standard $5.49, Pro $7.99, Enterprise $11.99 per seat per month billed annually ($4.99, $6.99, $9.99, $14.99 monthly); kiosk-only seats $0.79 to $2.39; no minimum; 7-day Pro trial

What it is

Clockify is a timer and timesheet with modules by tier: billable rates, kiosk, breaks and exports on Basic; approvals, invoicing, time off, attendance, overtime and QuickBooks on Standard; scheduling, forecasting, expenses, labor cost, budgets, GPS and an opt-in activity monitor on Pro; SSO and audit logs on Enterprise. It takes no screenshots on any plan. The Free plan was cut to five users with 31-day reports in April 2026; paid prices did not change. Full tiers are on our Clockify pricing page.

Where it wins

  • Approvals and invoicing at $5.49
  • Kiosk seats at a fifth of the full price for hourly staff
  • Free for five users
  • No screenshots, so no monitoring notice obligations

Where it loses

  • No payroll or contractor payments
  • No geofenced job sites or automatic clock-in
  • Activity monitor is apps and sites only, opt-in
  • Four tiers to navigate; features you need may sit one tier up

Choose it if

You want timesheets, approvals and invoices at the lowest price, you employ hourly staff who can use a kiosk, or you have decided not to monitor.

Moving between them

  1. Hubstaff to Clockify: export Hubstaff's Time and Activity report as CSV per member or per project with the date range you need; invite users to Clockify with matching emails; import through Workspace settings, Import (requires a paid plan or the trial). Screenshots do not transfer and should be deleted under your retention policy.
  2. Clockify to Hubstaff: export the Detailed report as CSV with all columns (a paid plan or trial is needed since Free exports PDF only); Hubstaff has no bulk time-entry importer, so historical time is usually kept in Clockify exports and tracking starts fresh. Recreate projects and rates by hand.
  3. Tell staff in writing before Hubstaff's agent is installed; New York, Connecticut and Delaware require it and every state is better for it. Our monitoring policy template covers the notice.
  4. Reconcile one pay period of totals by person before canceling the old tool. Both bill monthly options, so avoid buying annual seats on the new tool until the first month reconciles.

How this comparison was made

Both products were checked on the criteria used across our tool reviews: features by tier, free-plan limits, published pricing and minimums, platforms, monitoring behavior, integrations and import paths. Hubstaff prices and features were read from hubstaff.com/pricing in October 2026 and rechecked in November; Clockify prices and plan contents from clockify.me/pricing and its help center on November 9, 2026. Team costs are arithmetic from list prices on annual billing. Neither vendor was contacted and WorkIntel Hub has no affiliate relationship with either.

Key takeaways

  • Clockify Standard at $5.49 per seat has approvals, invoicing, attendance and time off; Hubstaff Grow at $7.50 has approvals and invoicing plus screenshots and activity levels; Hubstaff Team at $10 adds payroll, scheduling and GPS job sites.
  • Hubstaff captures screenshots and activity scores on every paid plan; Clockify takes no screenshots on any plan and offers only an opt-in apps-and-sites monitor on Pro.
  • Clockify is free for 5 users and has no minimum; Hubstaff has no free plan and a 2-seat minimum. Clockify's kiosk-only seats cost $0.79 to $2.39; Hubstaff has no kiosk.
  • Fifteen people with approvals and invoicing cost $82.35 a month on Clockify Standard and $112.50 on Hubstaff Grow; with payroll and scheduling, Hubstaff Team is $150 and Clockify Pro (no payroll) is $119.85.
  • Hubstaff's exclusive features are payroll and contractor payments, geofenced job sites with automatic clock-in, and the monitoring itself; Clockify's are the kiosk, cheaper seats and invoicing at a lower tier.
  • Screenshot monitoring requires written notice in New York, Connecticut and Delaware and a policy employees have signed; Clockify users have no such obligation.
  • Choose Clockify for timesheets, approvals and invoices at the lowest price without surveillance; choose Hubstaff when clients require proof of hours, contractors are paid from tracked time, or field crews need job-site clock-ins.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hubstaff or Clockify cheaper?

Clockify, at every comparable tier. Approvals and invoicing cost $5.49 per seat on Clockify Standard and $7.50 on Hubstaff Grow. Clockify is free for five users and sells kiosk-only seats from $0.79; Hubstaff has no free plan, a two-seat minimum and no reduced seats. Hubstaff Team at $10 includes payroll and GPS job sites, which Clockify does not have at any price.

Does Clockify take screenshots like Hubstaff?

No. Clockify takes no screenshots on any plan. Its Pro plan has an optional activity monitor that records applications and websites used while the timer runs, if the user enables it. Hubstaff captures screenshots on every paid plan, limited on Starter and up to three every ten minutes on Team, with a blur option and per-user settings.

Which is better for paying contractors?

Hubstaff. Its Team plan converts tracked hours into payments through PayPal, Wise, Gusto, Deel and other processors, with pay rates per member and automatic or manual payroll runs. Clockify reports billable and cost amounts and exports to QuickBooks on Standard but does not pay anyone.

Which is better for hourly or field staff?

It depends on the setting. Clockify's kiosk with QR codes and photo capture, and kiosk-only seats from $0.79, suit a fixed location such as a restaurant or warehouse. Hubstaff's GPS job sites that clock mobile workers in and out automatically when they arrive suit construction, cleaning and service crews. Clockify's GPS on Pro records location but does not geofence.

Does Hubstaff have a free plan?

Not for new accounts. Hubstaff offers a 14-day trial without a card and then requires a paid plan with a minimum of two seats, from $4.99 per seat on annual billing. Clockify's Free plan covers five users with 31-day reports and PDF export.

Can I import Hubstaff data into Clockify?

Yes, by exporting Hubstaff's Time and Activity report as CSV and using Clockify's import under Workspace settings, which requires a paid Clockify plan or the trial. Project and member names need to match. Screenshots and activity data do not transfer. Going the other way, Hubstaff has no bulk importer for time entries, so history is usually archived rather than moved.

Do I need to tell employees before using Hubstaff?

Yes in New York, Connecticut and Delaware, which require written notice of electronic monitoring, and in practice everywhere, since a signed monitoring policy is what makes screenshot capture defensible. California employees can request the data collected about them. Clockify's timesheet features create no such obligation; its Pro activity monitor is opt-in by the user.

Which has better integrations?

Clockify lists more (80 or more, including QuickBooks on Standard, Jira, Asana, Trello and Google Calendar); Hubstaff has 35 or more from Grow, with the payment processors and Gusto and Deel payroll that Clockify lacks. Both have APIs; Clockify's is limited on the Free plan.
