QuickBooks Time Pricing (2026): Base Fee Plus Per User, Explained

P Pavan
October 28, 2026 · 8 min read

QuickBooks Time costs a base fee plus a per-user fee, billed monthly: Time Premium is $20 a month plus $10 per user and Time Elite is $40 plus $12, after a promotion that halves the base fee for the first three months. Both require a QuickBooks Online subscription, which is a separate cost. The per-user fees rose by $2 on July 1, 2026, and many competitor articles still quote the old $8 and $10. The structure makes it expensive for a crew of two and reasonable for a crew of fifty, and geofencing, the feature field employers buy it for, is Elite only.

This page sets out the October 2026 prices and the promotion rules, what Premium and Elite include, the Workforce payroll bundles that include time tracking, the billing rules, what teams of 1 to 100 actually pay, the price history back to TSheets, what customers complain about, and the per-user tools that cost less for small teams.

QuickBooks Time is billed monthly only; no annual option appeared on the pricing page. The 50% promotion applies to the base fee for three months on new subscriptions and does not reduce the per-user fee; Intuit says it may end the offer at any time. The 30-day free trial covers the base fee only: per-user fees, state tax filing fees and add-ons are charged from the start. No credit card, contract or signature is required to start the trial. The per-user fees rose by $2 on July 1, 2026, from $8 and $10 to $10 and $12, and Intuit's own product page still showed a placeholder per-worker fee when we checked, so use the pricing page, not the product page or a third-party article, as the source. Geofencing is the feature that moves most field crews to Elite; the pricing page lists it under Workforce Elite and the earlier Time Elite feature lists, and did not list it under Time Premium. Project tracking, mileage and signatures are Elite only. Desktop monitoring, screenshots and activity tracking do not exist on any plan; QuickBooks Time is a clock and scheduler, not monitoring software.

The payroll bundles

Intuit sells time tracking two ways: as QuickBooks Time on top of a QuickBooks Online subscription, or inside QuickBooks Workforce, its payroll product. Workforce Premium at $13 per employee per month includes time tracking tools, time off and payroll with tax filing; Workforce Elite at $134 base plus $17 per employee adds geofencing, mileage, project tools, tax penalty protection, a personal HR advisor, performance management and unlimited HR workflows. The bundle is the cheaper route if you need payroll anyway: a ten-person team on Workforce Premium pays $130 a month for payroll and time together, against $120 for Time Premium alone plus a separate payroll product. The names changed in 2025 and 2026 from QuickBooks Online Payroll Core, Premium and Elite to Workforce tiers; third-party pages still describe the older bundles and prices. The standalone QuickBooks Online subscription that Time Premium and Elite require is a further cost: Simple Start, Essentials, Plus or Advanced, each with its own base fee.

Billing rules that change the price

  • Base fee plus per user means the per-head cost falls with size: Time Premium is $30 a user for one person, $12 for ten, $10.20 for 100.
  • Monthly only. There is no annual discount, which simplifies leaving but removes the 16 to 20% saving most competitors offer.
  • The trial is of the base fee; users added during the trial are billed per user from day one.
  • The promotion is three months and applies to the base fee only; the fourth invoice is the real price.
  • Requires QuickBooks Online. A business on QuickBooks Desktop, Xero or another ledger cannot buy Time Premium or Elite standalone.
  • Proration, refunds and cancellation terms are not on the pricing page; they follow Intuit's general subscription terms, which allow cancellation at any time with service to the end of the billed month and no refund of the current month.

Price history

TSheets, which Intuit bought in January 2018, charged a $20 base plus $8 per user for Premium and $40 plus $10 for Elite for most of the period from 2020 to mid-2026, with the base fee discounted 50% for three months as a standing promotion. On July 1, 2026 the per-user fees rose by $2 to $10 and $12; the base fees did not change. The payroll bundles were renamed from QuickBooks Online Payroll to QuickBooks Workforce in 2025 and 2026 with new base and per-employee prices. Several competitor articles in October 2026 still quote $8 and $10.

  • QuickBooks Time in October 2026: Time Premium $20 base plus $10 per user per month; Time Elite $40 base plus $12 per user; monthly billing only; base fee halved for the first three months of a new subscription; one admin included; requires QuickBooks Online.
  • Per-user fees rose $2 on July 1, 2026 (from $8 and $10); the base fees did not change.
  • Premium includes GPS at punch, scheduling, approvals, time off, alerts, kiosk with facial recognition and payroll sync; Elite adds geofencing, mileage, project tracking with estimates and timesheet signatures.

  • Ten people cost $120 a month on Premium ($110 during the promotion) and $160 on Elite; one person costs $30 and $52.

Frequently asked questions

How much does QuickBooks Time cost per month?

Time Premium is $20 a month plus $10 per user, and Time Elite is $40 plus $12 per user, billed monthly, with the base fee halved for the first three months of a new subscription. Both require a QuickBooks Online subscription. Prices were read on October 28, 2026.

Did QuickBooks Time raise its prices in 2026?

Yes. On July 1, 2026 the per-user fees rose by $2, from $8 to $10 on Premium and from $10 to $12 on Elite. The base fees of $20 and $40 did not change. Many third-party articles still quote the pre-July rates.

Is there a free version of QuickBooks Time?

No. There is a 30-day trial that waives the base fee only; per-user fees, state tax filing fees and add-ons are charged from the start. No card is required to begin the trial.

Does QuickBooks Time charge annually?

No annual option appeared on the pricing page in October 2026; billing is monthly. That means no annual discount but also no annual commitment.

What is the difference between QuickBooks Time Premium and Elite?

Both include mobile time tracking with GPS, timesheets and approvals, scheduling, time off, alerts, a kiosk with optional facial recognition, photo attachments and payroll sync. Elite adds geofencing, mileage tracking, project tracking with estimates against actuals and an activity feed, and timesheet signatures, for $20 more base and $2 more per user.

Do I need QuickBooks Online to use QuickBooks Time?

Yes for Time Premium and Time Elite; Intuit states the integration requires an active QuickBooks Online, Payroll or Desktop account. Time tracking is also included in the QuickBooks Workforce payroll bundles.

How much is QuickBooks Time for 10 employees?

$120 a month on Time Premium ($20 base plus 10 times $10), or $110 during the three-month promotion, and $160 on Time Elite ($140 promotional), plus the QuickBooks Online subscription. The Workforce Premium bundle with payroll is $13 per employee plus a base fee not shown on the page.

What is cheaper than QuickBooks Time for a small crew?

Jibble is free for unlimited users with GPS, geofences and a kiosk and exports to QuickBooks; Connecteam is free for ten users; When I Work starts at $2.50 per user; Clockify at $3.99; Hubstaff Team at $10 includes GPS job sites and QuickBooks sync. For a two-person crew any of these costs less than the $40 QuickBooks Time charges.
