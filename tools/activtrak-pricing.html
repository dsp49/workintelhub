ActivTrak Pricing (2026): New Plan Names, Add-ons, Real Cost

P Pavan
November 9, 2026 · 10 min read

ActivTrak costs $10, $15 or $17 per monitored worker per month on annual billing for its three main plans, which since July 20, 2026 are called Work Activity Tracking, Workforce Management and Productivity Optimization (formerly Essentials, Essentials Plus and Professional), with a $21 Workflow plan sold through sales, a bundle of the two upper tiers at a 30 percent discount, and a Free plan for three people. There is no monthly billing. Screenshots are not in any plan; they are an add-on on the two lower tiers only, triggered by alarms, and longer data retention costs $1 per worker per month per year.

This page sets out the November 2026 prices under the new names, every add-on and what it costs, a feature table across the tiers, the billing rules (annual only, per-worker licensing, mandatory paid support above 50 licenses), what teams of 3, 25, 100 and 250 pay, the price history from 2024 through the July 2026 reshuffle, the standard complaints and when a competitor fits better. If you are reading a review that mentions Essentials or Professional, it predates July 2026.

Two things about the add-ons matter for budgeting. The split between Workforce Management and Productivity Optimization is unusual: the higher-priced plan drops alarms and screenshots. ActivTrak is steering compliance-minded buyers (alarms, screenshots, website blocking) to the $10 and $15 tiers and coaching-minded buyers (habits, workload, AI advisor) to the $17 tier, with the bundle for organizations that want both. A team that wants alarms and the Work Advisor needs the bundle at about $22.40.

Billing rules that change the price

  • Annual only. There is no monthly option on any paid plan; the annual fee is paid upfront. Multi-year contracts attract a discount through sales.
  • Per worker. You license the number of people monitored, not administrators. Exceeding the licensed count triggers a notification and a 14-day grace period; after that, reports show no data until the count is back within the license or more seats are bought.
  • Trial. Fourteen days of the Workforce Management plus Productivity Optimization bundle, with Screen Details for selected use cases, then automatic downgrade to Free for three workers. A Free account that is not logged into for 30 days is marked inactive and deleted 30 days later.
  • Support is mandatory at scale. Accounts with more than 50 licenses must buy Premier or Signature support, which is quoted rather than listed. Budget for it in any deployment above 50 people.
  • Retention is a budget line. Add $1 per worker per month per extra year up to three years total.
  • Discounts. No nonprofit or education pricing is published. Volume discounts for larger deployments are by quote.
  • Platforms. The agent runs on Windows, macOS and ChromeOS (as a Chromebook agent or Chrome extension); there is no Linux agent and no mobile agent, so Linux developers and field staff on phones are outside its reach.

For comparison at 25 people: Teramind Starter is $350 a month on annual billing with screen recording included; Insightful is about $200 to $300 depending on tier; Hubstaff Team is $250 with screenshots and payroll; Time Doctor Standard is $292.50. ActivTrak is mid-priced for the category, cheaper than Teramind and dearer than the time-tracker-based monitors, and its distinguishing cost is the add-on structure rather than the headline.

Price history

ActivTrak sold a free plan and tiers named Advanced and Premium before 2024. In the first quarter of 2024 it stopped supporting the Free plan as a product (it survives as a three-worker tier), introduced the Essentials and Professional names and a Professional bundle at $19, and in the second quarter raised Professional from $17 to $19. Essentials Plus arrived at $15 in the third quarter of 2024. Through 2025 features moved down the tiers without price changes. On July 20, 2026 the plans were renamed Work Activity Tracking, Workforce Management and Productivity Optimization, the top plan was cut back to $17, the $4 AI Insights add-on and the 30 percent bundle discount appeared, and conversational AI was added to the two upper tiers; existing accounts were mapped across from August 2026 (Advanced and Essentials to Work Activity Tracking, Essentials Plus to Workforce Management, Premium and Professional to the bundle). The net movement over three years is a flat $10 entry price, a top tier that went $17 to $19 and back to $17, and more of the value moved into add-ons.

What buyers complain about

Annual only

A 25-person company that wants to try monitoring for a quarter cannot; the smallest commitment is $3,000 for a year on the entry plan, after a 14-day trial.

Fixed by: running the trial as a real pilot with a written policy in place, and using Free for three workers if the question is whether the data is useful at all

Screenshots cost extra and are not on the plan you expected

Buyers assume the $17 plan includes everything the $10 plan does. It does not include alarms or the Screen Details add-on.

Fixed by: choosing Work Activity Tracking or Workforce Management if alarms and alarm-triggered screenshots matter, or the bundle if you also want the coaching features

Short default retention

Six months on the two lower plans is less than most HR and legal teams want for an investigation record.

Fixed by: budgeting $1 per worker per month per extra year, or exporting monthly through ActivConnect

Mandatory paid support above 50 licenses

The support fee is quoted, not listed, and appears late in procurement.

Fixed by: asking for the support quote with the license quote

Plan renames

Three sets of names in three years make old reviews, internal documents and this year's budget disagree with the invoice.

Fixed by: mapping Essentials to Work Activity Tracking, Essentials Plus to Workforce Management and Professional to Productivity Optimization when reading anything written before July 2026

If ActivTrak is not the right price

If you need screen recording, keystrokes or data loss prevention, you have outgrown ActivTrak and Teramind is the usual next step; ActivTrak vs Teramind compares them and Teramind pricing has its tiers. If you want the analytics with monthly billing, Insightful bills monthly from about $8 and keeps 24 months of data. If you want time tracking with activity data at a lower price, Hubstaff Grow at $7.50 or DeskTime Pro at about $6.42 include it. If you want no images at all with long retention, WorkTime keeps data indefinitely. The full list is in ActivTrak alternatives; the category comparison is best productivity monitoring software.

Frequently asked questions

How much does ActivTrak cost?

$10 per worker per month for Work Activity Tracking, $15 for Workforce Management, $17 for Productivity Optimization and $21 for Workflow, all billed annually; a bundle of Workforce Management and Productivity Optimization is about $22.40 after a 30 percent discount. A Free plan covers three workers with 30 days of history. Add-ons include AI Insights at $4, extra data history at $1 per year, and Screen Details, integrations and premium support by quote.

Does ActivTrak have monthly billing?

No. ActivTrak's pricing page states that it currently offers only annual plans, paid upfront. The 14-day trial and the three-worker Free plan are the only ways to use it without an annual commitment.

What happened to ActivTrak Essentials and Professional?

They were renamed on July 20, 2026. Essentials became Work Activity Tracking ($10), Essentials Plus became Workforce Management ($15) and Professional became Productivity Optimization, with its price cut from $19 to $17. Existing accounts were migrated from August 2026; legacy Advanced accounts map to Work Activity Tracking and legacy Premium accounts to the Workforce Management plus Productivity Optimization bundle.

Does ActivTrak take screenshots?

Not by default and not on any plan as standard. Screen Details is a paid add-on available only on Work Activity Tracking and Workforce Management, priced by quote, which captures screenshots and screen views under configured alarm conditions along with title bars and full URLs. ActivTrak states that it does no keystroke logging, video or camera monitoring.

Is ActivTrak free?

There is a Free plan for up to three workers with basic activity visibility, 30 days of data history and no exports, alarms or screenshots. Accounts not logged into for 30 days are marked inactive and deleted 30 days later. Beyond three people the entry plan is $10 per worker per month on annual billing.

How long does ActivTrak keep data?

Thirty days on Free, six months on Work Activity Tracking and Workforce Management, and one year on Productivity Optimization. Retention can be extended in one-year increments to three years total at $1 per worker per month for each additional year.

Is there a minimum number of users for ActivTrak?

None is published for the paid plans as of November 2026, despite the five-seat minimum cited in older reviews. The stated limits are that Free is capped at three workers and that accounts with more than 50 licenses must purchase a Premier or Signature support plan.

Is ActivTrak cheaper than Teramind?

Yes at the entry level: ActivTrak starts at $10 per worker against Teramind Starter at $14 on annual billing with a five-seat minimum. The products differ in kind: Teramind records screens continuously and offers keystroke logging and data loss prevention on higher tiers, while ActivTrak captures screenshots only through an alarm-triggered add-on and does no keystroke logging. Teramind also offers monthly billing, which ActivTrak does not.
