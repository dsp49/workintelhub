Time Doctor Pricing (2026): Plans, $200 Integrations and the Real Cost

P Pavan
October 28, 2026 · 9 min read

Time Doctor costs $6.70, $11.70 or $16.70 per user per month on annual billing for Basic, Standard and Premium, or $8, $14 and $20 month to month, with Enterprise by quote. Two facts matter more than the headline. Screenshots are on every plan and cannot be turned off, so there is no Time Doctor plan for a team that wants timesheets without surveillance. And the features a managed team needs, attendance, approvals, payroll and app usage, start at Standard, so $11.70 is the realistic entry price, before add-ons that include $200 a month for each HRIS or payroll integration.

This page sets out the October 2026 prices and the two-months-free annual discount, what each plan includes feature by feature, every add-on, the billing and cancellation rules, what teams of 5, 25 and 100 actually pay, the price history, the billing complaints that dominate Time Doctor's reviews with dated quotes, and the tools that cost less or more at the same level.

The $200-per-integration charges are the ones that surprise buyers: a Standard-plan team of 20 that connects Time Doctor to its HRIS and its payroll system pays $234 for seats and $400 for the two integrations. At that point Premium, which includes the API and SSO, or a competitor with free integrations, is cheaper.

Billing rules that change the price

  • Cards accepted: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover; invoicing for larger annual contracts.
  • Trial to paid: the Premium-feature trial converts to whichever plan you choose; billing starts at the end of the 14 days.
  • Cancellation is by the account owner in Settings, then Billing, then Cancel account, with a reason dialog. Email requests are not the published path, which is where many of the complaints below begin.
  • Refunds are governed by a separate refund policy page linked from the pricing FAQ; the reviews indicate annual plans are rarely refunded.
  • Proration and inactive users: not stated on the pricing page. Disable users you are not tracking; a disabled user should not consume a seat, but confirm on your invoice.
  • Data history is a plan feature: three months on Basic, six on Standard, two years on Premium. Downgrading loses access to the older data.

Price history

Time Doctor's Basic, Standard and Premium tiers were priced at $7, $10 and $20 per user monthly through 2022 and 2023 with annual discounts. The current monthly rates of $8, $14 and $20 and annual rates of $6.70, $11.70 and $16.70 were in place by 2025, which represents an increase of about $1 on Basic and $4 on Standard month to month. The add-on model, including the $200 integration charges, appeared in 2025. In 2026 the company added the Benchmarks AI report and an MCP connector to Premium without a list price change. Wayback Machine snapshots could not be fetched on October 28, 2026 to pin the dates.

What customers complain about

Time Doctor's Trustpilot profile is among the worst in the category: 1.2 of 5 across 235 reviews in October 2026, and almost every one-star review is about billing. Five from the past eighteen months:

Charges after cancellation

The dominant theme. The published path is Settings, Billing, Cancel account by the owner; cancellations attempted by email or by a non-owner appear not to take effect.

I've asked to cancel my membership multiple times and am still being charged.Trustpilot, one star, December 16, 2025

Fixed by: cancel in the account as the owner, screenshot the confirmation, and watch the card for two cycles

Cannot close the account or delete data

A September 2026 reviewer could not close the account, delete records or stop charges.

it is impossible to close the account, delete records and stop the chargesTrustpilot, one star, September 7, 2026

Reviewers describe being moved to or charged for annual billing and refused a return to monthly.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Time Doctor cost per user?

$6.70 (Basic), $11.70 (Standard) or $16.70 (Premium) per user per month on annual billing, or $8, $14 and $20 month to month, with Enterprise by quote. Annual billing gives two months free, a 16.6% discount. Prices were checked in October 2026.

Does Time Doctor have a free plan?

No. It offers a 14-day free trial of the Premium features without a credit card. There is no permanent free tier.

Which Time Doctor plan includes payroll and attendance?

Standard, at $11.70 per user on annual billing. Basic covers time tracking, screenshots, projects and the timeline report only. Standard adds schedules, attendance, approvals, app and web usage, productivity ratings, leave and break tracking, payroll and 60 or more integrations.

Can I turn off screenshots in Time Doctor?

No plan removes them. Screenshots are a feature of Basic, Standard and Premium alike; administrators can adjust frequency and blur them but not remove the capability. Teams that want timesheets without screenshots need a different tool.

What are Time Doctor's add-ons?

On Basic and Standard: Software Cost Insights at $3 per user per month, HRIS native integration at $200 per integration per month, payroll native integration at $200 per integration per month, and per-user add-ons for Benchmarks AI, unusual activity reporting, meeting insights, Office vs Remote and video screen recording, plus per-account add-ons for SSO, automated provisioning and BigQuery access. Premium includes most of these.

How do I cancel Time Doctor?

The account owner opens Settings, then Billing, and clicks Cancel account in the billing details; a dialog asks for a reason and confirming cancels. Keep a screenshot. Email requests are not the published path, and charges after attempted cancellation are the most common complaint in Time Doctor's reviews.

Does Time Doctor refund annual plans?

Its pricing FAQ links to a separate refund policy rather than stating terms, and multiple reviewers report annual charges refused for refund. Choose monthly billing until you are certain, and check the first invoice for the billing cycle.

Is Time Doctor cheaper than Hubstaff?

No at the realistic plans. Time Doctor Standard is $11.70 per user against Hubstaff Team at $10 on annual billing, and Hubstaff includes payroll integrations that Time Doctor sells at $200 a month each. Time Doctor Basic ($6.70) is dearer than Hubstaff Starter ($4.99) but includes screenshots on every plan where Hubstaff's are limited on Starter.
