Teramind Alternatives (2026): 12 Tools From DLP to Light Monitoring

P Pavan
October 28, 2026 · 16 min read

Teramind is two products in one agent: a forensic platform that records screens, keystrokes, email and file movement and blocks data loss in real time, and an employee monitoring dashboard that reports productivity. People leave for reasons that point in opposite directions. Security teams leave for a vendor with better support or broader DLP coverage; everyone else leaves because they bought the forensic platform to see who was working, found the agent heavy and the support slow, and never configured a DLP rule.

This guide sorts the alternatives by that split, with dated quotes for the reasons people give, October 2026 pricing where vendors publish it and a clear mark where they do not. Veriato, Syteca, DTEX, Forcepoint and Microsoft Purview replace the security platform; Controlio, CurrentWare and Kickidler replace the recording and blocking for a fraction of the price; ActivTrak, Insightful and Hubstaff replace the dashboard with far less capture and far less legal exposure.

The short answer

If you need insider risk and data loss prevention at Teramind's depth, the like-for-like choices are Veriato ($18, five-seat minimum), Syteca, DTEX and Forcepoint by quote, and Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management if you already own Microsoft 365 E5.

If you bought Teramind for monitoring and never used the DLP rules, Controlio ($6.67), CurrentWare ($12 to $20) and Kickidler record screens and block sites for a third of the price, and ActivTrak ($10) and Insightful ($8) give the analytics without recording.

If the agent's weight and the support queue are the problem, which is what Teramind's public complaints are about, Insightful, ActivTrak and Hubstaff run lighter agents with US support hours.

What Teramind costs and does in October 2026

Teramind

The incumbent
Runs on Windows, macOS; cloud or self-hosted; virtual desktop and terminal server support Pricing (October 2026) Starter from about $14 per seat per month on annual billing (about $15 monthly), UAM and DLP tiers higher, Enterprise and Government by quote; the seat selector starts at 5; annual billing saves about 8%; free trial of unspecified length; on October 28, 2026 the pricing page rendered its dollar figures as placeholders to automated requests, so confirm every number on the live page Monitoring Full monitoring

What it is

Teramind is an insider threat and data loss prevention platform that also sells as employee monitoring. Every plan records the screen with live view and playback on policy breaches, tracks applications, websites, files, email and printing, and reports productivity. UAM adds keystroke logging, recording of AI chat conversations and SIEM integration; DLP adds content rules, 200 prepackaged policies, automatic blocking and OCR; Enterprise adds AWS GovCloud and Azure Government hosting and an SLA. Keystroke logging is a feature administrators can turn off, which the vendor describes as invasive. A 2026 release added native monitoring of Windows desktop apps and a Mac agent update, and the company's marketing now centers on AI governance.

Better than Teramind

  • The deepest capture of any mainstream tool: screen video, keystrokes, email content, file movement and clipboard
  • Rules that block actions in real time rather than report them afterward
  • Self-hosted and government cloud options with an SLA

Where Teramind is still better

  • Its Trustpilot score is 2.7 of 5 across 7 reviews, with support, agent performance and billing the complaints; the sample is tiny
  • The 5-seat minimum and tier structure make it expensive for a small team that wants monitoring alone
  • Dollar prices are not reliably published, and the capture depth creates legal exposure in notice and consent states

Who should switch to it

Security and compliance teams with a genuine insider risk or data exfiltration problem, a legal basis for keystroke and content capture, and the staff to tune the rules. Our employee monitoring software review places it among its peers.

Why people leave Teramind

Teramind's public review footprint is small and the vendor does not reply to its negative Trustpilot reviews. The complaints below are the ones with evidence, each with a fix.

Support that does not answer

Two 2026 reviewers describe support that could not get the silent installer working and a chatbot that could not add an employee; a 2025 reviewer describes emails unanswered while the subscription kept billing.

Its all AI support and I cannot even add a new employee.Trustpilot, one star, July 31, 2026

Fixed by: vendors with staffed US support: ActivTrak, Insightful, Veriato, CurrentWare.

The agent slows machines

Continuous screen recording, OCR and content inspection cost CPU and disk, and a March 2026 reviewer blamed the agent for slowing employees' computers.

The software caused ongoing issues, significantly slowing down our employees' computersTrustpilot, one star, March 30, 2026

Fixed by: lighter agents: ActivTrak (no recording), Insightful (screenshots on demand), Hubstaff, Controlio (recording with a smaller footprint).

Recordings that do not play

Reviewers report recordings captured but unplayable, and the product stopping silently. For a tool bought as evidence, an unplayable recording is a failed audit.

recordings weren't working anymore as in sometimes were taken but you couldn't play themTrustpilot, one star, July 19, 2025

Fixed by: Veriato and Kickidler for screen video with local playback; CurrentWare for screenshots stored on your own server.

Billing while broken

The same reviewers describe continued billing during unresolved support cases and refused refunds.

we continue to be billed for a product we are not using while waiting for a resolution.Trustpilot, one star, March 30, 2026

Fixed by: month-to-month billing or published refund terms: Hubstaff (30-day money-back), Controlio and CurrentWare (annual, but with trials of 14 days on real machines).

More capture than the law or the team can carry

Keystroke logging and email content capture are lawful on company systems under a disclosed policy, but they record personal passwords and privileged communications, and California bars emotion-inferring surveillance from January 1, 2027. Many Teramind buyers wanted productivity reports and got a forensic platform.

Fixed by: tools that record less by design: ActivTrak (no keystrokes, no recording), Insightful, Hubstaff; see our keylogger law guide.

Price for a small team

The 5-seat starting point and tiered pricing mean a ten-person team that wants screen recording pays enterprise rates for features it will not configure.

Fixed by: Controlio Basic $6.67, Kickidler from about $2.75, CurrentWare BrowseReporter $12, Time Champ $3.90 with screen recording and DLP as add-ons.

The alternatives in detail

Veriato

Insider risk with recording, on your own servers if you want
Runs on Windows, macOS, Android; cloud or on-premises Pricing (October 2026) User Activity Monitoring $18 per user per month billed annually with a 5-user minimum; Insider Risk Management by quote with a 20-user minimum

What it is

Veriato records screens, keystrokes, email, file movement and web activity and scores insider risk from behavior, with on-premises deployment for regulated buyers. It is the closest like-for-like replacement for Teramind's UAM tier.

Better than Teramind

  • Risk scoring across users rather than rule-by-rule alerts
  • On-premises deployment from five seats
  • An established vendor with staffed support

Where Teramind is still better

  • No real-time DLP blocking at Teramind's level
  • $18 per user is above Teramind's entry price
  • Same legal exposure from keystroke capture

Who should switch to it

Security teams replacing Teramind UAM that want the same evidence with a different vendor.

Switching

No migration of recordings; archive Teramind exports and run both through one investigation cycle.

How to leave Teramind cleanly

  1. Export what the record retention policy requires: investigation recordings, alert logs and reports, and store them under the same access controls, because they contain keystrokes and content.
  2. Confirm the cancellation in writing and check for continued billing; the public complaints describe charges during unresolved support cases.
  3. Remove the agent from every endpoint with the silent uninstaller, including machines of departed staff, and verify from the console that no agents report.
  4. Reissue the monitoring notice. Dropping keystroke and content capture is a change employees should be told about, and Connecticut, Delaware and New York require written notice of what the replacement records; our monitoring policy template has the wording.
  5. Delete the data you no longer have a basis to keep. Keystroke logs that include personal passwords are a liability with no purpose once the tool is gone.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to Teramind?

For insider risk and DLP, Veriato ($18 per user, five-user minimum) is the closest replacement with on-premises deployment, and Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management is the best answer for Microsoft 365 E5 tenants. For teams that only used Teramind's monitoring, Controlio ($6.67) covers recording and blocking and ActivTrak ($10, free for three) covers productivity analytics without recording anything.

How much does Teramind cost?

Teramind's Starter tier was about $14 per seat per month on annual billing in October 2026, with UAM and DLP tiers higher and Enterprise by quote; the seat selector starts at five and annual billing saves about 8%. On October 28, 2026 the pricing page did not render its dollar figures to automated requests, so confirm on the live page.

Is there a free alternative to Teramind?

ActivTrak is free for three users with 30 days of history and no recording. Hubstaff and Controlio offer 14-day trials and Insightful a 7-day trial. No free product offers Teramind's recording, keystroke or DLP features.

Which Teramind alternatives record screens?

Veriato, Controlio and Kickidler record screen video with playback; Syteca records sessions; Forcepoint offers video replay for investigations; Insightful and Time Champ sell recording as add-ons; CurrentWare takes screenshots. ActivTrak, DTEX, Hubstaff and Microsoft Purview do not record screens.

Which alternatives log keystrokes?

Veriato, Controlio and Kickidler include keystroke logging; Time Champ sells it as an add-on. ActivTrak, Insightful, Hubstaff, DTEX and Microsoft Purview do not. Keystroke capture on company devices is lawful under a disclosed policy, but using captured personal passwords is not, and the logs are a liability to store.

Can Teramind be self-hosted, and which alternatives can?

Yes; Teramind offers cloud and self-hosted deployment and government cloud hosting on Enterprise. Veriato, Controlio (from 10 users), CurrentWare, Kickidler, Syteca and Forcepoint offer on-premises deployment; Insightful from 250 seats. ActivTrak, Hubstaff, DTEX and Purview are cloud only.

Does Microsoft 365 include an insider risk tool?

Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management is included with Microsoft 365 E5 and available through the E5 Compliance and E5 Insider Risk Management add-ons, with some indicators billed pay-as-you-go. It correlates Microsoft 365, Defender and HR signals into risk policies and pairs with Purview DLP; it does not record screens.

What should I do with Teramind data when I leave?

Export the recordings and logs your retention policy requires and store them under the same access controls, delete the rest, especially keystroke logs that may contain personal passwords, uninstall the agent from every endpoint and verify in the console, and issue a new monitoring notice describing what the replacement records, which Connecticut, Delaware and New York require in writing.
