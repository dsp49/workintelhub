What Can Insightful See? Apps, Screenshots and Stealth Mode Explained

P Pavan
November 26, 2026 · 6 min read

Insightful, the monitoring tool formerly called Workpuls, sees what happens on your work computer through an agent your employer installs on Windows, macOS or Linux. Depending on what your employer has switched on and paid for, it records the applications and websites you use, how active or idle you are, your clock-in times and breaks, and screenshots of your screen. With paid add-ons it can take screenshots on demand, take up to 120 an hour, or record your screen as video. It does not log keystrokes in any mode, and it has no mobile app, so it does not track your phone or your GPS location.

The part most people ask about is stealth mode. Insightful can run hidden on company-owned devices, in which case you may not see that it is there at all. This page explains each item, how visible and stealth mode differ, what the law says about being told, and how to find out what your own setup records.

Apps, websites and activity

The core of Insightful is a record of how your computer time is spent: which applications are in use, which websites you visit, and when you are active or idle. Your employer classifies applications as productive or not by team, so the same website can count differently for a marketer and an accountant. From that the reports work out utilization and capacity for your team.

An app or website name shows where you were, not what you did there. A long stretch in a browser tab is recorded as time on that site; the words you wrote in it are not, because Insightful does not log keystrokes. Like every activity-based tool, the score falls during calls, reading and meetings, and reviewers find its automatic time mapping less reliable than a manual log. If you spend your day on calls, ask how your role is classified.

Screenshots and screen recording

Screenshots are optional. If your employer turns them on, Insightful captures images of your screen automatically. Two paid add-ons go further: on-demand screenshots, taken when a manager asks, and increased-frequency screenshots of up to 120 an hour, which is one every 30 seconds. A third add-on, screen recording, captures video rather than stills.

Anything on your screen at the moment of capture is in the image or video: a chat window, a document, a banking page. Because each add-on costs your employer money per seat, it is fair to ask which ones are active on your account; the answer is a line on an invoice, not a guess. Our guide to computer screen monitoring covers how screenshot archives are kept and who can see them.

Visible mode and stealth mode

Insightful runs in one of two modes. In visible mode, the default on personal devices, you have your own dashboard and can check what the agent records about you. In stealth mode, available on company-owned devices, the agent runs without that dashboard and without automatic time mapping, so you may have no on-screen sign that it is there.

Our research for the Insightful alternatives guide took this description of stealth mode from a competitor's review rather than Insightful's own pages, so check Insightful's current documentation for the exact behavior. Hidden or not, monitoring agents usually leave ordinary traces such as an entry in the process list or the installed programs; our guide on how to detect employee monitoring walks through where to look.

Does your employer have to tell you?

Monitoring a company-owned computer is broadly permitted in the US. What several states require is notice. Connecticut, Delaware and New York have dedicated electronic monitoring notice laws: New York requires prior written notice on hiring, acknowledged by the employee, and Delaware requires either a daily electronic notice or a one-time notice the employee acknowledges. Maine requires written notice to affected employees at least once a year. Running the agent in stealth mode does not remove those duties: the software can be hidden, the policy cannot.

Outside those states, telling employees is still the norm for any employer that wants the data to be trusted. Our monitoring laws by state guide sets out each rule, and the Connecticut electronic monitoring law page covers the strictest of them in detail.

How long the record is kept

Insightful's pricing page shows 24 months of data retention on every plan. That is long for the category: two years of app use, activity and, where enabled, screenshots. Employers on Enterprise can also host Insightful on their own servers from 250 seats, and a data warehouse add-on lets them copy the data into their own systems, where their own retention rules apply. If retention matters to you, ask how long your employer keeps the data and who can open it, which matters more than how often it is collected.

How to check what your account records

  1. Look for your dashboard. If you have an Insightful employee dashboard, you are in visible mode and can see what is recorded. If you have none, ask whether the agent runs in stealth mode.
  2. Read the monitoring policy. In Connecticut, Delaware and New York your employer must give you notice of monitoring. Ask HR for the policy if you have not seen one.
  3. Ask which features and add-ons are on. Screenshots, on-demand screenshots, higher-frequency screenshots and screen recording are each a setting, and the last three are paid extras.
  4. Ask how your role is classified. Which apps and sites count as productive for your team decides how your activity looks in reports.
  5. Keep personal life off the work device. Anything on screen can be captured, and a personal phone on your own network is outside Insightful's reach.

For employers: what to switch on

Every item above is a choice, and the add-ons are a choice you pay for. Turn on what answers a question you have written down, run the agent visibly so people can check their own record, and put the list in writing before installation; the monitoring policy template covers the wording. The costs of each add-on are set out in our Insightful pricing guide and the trade-offs in our Insightful review. If stealth mode or screenshots are why your team distrusts the tool, the visible, image-free alternatives to Insightful start with ActivTrak and WorkTime, and Insightful vs Hubstaff shows how a visible time tracker compares.

Key takeaways

  • Insightful records the apps and websites you use, active and idle time, and attendance on Windows, macOS and Linux work computers.
  • Screenshots are optional; on-demand screenshots, up to 120 an hour, and screen recording are paid add-ons your employer must switch on.
  • Insightful does not log keystrokes in any mode and has no mobile app, so it does not track your phone or GPS location.
  • Stealth mode hides the agent on company-owned devices and removes your dashboard; personal devices default to visible mode.
  • Connecticut, Delaware and New York require employers to give notice of electronic monitoring, and stealth mode does not change that.
  • Every plan keeps 24 months of data, so ask who can see it and how long your employer keeps it.

Frequently asked questions

Can Insightful see my screen?

Only if your employer has turned screenshots on, or bought the screen recording add-on. On-demand screenshots and increased-frequency screenshots of up to 120 an hour are further paid add-ons.

Does Insightful record keystrokes?

No. Insightful does not log keystrokes in any mode, so it does not record what you type. It records which applications and websites you use and whether you are active or idle.

Can Insightful run without me knowing?

Yes, on company-owned devices in stealth mode, which removes the employee dashboard. In Connecticut, Delaware and New York your employer must still give you notice of monitoring.

Does Insightful track my location?

Not by GPS. Insightful has no mobile app. On its Combo plan it reports whether a day was worked in the office, remotely or hybrid.

Can Insightful see my messages?

Not as text. It does not read chat or email content, but a screenshot or recording taken while a message is on screen will show it.

How long does Insightful keep my data?

Insightful's pricing page shows 24 months of data retention on every plan. Your employer may also copy data into its own systems through the data warehouse add-on.
