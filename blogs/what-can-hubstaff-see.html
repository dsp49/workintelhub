What Can Hubstaff See? Screenshots, Activity and GPS Explained

P Pavan
November 26, 2026 · 5 min read

Hubstaff sees what happens on your device while its timer is running, and only what your employer has switched on. That can include screenshots of your screen (one to three every ten minutes), an activity percentage based on keyboard and mouse input, the apps and websites you use, and periods of idle time. On the mobile app it can record your GPS location while you are clocked in. It does not record what you type, read your messages, or switch on your camera.

The rest of this page explains each item, what your manager actually sees on the dashboard, what Hubstaff cannot see, and how to find out what your own account records. If you are an employer deciding what to switch on, the same detail tells you what your team will be living with.

The timer is the boundary

Hubstaff is a time tracker first. Everything it records is tied to the timer: you start it against a project or your employer's settings start it for you, and recording stops when the timer stops. When you are off the clock, the desktop app is not taking screenshots or counting input, and the phone app is not recording location.

That boundary is also the main risk. If you forget to stop the timer before checking personal email or banking on a work laptop, those screens can be captured. The simplest protection is habit: stop the timer before anything personal, and keep personal browsing off work devices. Our guide to detecting employee monitoring covers how to tell what else may be installed on a work device.

Screenshots

When screenshots are on, Hubstaff captures your screen at random moments up to three times in each ten-minute block. Your employer chooses the frequency and can blur the images so that the general activity is visible but text is not. Screenshots can also be disabled for whole projects, which Hubstaff recommends for calls and meetings.

What a manager sees is a grid of thumbnails beside each time block, with the activity percentage for that block. Anything on your screen at the moment of capture is in the image: a chat window, a document, a browser tab. Text is readable unless blurring is on.

The activity percentage

Hubstaff's activity level is the percentage of seconds in a tracked period in which you pressed a key or moved the mouse. It does not record which keys, and it does not judge whether the work was useful. Ten minutes of typing scores high; ten minutes on a phone call, reading a contract or thinking scores near zero.

Hubstaff's own support guidance says the number is a poor measure for people on calls and suggests disabling activity recording for those projects. If your manager reads a low percentage as low effort, that guidance is worth pointing to. Some people respond to a low score with a mouse jiggler; that tends to end in a dishonesty finding, which is far worse than the score.

Apps, websites and idle time

If app and URL tracking is enabled, Hubstaff records the names of applications you use and the websites you visit while the timer runs, with time spent in each. It does not record what you did inside them: a URL shows the site and page, not what you wrote there.

Idle time is a stretch with no keyboard or mouse input. Depending on your employer's settings, Hubstaff may ask whether to keep or discard idle time, or flag it on your timesheet. Long meetings and presentations can trigger it, which is a common reason for timesheet corrections.

GPS on the mobile app

On the Hubstaff phone app, an employer can record your location while you are clocked in and set job sites that clock you in and out automatically when you arrive and leave. Location is tied to the timer like everything else. If location tracking worries you on a personal phone, our guides on personal phone monitoring and employee GPS tracking cover the legal limits.

How to check what your account records

  1. Open your own dashboard. Hubstaff shows tracked members their own time, activity and, where enabled, screenshots. What you see there is what your manager sees.
  2. Read the monitoring policy. In Connecticut, Delaware, Maine and New York, employers must give notice of electronic monitoring, in writing or electronically depending on the state. Elsewhere it is good practice. Ask for the policy if you have not seen one; our monitoring laws by state guide lists the rules.
  3. Ask which features are on. Screenshots, blur, app and URL tracking, and GPS are each a setting. A direct question to your manager or HR usually gets a direct answer.
  4. Ask about retention. How long screenshots are kept, and who can see them, matters more than how often they are taken.

For employers: what to switch on

Every item above is a setting, and each one you enable is something your team will notice. Turn on what you will actually use, blur screenshots if you need proof of work rather than content, disable activity recording for meeting-heavy roles, and write it down before the agent is installed. The monitoring policy template covers the wording. Key takeaways

  • Hubstaff records only while its timer runs, and only the features your employer has switched on.
  • Screenshots, if on, are taken one to three times every ten minutes and can be blurred or disabled per project.
  • The activity percentage counts seconds with keyboard or mouse input; it does not record what you type.
  • App and URL tracking shows which sites and programs you used, not what you did inside them.
  • The mobile app can record GPS location while you are clocked in, and job sites can clock you in automatically.
  • Hubstaff does not use your webcam or microphone and does not read messages, except what a screenshot happens to capture.

Frequently asked questions

Can Hubstaff see my screen?

Only if your employer has turned screenshots on. Hubstaff then captures your screen at random moments, one to three times every ten minutes while the timer runs. Employers can blur the images or disable them per project.

Does Hubstaff record keystrokes?

No. Hubstaff counts whether a key was pressed or the mouse moved in each second to calculate an activity percentage, but it does not record which keys you pressed or what you typed.

Can Hubstaff see my browsing history?

If app and URL tracking is enabled, it records the websites you visit while the timer runs. It does not see browsing when the timer is off, and it does not record what you type on those sites.

Does Hubstaff track you when the timer is off?

No. Hubstaff records screenshots, activity, apps, URLs and location only while you are clocked in.

Can Hubstaff see my messages?

Not as text. It does not read chat or email content, but a screenshot taken while a message is open on screen will show it unless blurring is on.

Does Hubstaff use the webcam?

No. Hubstaff does not capture webcam images or audio.
