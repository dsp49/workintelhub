ActivTrak vs Insightful (2026): Screenshots, Retention and Price

P Pavan
November 26, 2026 · 6 min read

ActivTrak and Insightful are the two productivity analytics tools buyers shortlist most often, and each is the other's most common replacement. Both record application and website use, active and idle time, and turn it into utilization and capacity reports. They differ on three things that decide most evaluations: whether screenshots are part of the product, how long the data is kept, and how you are billed.

ActivTrak takes no screenshots on its standard plans, has no stealth mode and leads on workload, focus and burnout analytics, but sells annual contracts only and keeps six months to a year of history. Insightful puts screenshots on its base plan, keeps 24 months, runs on Linux and adds attendance and projects, but sells recording and extra capture as add-ons and offers a stealth mode. This comparison uses October 2026 pricing read from both vendors' pages.

The short answer

Choose ActivTrak if you want to be able to say, truthfully, that nobody is being photographed: no screenshots on standard plans, no keystrokes, a visible agent, and the most developed workload and burnout reports. The realistic plan is Workforce Management at $15 or Productivity Optimization at $17, annual only.

Choose Insightful if you want screenshots available, two years of history, a Linux agent, attendance and projects in the same tool, and the option of month-to-month billing. The realistic plan is Workforce Analytics at $8, plus add-ons for what you need.

Choose neither if you need mobile or GPS, keystroke logging or data loss prevention. Hubstaff covers the first; Teramind and Veriato the second.

Screenshots and stealth: how each one feels to staff

This is the difference that decides how employees experience the tool. ActivTrak's standard plans take no images at all. Screenshots, full URLs, live screen view and website blocking come only with the Screen Details add-on, which is priced on request, sold only on Work Activity Tracking and Workforce Management, and not offered on the higher plans. ActivTrak has no stealth mode. A manager can tell a team that the tool sees which applications are open and for how long, and nothing more, and that will be true.

Insightful includes screenshots on its base plan and sells on-demand screenshots, capture of up to 120 an hour, and screen recording as add-ons. It also offers a stealth mode on company-owned devices, which removes the employee's own dashboard. Employees who discover a hidden agent tend to treat every later number as suspect, and in Connecticut, Delaware and New York a hidden agent sits badly with the written monitoring notice the law requires. If you choose Insightful, run it visibly. Our guide to what ActivTrak can see sets out the employee view on one side, and what Insightful can see on the other.

Analytics: workload versus capacity and attendance

Both tools classify applications and websites by role and report utilization and capacity. ActivTrak goes further on the management layer: focus time, schedule adherence, workload balance and, on the higher plans, burnout signals, capacity planning and AI coaching prompts for one-on-ones. Its offline meeting time and calendar integrations try to stop meetings counting as idle. Our productivity monitoring review ranks ActivTrak first and Insightful second, largely on these reports.

Insightful's advantage is breadth. On its analytics plan it adds time and attendance, automatic and manual clock-in, break capture, shift scheduling, projects and manual time, so a team can run hours and analytics in one tool. Combo adds payroll and location insights. Its 24 months of history also make year-on-year comparison possible without buying extra years. Reviewers find its automatic time mapping less reliable than manual logs, and like every activity-based tool its score drops during calls and reading.

Price at the plan you will actually use

Headline prices flatter both. ActivTrak's $10 plan lacks the reports most buyers want, which sit on the $15 and $17 plans. Insightful's $8 plan includes screenshots, but recording and workspace security are $4 each, so an $8 plan with both is $16, the same as Combo.

Switching between them

Neither tool imports the other's data. Moving from ActivTrak, export through the ActivConnect add-on or download report files before the contract ends, and remember that ActivTrak's remote uninstall deletes that computer's data, so export first. Moving from Insightful, pull its reports to CSV first, because its 24 months is the record nobody else will hold, and reclassify your applications in the new tool before the old contract runs out. Either way, if the new tool records more, issue a fresh monitoring notice.

Key takeaways

  • ActivTrak takes no screenshots on standard plans and has no stealth mode; Insightful includes screenshots on its base plan and offers stealth on company devices.
  • ActivTrak costs $10 to $21 per user per month on annual contracts only; Insightful costs $8 (Workforce Analytics) or $16 (Combo), with month-to-month on select plans.
  • Insightful keeps 24 months of history; ActivTrak keeps six months to a year, extendable at $1 per user per month per year.
  • ActivTrak leads on workload, focus and burnout analytics and runs on Chromebook; Insightful adds Linux, attendance, projects and payroll on Combo.
  • Neither logs keystrokes, and neither has a mobile app or GPS.

Frequently asked questions

Is ActivTrak better than Insightful?

It depends on whether you want screenshots. ActivTrak takes none on its standard plans, has no stealth mode and has the stronger workload and burnout reports. Insightful includes screenshots, keeps 24 months of history, runs on Linux and adds attendance and projects, at a lower entry price.

Which is cheaper, ActivTrak or Insightful?

Insightful at entry: Workforce Analytics is $8 per user per month against ActivTrak's $10, and $15 for the Workforce Management plan most ActivTrak buyers choose, as read in October 2026. Insightful's add-ons at $3 to $4 each narrow the gap.

Does ActivTrak or Insightful take screenshots?

Insightful includes screenshots on its base plan and sells more frequent capture and screen recording as add-ons. ActivTrak takes screenshots only through the Screen Details add-on on its two lower plans, and not at all on the higher ones.

Which keeps data longer?

Insightful, with 24 months on every listed plan. ActivTrak keeps six months on Work Activity Tracking and Workforce Management and a year on Productivity Optimization, extendable to three years at $1 per user per month for each extra year.

Do ActivTrak and Insightful work on Linux?

Insightful has a Linux agent. ActivTrak has no native Linux agent and suggests its Chrome-based agent for other operating systems. Neither has a mobile app.
