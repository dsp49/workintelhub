How Many Weeks in a Work Year

WH The WorkIntel Hub editorial team
October 5, 2026 · 7 min read

A calendar year has 52 weeks and one day (two in a leap year), which is 52.14 weeks on average. Payroll rounds that to 52, which is where the 2,080-hour year comes from. But the answer most people are looking for is different: how many weeks do you actually work? For a full-time US employee with three weeks of leave, ten paid holidays and a few sick days, the figure is about 46 to 47 weeks, and for budgeting, pricing or deciding whether a salary is fair, that is the number that matters.

The calculator gives all three figures for any year, schedule and leave allowance, and counts the paydays. The rest of the page explains where each number comes from, which one to use for which purpose, and how the 53-week years that trip up weekly payroll happen.

The three numbers

Weeks actually workedAbout 46 to 48Work days minus PTO, holidays and sick days, ÷ 5Budgets, pricing, billable targets, what a salary buys

An employee with 15 days of PTO, 10 paid holidays and 3 sick days takes 28 days off, which is 5.6 weeks, leaving 46.6 weeks worked in a year with 261 weekdays. At 40 hours that is about 1,865 hours, against the 2,080 payroll convention; the work hours in a year page works the same arithmetic in hours and the working days in 2027 page in days.

Which number for which purpose

  • Converting salary to an hourly rate: 52 weeks, 2,080 hours. It is the convention payroll systems use and it keeps comparisons consistent; the salary to hourly calculator applies it.
  • Budgeting labour or planning capacity: weeks actually worked. A plan built on 52 weeks overcommits by 10 to 12 per cent, which is the gap between the plan and the year that every capacity plan eventually discovers.
  • Setting billable targets: weeks actually worked, then the billable share of each. The billable hours calculator turns a weekly target into an annual figure and shows what utilisation it implies.
  • Comparing a contract rate to a salary: weeks the contractor can bill, usually 44 to 46 once unpaid leave, admin time and gaps between engagements are removed.
  • Counting paydays: the number of Fridays (or whichever weekday) in the year. A year with 53 of them has 53 weekly paydays and, if the bi-weekly cycle is anchored to the first one, 27 bi-weekly paydays; the pay periods guide covers what employers do about it.

Why some years have 53 weeks

Because 52 weeks is 364 days and the year has 365 or 366, each year starts one weekday later than the last (two after a leap year). Every five or six years the extra day lands so that a given weekday occurs 53 times. 2027 starts on a Friday and has 53 Fridays; 2032, a leap year starting on a Thursday, has 53 Thursdays and 53 Fridays. Accounting systems that use a 52-or-53-week fiscal year, common in retail, add a 53rd week on the same schedule. Nothing about the amount of work changes; only the number of pay periods, which is why the question matters to payroll and to nobody else.

Key takeaways

  • A year has 52.14 calendar weeks; payroll uses 52; a full-time employee with typical leave works about 46 to 48.
  • Use 52 weeks (2,080 hours) for salary conversion and FTE counts; use weeks actually worked for budgets, capacity, billable targets and contract comparisons.
  • 15 PTO days, 10 holidays and 3 sick days remove 5.6 weeks, leaving about 46.6 worked in a 261-weekday year.
  • Years with 53 of the payday weekday (2027 for Fridays) have 53 weekly paydays and possibly 27 bi-weekly ones.

Frequently asked questions

How many weeks are in a work year?

Payroll uses 52. The calendar has 52.14 (365 days divided by 7). A full-time employee who takes three weeks of leave, ten paid holidays and a few sick days works about 46 to 47 weeks. Which number to use depends on the purpose: 52 for salary conversion, weeks actually worked for budgets and targets.

How many work weeks are in 2027?

2027 has 261 weekdays, which is 52.2 five-day work weeks, and 53 Fridays. An employee with 15 days of PTO, 10 holidays and 3 sick days works about 46.6 weeks. The calculator on this page adjusts for any leave allowance and schedule.

How many weeks is 2,080 hours?

52 weeks at 40 hours. The 2,080 figure is the payroll convention for a full-time year and ignores holidays and leave; actual hours worked for a full-time employee with typical leave are closer to 1,850 to 1,900.

How many weeks do I work if I get four weeks of vacation?

About 45.6 with ten paid holidays and three sick days: 261 weekdays minus 20 vacation days, 10 holidays and 3 sick days leaves 228 days, or 45.6 five-day weeks. Fewer holidays or sick days push the figure toward 47.

Why does 2027 have 53 pay periods?

Because it starts on a Friday and contains 53 of them. A weekly schedule paid on Fridays has 53 paydays; a bi-weekly schedule whose first 2027 payday is January 1 has 27. Schedules anchored to January 8 get the usual 26. It is a payroll effect only; the amount of work is unchanged.
