Time Tracking Software Pricing (2026): Per-User Costs Compared

P Pavan
November 26, 2026 · 9 min read

Time tracking software costs between $3.90 and $9 per user per month on annual billing for an entry plan, and between $5.49 and $16 for the plan that adds timesheet approvals, based on the twelve tools we compared in October 2026. Several tools are free for small or even unlimited teams, and one, QuickBooks Time, adds a monthly base fee of $20 or $40 on top of the per-user price.

The entry price is rarely the bill. Seat minimums, base fees, monthly billing and the tier that carries approvals, invoicing or payroll decide what a team pays. This page sets out both prices for each tool, explains where the bill grows, and the calculator works out the monthly and annual cost for your headcount.

The short answer

Entry plans run $3.90 to $9 per user a month on annual billing: Time Champ $3.90, Clockify and TimeCamp $3.99, Hubstaff $4.99, DeskTime $6.42, Time Doctor $6.70, Everhour $8.50, and Toggl Track, Harvest and Timely $9.

The plan with approvals costs more: $5.49 on Clockify, $9.99 on TimeCamp, $11.70 on Time Doctor, $14 on Harvest and $16 on Toggl Track.

Monthly billing adds 9% to 50%, and minimums (Hubstaff 2 seats, Everhour 5) and base fees (QuickBooks Time) matter most for small teams.

Five things that move the bill

  1. The plan you actually need. Approvals, invoicing, payroll and attendance usually sit one or two tiers above the entry plan. Toggl Track's approvals are on Premium at $16, not Starter at $9; Time Doctor's attendance and payroll are on Standard at $11.70, not Basic at $6.70.
  2. Seat minimums. Hubstaff bills at least two seats and Everhour at least five, so a solo user or a team of three pays for seats nobody uses.
  3. Base fees. QuickBooks Time charges $20 a month on Premium or $40 on Elite before the per-user price. At five users that is $70 a month on Premium; at 50 users it is $520.
  4. Monthly billing. Paying month to month costs more everywhere it is offered: Clockify Basic rises from $3.99 to $4.99, Toggl Starter from $9 to $12, TimeCamp Ultimate from $9.99 to $13.99, and Hubstaff Starter from $4.99 to $7.
  5. Add-ons. Some features are sold separately, such as Hubstaff's GPS and Insights on lower tiers, Timely's Tasks add-on from $5, and Time Champ's project management and HR modules. Price the add-on against the next tier up.

What a team pays at 5, 15 and 50 users

At five users the free plans and the $4 tier dominate: Clockify is free, Jibble and TimeCamp are free at any size, and Time Champ Starter is $19.50 a month. At 15 users the cheapest paid entry plans cost about $58 to $60 a month, and Toggl Starter or Harvest Teams cost $135. At 50 users the spread on annual billing runs from about $195 a month (Time Champ Starter) to $800 (Timely Premium), and QuickBooks Time Premium costs $520 including its base fee.

Those figures are entry plans. If you need approvals, recalculate on the higher tier: 15 users on Clockify Standard cost $82.35 a month, on Time Doctor Standard $175.50, and on Toggl Premium $240. The calculator above does this for any headcount.

Price is not the same as value

A cheaper tool that people stop using costs more than a dearer one they keep using, because the hours it was meant to capture end up reconstructed from memory. Before comparing prices, decide which kind of tool you are buying: a project and billing tracker, a time clock for hourly staff, an automatic tracker, or a tracker with monitoring. Prices only compare within a kind. Our checklist of time tracking software features sets out what each kind should do, and the full comparison of twelve time trackers covers who each tool fits.

The other cost is the one the software saves. If you are building a case for the purchase, the time tracking cost-benefit analysis template sets the subscription against recovered billable hours and payroll accuracy. And if part of your team is salaried, check first whether you need their hours at all: tracking hours for salaried employees explains what federal law requires for exempt and non-exempt staff.

Four ways to pay less

  • Pay annually for your stable core. Annual billing is cheaper on every tool that offers both, but several vendors, Hubstaff and Everhour among them, do not refund seats or months you stop using mid-term.
  • Buy the tier for the feature, not the tier above. If approvals are the only reason to upgrade, Clockify Standard at $5.49 and Hubstaff Grow at $7.50 carry them for less than half of Toggl Premium.
  • Count who really needs a seat. Some tools bill everyone who logs in, managers included. Hubstaff's free Project Viewer role exists for people who only read reports.
  • Run one real pay period in the trial. Harvest and Toggl Track offer 30 days, Hubstaff and Time Doctor 14 days. Use them to check whether you used anything above the entry plan.

Key takeaways

  • Entry plans cost $3.90 to $9 per user a month on annual billing in October 2026, from Time Champ at $3.90 to Toggl Track, Harvest and Timely at $9.
  • The plan with timesheet approvals costs $5.49 on Clockify, $7.50 on Hubstaff, $9.99 on TimeCamp, $11.70 on Time Doctor, $14 on Harvest and $16 on Toggl Track.
  • Jibble and TimeCamp are free for unlimited users; Clockify is free for 5 and Toggl Track for 3 on new accounts.
  • Hubstaff's 2-seat and Everhour's 5-seat minimums, and QuickBooks Time's $20 or $40 base fee, weigh most on small teams.
  • Monthly billing costs 9% to 50% more than annual on the tools that offer both.
  • Compare prices only within one kind of tool: billing tracker, time clock, automatic tracker or tracker with monitoring.

Frequently asked questions

How much does time tracking software cost per user?

In October 2026, entry plans cost $3.90 to $9 per user per month on annual billing across twelve common tools, and plans with timesheet approvals cost $5.49 to $16. QuickBooks Time adds a $20 or $40 monthly base fee to its $10 or $12 per-user price.

What is the cheapest time tracking software?

Jibble and TimeCamp have free plans for unlimited users, and Clockify is free for up to 5 users. Among paid entry plans, Time Champ Starter at $3.90 and Clockify Basic and TimeCamp Starter at $3.99 per user a month on annual billing were the lowest we recorded.

Is annual or monthly billing better for time tracking software?

Annual is cheaper on every tool that offers both, by 9% to 50%. Monthly makes sense when headcount swings, because several vendors do not refund annual seats or months you stop using before renewal.

Why does QuickBooks Time cost more for small teams?

QuickBooks Time charges a monthly base fee of $20 on Premium or $40 on Elite on top of $10 or $12 per user. For five users on Premium that is $70 a month, or $14 a user, against $3.99 to $9 for most per-user tools.

Which time tracking software has the cheapest timesheet approvals?

Jibble includes approvals on its free plan. Among paid plans in our comparison, Clockify Standard at $5.49 and Hubstaff Grow at $7.50 per user a month on annual billing were the lowest; Toggl Track puts approvals on Premium at $16.

Do time tracking tools have seat minimums?

Some do. Hubstaff bills at least 2 seats on every paid plan and Everhour at least 5 on its Team plan. Timely caps users instead: 5 on Starter and 50 on Premium.
