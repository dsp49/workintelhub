Insightful Pricing (2026): Plans, Add-ons and What Teams Really Pay

P Pavan
November 26, 2026 · 7 min read

Insightful, the workforce analytics tool formerly called Workpuls, charges per seat per month on annual billing: Workforce Analytics at $8, Workflow Optimization at $12 (in beta for select customers) and Combo at $16. Enterprise and Hyperscaler are priced by quote. Month-to-month billing exists on select plans, but Insightful does not publish that price.

The plan price is only the start. Screen recording, on-demand screenshots, higher-frequency screenshots and the workspace security module are each sold as a $3 to $4 add-on per seat, so a team that wants recording and security on the $8 plan pays $16 before it has bought Combo. This page sets out what each plan costs, where the extra charges come from, and how the bill compares with the tools people move to. The calculator turns it into a monthly and annual figure for your team.

The short answer

Most teams start at $8 per seat a month on Workforce Analytics, billed annually, which already includes app and website tracking, activity, optional screenshots and time and attendance.

Add-ons decide the real price. Recording, on-demand screenshots and workspace security are $4 each and higher-frequency screenshots $3, so two add-ons double the base plan.

Budget for every device-level account. Reviewers report system accounts being counted as billable seats; audit the seat list in the first month.

Each one changes what your staff are living with, and in Connecticut, Delaware and New York employees must be given notice of electronic monitoring. Where the bill grows: five hidden costs

  1. Add-ons stack. Workspace security plus screen recording adds $8 a seat, which takes the $8 plan to $16, the same as Combo without Combo's payroll and location insights.
  2. Seat counting. Reviewers describe system accounts such as local admin and default profiles being counted as billable users, and disputes over seats added automatically. Check the seat list against your headcount every month at first.
  3. Annual billing. The published prices are annual. Month-to-month is on select plans only and its price is not published, so a team that shrinks may keep paying for seats until renewal.
  4. A second tool for field staff. Insightful runs on Windows, macOS and Linux with no mobile app and no GPS. Drivers and anyone who clocks in from a phone need something else.
  5. On-premises needs scale. Self-hosting is an Enterprise option from 250 seats. A smaller regulated employer that must keep data in house cannot buy it.

None of these is rare in the category, but together they explain why the tools people switch to from Insightful are mostly the ones that put screenshots or recording on plan rather than the ones with the lowest headline.

Five ways to pay less for Insightful

  • Price the add-ons before the plan. If you need two of them, compare the total against Combo and against tools that put recording on plan.
  • Buy add-ons for the seats that need them. Recording for a security team does not have to mean recording for the whole company; ask whether add-ons can be applied to a subset.
  • Audit the seat list monthly. Remove system accounts, shared machines and departed staff before they reach an invoice.
  • Use the 7-day trial on one question. Seven days is short; decide in advance which report you need and check that the base plan produces it.
  • Leave off what you will not read. Screenshots no one opens are a privacy cost as well as a line item, and our monitoring policy template is easier to write when the list is short.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Insightful cost per user?

Insightful costs $8 per seat per month for Workforce Analytics and $16 for Combo, billed annually, with Workflow Optimization at $12 in beta and Enterprise by quote, as read from its pricing page in October 2026. Add-ons cost $0.50 to $4 per seat each.

Does Insightful have a free plan?

No. Insightful offers a 7-day trial. For a permanently free option, ActivTrak is free for 3 users and WorkTime for 3 employees, both with limited history.

What are Insightful's add-ons?

Workspace Security ($4), On-Demand Screenshots ($4), Increased-Frequency Screenshots of up to 120 an hour ($3), Screen Recording ($4) and Data Warehouse Integrations ($0.50), each per seat per month.

Can you pay for Insightful monthly?

Month-to-month billing is available on select plans, but Insightful does not publish the price. The published prices are for annual billing.

Is Insightful cheaper than ActivTrak?

At entry level, yes: Insightful starts at $8 per seat against ActivTrak's $10, both on annual billing, and Insightful keeps 24 months of data against ActivTrak's six months to a year on the lower plans. Add-ons can close the gap.

Is Insightful the same as Workpuls?

Yes. Workpuls was renamed Insightful on April 6, 2022, so older reviews and price lists may still use the old name.
