HR Software Pricing (2026): What Small and Mid-Size Teams Pay

P Pavan
November 26, 2026 · 8 min read

Most small and mid-size companies pay between $1.25 and $25 per employee per month for HR software, plus a base fee on some plans. A budget HRIS such as Zoho People costs $1.25 to $5, a core HRIS such as BambooHR lists at $10 to $25, a payroll-first product such as Gusto charges $49 to $180 a month plus $6 to $22 per person, and a PEO such as Justworks charges $59 to $109 per employee before health premiums. Full HCM suites are quoted, and independent estimates for them cluster around $20 to $35 per employee.

The headline price is rarely the bill. Add-ons for payroll, time and benefits, implementation fees and renewal increases move the total more than the tier you choose. This page collects the published prices recorded in our HR software comparison and our workforce management review, explains the pricing models behind them, and ends with a calculator for a first-year budget.

The short answer

Under 25 people: expect anything from free to a few hundred dollars a month. Free tiers from Zoho People, Deel HR and Homebase are real products, and Gusto Simple is $49 plus $6 per person.

25 to 150 people: a core HRIS at roughly $10 to $25 per employee, more once payroll and benefits add-ons are included.

150 people and up: mostly quote-only suites, with independent estimates of $20 to $35 per employee and an implementation fee on top.

A PEO is a different budget line: $59 to $125 per employee per month, with health premiums charged on top.

The six pricing models, and what each does as you grow

  • Per employee per month. Zoho People, BambooHR. Linear: double the headcount, double the bill. Check whether the vendor counts every employee on payroll or only people who log in; Workday and most suites count everyone on payroll.
  • Base fee plus per person. Gusto, Rippling, and Paycor's last published ladder. The base fee dominates on a team of five and fades by fifty.
  • Per location. Homebase. Cheap at two or three sites, awkward at forty.
  • PEO per employee. Justworks, Deel's US PEO. The fee buys co-employment, payroll under the PEO's tax accounts and access to large-group benefits. Reasonable at 20 people and a large line at 200.
  • Employer of record per employee. Deel at $599. A bridge into a country where you have no entity; once a country has more than a handful of employees, an entity and local payroll are usually cheaper.
  • Quoted suites. HiBob, Paycor, Workday, and in workforce management UKG Ready (estimates of $20 to $33 per employee), ADP Workforce Now ($23 to $30) and Dayforce ($22 to $34). The price depends on modules, headcount, contract length and timing.

Costs that never reach the pricing page

  1. Implementation. Rippling, HiBob and Paycor commonly charge it. For UKG, Dayforce and Workday our workforce management research found implementations of 20% to 60% of first-year software cost, and Workday programs for large organizations run to six or seven figures.
  2. Add-ons and modules. A company that starts on Rippling with HR and payroll and adds four modules over a year can find its cost per head has tripled.
  3. Renewal increases. Rippling, Paycor and Workday sell multi-year terms, and renewal increases of 5% to 15% are commonly reported. Ask for the renewal cap in writing.
  4. Minimums and band boundaries. BambooHR's flat rate under 25 employees, free tiers that end at 5 or 10 people, and per-employee rates that change as headcount crosses a band.
  5. Migration and parallel running. If payroll is moving, budget for one cycle run in both systems and for the staff time to reconcile it.

Gusto, Homebase and Zoho People are sold monthly or annually with no lock-in, which is worth something on its own if headcount is uncertain.

How to get a real number out of a quote

Ask for these in writing and keep the reply: full price at your headcount with the modules you will actually use, the implementation fee and what it includes, contract term, renewal cap, how employees are counted, data export format, and what happens to your data 90 days after cancellation.   • Published HR software prices run from free to about $25 per employee per month; PEOs charge $59 to $125 and an employer of record $599.
  • Zoho People costs $1.25 to $5, BambooHR lists $10 to $25, and Gusto charges $49 to $180 a month plus $6 to $22 per person.

Frequently asked questions

How much does HR software cost per employee?

Published prices run from $1.25 to $5 per employee per month for Zoho People and $10 to $25 list for BambooHR. Gusto charges $49 to $180 a month plus $6 to $22 per person. Quote-only suites are estimated at roughly $20 to $35 per employee, and Workday at about $34 to $45 at scale.

What is the cheapest HR software?

Among the ten platforms we compared, Zoho People is the lowest-priced real HRIS: free up to five employees, then $1.25 to $5 per employee per month on annual billing. Deel HR is free as a core HRIS, and Homebase Basic is free for one location with up to ten employees.

How much does a PEO cost?

Justworks published PEO Basic at $59 and PEO Plus at $109 per employee per month, with discounts from about 25 to 50 employees, and Deel's US PEO at $125. Health premiums are charged on top and are usually the larger number.

Do HR software vendors charge implementation fees?

Many do. Rippling, HiBob and Paycor commonly charge them, and for large suites such as UKG, Dayforce and Workday implementation has run 20% to 60% of first-year software cost. Gusto, Homebase and Zoho People can be set up in days without one.

How much does BambooHR cost?

BambooHR lists Core at $10, Pro at $17 and Elite at $25 per employee per month, with a flat rate from about $250 a month for companies under 25 employees. The final price is confirmed by quote, and US payroll, time tracking and benefits administration are add-ons.

Why do HR software vendors not publish prices?

Suites such as HiBob, Paycor and Workday price by modules, headcount and contract length, and negotiate. Paycor withdrew its published pricing in 2025. Ask for the full price at your headcount, the implementation fee and the renewal cap in writing.
