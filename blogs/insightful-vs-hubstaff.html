Insightful vs Hubstaff (2026): Analytics, GPS, Price and Who Each Fits

P Pavan
November 26, 2026 · 6 min read

Insightful and Hubstaff both record application use, activity and optional screenshots, and both turn up on the same shortlists. They start from different places. Insightful is workforce analytics for the desktop: capacity and utilization reports, 24 months of history, and a stealth mode for company devices. Hubstaff is a time tracker first: a visible timer against projects, optional and blurrable screenshots, GPS job sites on the phone, and payroll from tracked hours.

The choice usually comes down to two questions. Do any of your people work away from a desk? And do you want the agent visible to the people it records? This comparison uses October 2026 pricing read from both vendors' sites and describes how each tool records work from its own published material.

The short answer

Choose Insightful if your team works at Windows, Mac or Linux desktops, you want capacity and utilization analytics with attendance, and two years of history matters. The realistic plan is Workforce Analytics at $8, plus add-ons if you need recording.

Choose Hubstaff if any of your staff work in the field, you want time tracked against projects and paid out through payroll, or you want a visible agent with no stealth option. The realistic plan is Grow at $7.50 or Team at $10.

Choose neither if you need keystrokes, data loss prevention or on-premises for a small team. Teramind and Veriato cover that.

Desk or field: the first question

Insightful covers desktops only. There is no mobile app, so it cannot record location, clock someone in from a job site, or cover a driver, a nurse on home visits or a technician on the road. If even part of your workforce is in that position, you will need a second tool next to Insightful, and two tools means two notices, two sets of settings and two bills.

Hubstaff's phone app records GPS location while people are clocked in, and job sites clock them in and out automatically when they arrive and leave. On the lower tiers GPS is an add-on. For an all-desk team the difference does not matter; for a mixed team it usually decides the evaluation. Our employee GPS tracking guide covers the rules for recording location.

Visible or hidden: the second question

Insightful offers a stealth mode on company-owned devices. Personal devices default to visible mode, and in stealth mode the employee dashboard and automatic time mapping are unavailable. Hubstaff has no stealth option: the timer sits in the tray, employees start and stop it, and they can see their own time, activity and screenshots.

Monitoring company equipment is broadly permitted in the US, but a hidden agent does not satisfy the monitoring notice laws in Connecticut, Delaware and New York, and employees who discover a hidden agent tend to treat every later report as suspect. If you plan to run Insightful hidden, read our monitoring laws by state first. For how each tool looks from the employee's side, see what Insightful can see and what Hubstaff can see.

Analytics versus time tracking

Insightful's core is reporting about how work time is spent: application and website use, active and idle time, productivity classified by team, capacity and utilization, and on Combo whether a day was worked in the office, remotely or hybrid. Time and attendance is on the base plan, so clock-in, breaks and shifts come with the analytics.

Hubstaff's core is hours against projects. Its activity level is the share of tracked seconds with keyboard or mouse input, and those hours feed time off, overtime, invoicing and payroll. Its analytics are thinner than Insightful's capacity reports, and the Insights module is an add-on on lower tiers. Both tools' activity measures drop during calls and meetings; neither measures output. The mouse jiggler guide explains what happens when people are managed on that number.

Price at the plan you will actually use

At base level the two are close: Insightful Workforce Analytics at $8 against Hubstaff Grow at $7.50 and Team at $10. The gap opens with add-ons. An Insightful seat with screen recording and workspace security is $16; a Hubstaff seat on Grow with GPS costs Grow plus the GPS add-on.

If you need what both lack, look past them. Key takeaways

  • Insightful is desktop workforce analytics with 24 months of history; Hubstaff is a time tracker with mobile GPS and payroll.
  • At base level, Insightful costs $8 and Hubstaff $7.50 (Grow) or $10 (Team) per seat per month on annual billing, as read in October 2026.
  • Insightful sells screen recording, on-demand and higher-frequency screenshots and security as $3 to $4 add-ons; Hubstaff sells GPS, extra screenshots and Insights as add-ons on lower tiers.
  • Insightful offers stealth mode on company devices; Hubstaff's agent is always visible.
  • Insightful has no mobile app or GPS; Hubstaff runs on iOS and Android with job sites.
  • Choose Insightful for office analytics and long history, Hubstaff for field and payroll-led teams.

Frequently asked questions

Is Insightful better than Hubstaff?

For office and hybrid teams that want capacity and utilization analytics with 24 months of history, Insightful is the stronger fit. For teams with field staff, contractors paid by the hour, or a preference for a visible agent, Hubstaff is.

Which is cheaper, Insightful or Hubstaff?

At base level they are close: Insightful Workforce Analytics is $8 and Hubstaff Grow $7.50 per seat per month on annual billing, as read in October 2026. Insightful becomes dearer once you add recording or security at $4 each per seat.

Does Insightful have GPS tracking like Hubstaff?

No. Insightful has no mobile app and no GPS. Its Combo plan reports whether days were worked in the office, remotely or hybrid. Hubstaff's mobile app records GPS location while people are clocked in.

Does Hubstaff have a stealth mode?

No. Hubstaff's agent is visible and employees start and stop the timer. Insightful offers stealth mode on company-owned devices.

Do Insightful and Hubstaff work on Linux?

Yes. Both offer a Linux desktop agent alongside Windows and macOS.
