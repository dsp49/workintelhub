2027 Payroll Tax Rates and Limits: The Complete Reference

P Pavan
November 13, 2026 · 12 min read

Most of the numbers a payroll team needs for 2027 were published between May and November 2026: the Social Security wage base with the cost-of-living adjustment in October, the income tax brackets and benefit limits in the IRS's annual inflation revenue procedure on November 5, and the health savings account limits in May. A few were still pending when this page was written, notably the 2027 retirement plan limits (normally announced in mid-November) and the final list of FUTA credit reduction states (announced after November 10). This page gathers what is confirmed, marks what is pending, and sets the 2026 figures alongside for comparison.

Use it with the year-end payroll checklist, the 2027 payroll calendar and the 2027 minimum wage table. Figures should be confirmed against the source cited before they are keyed into a payroll system.

Employers stop withholding Social Security tax once an employee's wages reach the base, including wages paid by a common paymaster; an employee with two unrelated employers may have tax withheld above the base by both and claims the excess on Form 1040. Medicare has no cap, and the 0.9% additional tax must be withheld from wages over $200,000 paid by one employer regardless of filing status.

FUTA and credit reduction

The federal unemployment tax is 6.0% of the first $7,000 of each employee's wages, reduced by a credit of up to 5.4% for state unemployment taxes paid on time, for a net rate of 0.6%, or $42 per employee a year. In states that have an outstanding federal unemployment loan for two consecutive January 1s and have not repaid it by November 10, the credit is reduced, raising the effective FUTA rate for that year's wages, payable with the fourth-quarter Form 940.

  • 2025 (final): California 1.2% reduction (net 1.8%), Virgin Islands 4.5%.
  • 2026 (potential, Department of Labor spreadsheet): California, 1.5% base reduction plus a possible benefit cost rate add-on of 3.8% unless the state obtained a waiver; Virgin Islands 4.8%, though it reportedly repaid its loans in June 2026 and may drop off. The final 2026 list is published after November 10, 2026; check the Department of Labor credit reduction page before filing the 2026 Form 940.

For a California employer, a 1.5% reduction adds $105 per employee to 2026 FUTA; the add-on, if applied, would add another $266.

Wage and hour figures

  • Federal minimum wage: $7.25, unchanged since July 24, 2009. Thirty states and many cities set higher rates; see the 2027 table.
  • FLSA exempt salary threshold: $684 a week ($35,568 a year), and $107,432 for highly compensated employees. The 2024 rule raising these figures was vacated in November 2024 and formally rescinded on May 15, 2026. State thresholds are higher in California, Colorado, New York and Washington; see the 2027 exempt threshold guide.
  • Standard mileage rate: 72.5 cents (January to June 2026) and 76 cents (July to December 2026); the 2027 rate is expected in late December. See the mileage calculator.
  • Tips and overtime deductions: up to $25,000 for qualified tips and $12,500 ($25,000 joint) for qualified overtime premium, for 2025 to 2028; see the tips and overtime calculators.

Forms, codes and deadlines

  • 2026 Forms W-2 and W-3: due to employees and the Social Security Administration by Monday, February 1, 2027, because January 31 falls on a Sunday. Forms 1099-NEC follow the same date.
  • New W-2 codes for 2026: box 12 code TP (total cash tips reported), TT (total qualified overtime compensation) and TA (employer Trump account contributions); box 14 split into 14a (other) and 14b (Treasury tipped occupation code).
  • Information return threshold: the reporting threshold for certain non-withholding payments (Forms 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC) rises from $600 to $2,000 for payments made after December 31, 2025, indexed after 2026.
  • Form 941 due dates in 2027: Q4 2026 on February 1; Q1 2027 on April 30; Q2 on August 2 (July 31 is a Saturday); Q3 on November 1 (October 31 is a Sunday); Q4 2027 on January 31, 2028.
  • Form 940 for 2026: February 1, 2027 (February 10 if all FUTA deposits were made on time).
  • ACA Forms 1095-C to employees by March 2, 2027 (the permanent 30-day extension); Forms 1094-C and 1095-C to the IRS by March 1 on paper or March 31, 2027 electronically.

Key takeaways

  • The 2027 Social Security wage base is reported at $192,300 (up from $184,500; confirm with the SSA fact sheet), making the maximum employee Social Security tax $11,922.60; FICA rates stay 6.2% and 1.45%, with the 0.9% Additional Medicare Tax over $200,000.
  • FUTA stays 6.0% on $7,000 (0.6% net); California is the main 2026 credit reduction state (1.5% plus a possible add-on), with the final list due after November 10, 2026.
  • Rev. Proc. 2026-35 sets 2027 brackets (12% from $12,800 single, $25,600 joint; 37% above $661,375 and $793,650) and a $16,600 / $33,200 standard deduction; supplemental withholding stays 22% and 37%.
  • 2027 benefit limits: HSA $4,500 / $9,000, HDHP minimum deductible $1,750 / $3,500, health FSA $3,500 with $700 carryover, dependent care FSA $7,500, transit and parking $350 a month, adoption $18,250, QSEHRA $6,700 / $13,550; ACA penalties $3,780 and $5,670.
  • 2027 401(k) limits had not been announced on November 13, 2026; 2026 limits are $24,500, $8,000 catch-up and $11,250 for ages 60 to 63, with mandatory Roth catch-up for prior-year wages above $150,000.
  • Federal minimum wage $7.25 and exempt threshold $684 a week ($107,432 HCE) are unchanged after the 2024 rule's rescission in May 2026.
  • 2026 W-2s are due February 1, 2027 with new box 12 codes TP, TT and TA and box 14b for the tipped occupation code; Form 941 dates in 2027 are February 1, April 30, August 2 and November 1.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Social Security wage base for 2027?

$192,300, according to reporting of the Social Security Administration's October 14, 2026 announcement, up $7,800 from $184,500 in 2026. At 6.2%, the maximum Social Security tax withheld from one employee in 2027 is $11,922.60, matched by the employer. Confirm the figure against the SSA's fact sheet before keying it.

What are the 2027 federal tax brackets?

For single filers: 10% up to $12,800, 12% to $52,025, 22% to $109,125, 24% to $208,325, 32% to $264,550, 35% to $661,375 and 37% above. For joint filers the thresholds are $25,600, $104,050, $218,250, $416,650, $529,100 and $793,650. Source: Revenue Procedure 2026-35.

What is the 2027 401(k) contribution limit?

Not yet announced as of November 13, 2026. The 2026 limit is $24,500 with an $8,000 catch-up for age 50 and over and $11,250 for ages 60 to 63. Actuarial projections put 2027 at about $25,500. The IRS normally publishes the figures in mid-November.

What are the 2027 HSA limits?

$4,500 for self-only coverage and $9,000 for family coverage, with a $1,000 catch-up for those 55 and older. The high-deductible plan minimum deductible is $1,750 self-only and $3,500 family, and the out-of-pocket maximum is $8,700 and $17,400. Source: Revenue Procedure 2026-24.

What is the 2027 FSA limit?

The health FSA salary reduction limit is $3,500 for 2027, with a carryover of up to $700 where the plan allows it. The dependent care FSA limit is $7,500 ($3,750 married filing separately), raised from $5,000 by the 2025 tax law and not indexed.

What is the FUTA rate for 2027?

6.0% on the first $7,000 of wages, with up to a 5.4% credit for timely state unemployment tax, for a net 0.6% ($42 per employee). Employers in credit reduction states pay more; California faces a reduction for 2026 wages, finalized after November 10, 2026.

When are 2026 W-2s due?

Monday, February 1, 2027, to employees and to the Social Security Administration, because January 31, 2027 is a Sunday. Forms 1099-NEC are due the same day. The 2026 W-2 has new box 12 codes for tips (TP), qualified overtime (TT) and Trump account contributions (TA).

Did the federal minimum wage or overtime threshold change for 2027?

No. The federal minimum wage remains $7.25 an hour and the FLSA exempt salary threshold remains $684 a week ($35,568 a year), with $107,432 for highly compensated employees. Many states and cities set higher minimum wages and several states set higher exempt thresholds.
