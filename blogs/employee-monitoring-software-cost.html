How Much Does Employee Monitoring Software Cost? 2026 Prices

P Pavan
November 20, 2026 · 10 min read

Employee monitoring software costs from nothing to about $35 per user per month, with most mid-market tools between $5 and $20. The spread follows what the software does: basic time and activity tracking with screenshots sits at the low end, productivity analytics in the middle, and insider-risk tools with keystroke capture, data loss prevention and session recording at the top. Seat minimums, annual-only billing, data retention limits and paid add-ons change the real cost more than the headline price.

The table below lists the published prices of 16 vendors as shown on their pricing pages on November 20, 2026. Prices change often; check the vendor's page before buying. Perpetual and on-premise licenses

A few vendors (Kickidler, StaffCop, Veriato, CurrentWare and others on request) sell perpetual licenses or on-premise deployments. Perpetual licenses cost roughly two to three years of subscription up front, plus annual maintenance for updates and support, and require your own servers and storage. They appeal to organizations that cannot send monitoring data to a vendor's cloud, such as government contractors, banks and some healthcare employers, and to buyers who prefer capital to operating expense. Over five years the total cost is usually similar to a subscription once hardware, maintenance and administrator time are included.

What drives the real cost

  • Seat minimums: Teramind (5), Veriato (5 and 20), Insightful's Insights plan (50) and CurrentWare's $1,440 minimum raise the cost for small teams.
  • Annual-only billing: ActivTrak and several others bill only annually, so seasonal headcount costs a full year.
  • Data retention: entry plans often keep screenshots or activity data for one to three months; longer history costs more (ActivTrak $1 per user per extra year, Apploye $2, Hubstaff's add-on). Retention also has legal implications; see how long to keep monitoring data.
  • Add-ons: AI features, data loss prevention, extra storage and premium support are frequently extra.
  • Administrator time: configuring productivity categories, reviewing alerts and answering employee questions takes hours each week; insider-risk tools need trained analysts.
  • Legal and change management: policies, notices, consent forms, works council negotiations for EU staff, and the cost of lost trust if the rollout is handled badly. See rolling out tracking without losing trust.

How to choose a price tier

  1. Start from the problem: billing hours, attendance, productivity insight, security investigations or compliance. Each points to a different tier.
  2. Buy the least intrusive tool that solves it; screenshots and keystroke logging cost more in trust and legal exposure than in license fees, and are restricted in the UK and EU.
  3. Check minimums, billing terms and retention before comparing headline prices.
  4. Pilot with a small team on a trial before signing an annual contract.
  5. Budget the policy, notice and training work alongside the license; see the monitoring policy template.

Key takeaways

  • Employee monitoring software costs from free (3 to 10 users) to about $35 per user per month; most mid-market tools cost $5 to $20.
  • Basic tracking tools such as Hubstaff ($4.99 to $10), Apploye ($4.50 to $8) and EmpMonitor ($3 to $4.66) sit at the low end; analytics tools such as ActivTrak ($10 to $21) and Insightful ($8 to $24) in the middle; insider-risk tools such as Veriato ($18) and Teramind (about $14 to $32, not displayed on its own page) at the top.
  • Seat minimums, annual-only billing, data retention limits and add-ons often change the real cost more than the headline price.
  • Perpetual and on-premise licenses from Kickidler, StaffCop, Veriato and CurrentWare cost two to three years of subscription up front plus maintenance and infrastructure.
  • At 50 users, expect about $3,000 a year for basic tracking, $6,000 to $10,000 for analytics and $18,000 for insider risk, plus administrator time.

Frequently asked questions

How much does employee monitoring software cost?

Most tools cost between $5 and $20 per user per month on annual billing, with free plans for very small teams and insider-risk tools up to about $35. For 50 users that is roughly $3,000 to $12,000 a year, or up to about $20,000 for insider-risk software.

Is there free employee monitoring software?

Yes, with limits. ActivTrak is free for up to 3 workers and Apploye for up to 10 users, with short data retention. Many time trackers have free plans but do not include monitoring features such as screenshots or app tracking.

Why is Teramind's price not shown on its website?

As of November 2026, Teramind's official pricing page shows plan names without figures. Third-party guides report Starter at about $14, UAM at about $28 and DLP at about $32 per seat per month on annual billing, with a 5-seat minimum; confirm with Teramind before buying.

Is perpetual monitoring software cheaper than a subscription?

Not usually over five years. Perpetual licenses cost about two to three years of subscription up front, plus annual maintenance, servers and administrator time. They suit organizations that must keep monitoring data on their own infrastructure.

What hidden costs come with monitoring software?

Seat minimums, annual-only contracts, paid data retention, add-ons such as AI and DLP, required support plans, administrator time for configuration and alert review, and the legal and change-management work of policies, notices and employee communication.

Which monitoring software is cheapest?

Among published prices in November 2026, EmpMonitor (from about $3 per user annually at volume), Apploye ($4.50) and Hubstaff ($4.99) were among the lowest, with free tiers from Apploye and ActivTrak for small teams.
