Tarun Kumar Reddy writes WorkIntel Hub's employee-side guides on workplace monitoring and privacy. Those are the questions people search for from the other side of the dashboard, whether an employer can read a Teams message, see a browsing history on the company Wi-Fi, reach into a personal phone, or tell when someone has gone idle. Each guide answers the question directly, separates what the software is technically capable of from what an employer is actually permitted to do, and says plainly where the honest answer is that it depends on the setup.

He also reviews WorkIntel Hub's monitoring and privacy coverage for accuracy. That work means checking each claim against the statute or agency guidance it rests on before it is published, and sending it back when a widely repeated assertion about surveillance law does not survive contact with the source. Articles that have been through that check carry a visible review line with the date it was done.

Areas of focus: US employee monitoring and electronic communications law, workplace privacy in practice, what monitoring software can and cannot capture across devices and networks, and state paid sick leave rules.
