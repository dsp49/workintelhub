# How to Use This Checklist
This document is a universal review framework. It is designed to evaluate every feature page, solution page, use-case page, and comparison page in the Time Champ website — covering 50+ pages consistently. Reviewers, writers, and SEO managers can use it at three stages: before writing (as a brief), during editing (as a review), and after publishing (as a QA audit).

### Three-stage usage model

### How to adapt this for any page
Step 1: Identify the page's Central Entity — the primary topic the page is built around (e.g. 'employee monitoring software', 'GPS tracking', 'timesheet management').
Step 2: Replace '[PAGE ENTITY]' in Section 3 criteria with that Central Entity before scoring.
Step 3: Identify the primary ICP persona for that page (see page type table in Appendix A) and check Section 4 criteria against that persona's language.
Step 4: Score every section. Log scores in the Summary Scorecard (page 3). Add reviewer name, date, and page URL.

Feature and solution pages serve two masters simultaneously: Google (traffic) and the buyer (leads). This section evaluates whether the page speaks in the ICP's own language and moves them toward a purchase decision. A page scoring below 14/22 here will generate traffic but not trials.



Section 4 score:  _____ / 22


Internal linking is how topical authority flows across the site. Each feature page must both send and receive link equity. A page with brilliant content but zero internal links is invisible to Google's topical authority model. Check both directions: links this page sends, and links this page receives.


Section 5 score:  _____ / 12


AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) means structuring content so Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini extract and cite it as a direct answer. AI engines look for: self-contained definitions, structured lists, comparison tables, and FAQ blocks with clean, concise answers. The checks below validate each trigger.


Section 6 score:  _____ / 11


Schema is the formal encoding of your content's meaning. It tells search engines and AI crawlers exactly what type of content this is, what it does, and who it's for — in machine-readable format. Each point is binary: it is either present and valid, or it is not.


Section 7 score:  _____ / 5


This section is a red-flag checklist, not a scored section. AI-generated content has predictable patterns — overused abstract nouns, hedging verbs, and filler transition phrases that no experienced copywriter would write. These words signal AI origin to both human readers and AI detection tools, and they erode brand credibility. Search the page text for every term below before approving.



Additional red-flag patterns to search for manually:
- Multiple paragraphs beginning with 'In addition to...' or 'Not only that...'
- Any sentence longer than 35 words (AI tends to over-connect clauses)
- Pairs of synonyms used side-by-side: 'clear and transparent', 'fast and efficient'
- Three-part lists that feel arbitrary: 'accuracy, efficiency, and reliability'
- Any paragraph that could appear unchanged on a competitor's site

Each feature page should contextually link to at least 3–5 pages below. These are the highest-priority internal link paths based on semantic closeness and topical authority flow. Links must appear in body copy — not only in navigation or footers.

| AI Cliché Word / Phrase | Why It Signals AI Writing | Replace With |
|---|---|---|
| Streamline / Streamlines | Default AI verb for 'make easier' — appears in nearly every AI-generated SaaS page | simplify, reduce steps in, cut the time spent on |
| Empower / Empowers | Vague activation verb. Means nothing without a specific outcome. | let your team, give managers the ability to, help teams |
| Leverage | Corporate filler — AI uses it where a simpler verb would do | use, apply, make the most of |
| In today's fast-paced world / In today's dynamic landscape | Classic AI opener. Zero semantic value. Repeats itself across millions of pages. | Remove entirely. Start with the reader's pain directly. |
| Seamless / Seamlessly | Overused SaaS adjective. No reader believes a product is truly 'seamless'. | without switching tools, in one step, without logging out |
| Robust | Hollow adjective — describes everything and nothing simultaneously | comprehensive, includes X Y and Z, covers every case where |
| Actionable insights | Buzzword pair. What insight is not actionable? Say what action it enables. | reports that show you exactly when to realign workloads |
| Cutting-edge / State-of-the-art | Empty superlative. Unprovable and distrusted by readers. | built for [specific use case], designed to handle [specific problem] |
| Game-changing / Revolutionary | Hyperbole that reduces credibility. No one believes their time-tracking tool is revolutionary. | the difference is measurable — [specific stat or outcome] |
| Holistic / End-to-end | AI filler adjective for 'covers everything'. Means nothing on its own. | from time tracking to payroll export, in a single platform |
| Unlock the potential of | Abstract benefit claim — AI's way of avoiding specifics | give your managers visibility into [specific thing] |
| Foster / Fostering | HR buzzword — sounds corporate, means nothing measurable | build, create, support, improve |
| Elevate your [X] | Vague directional verb. Elevate how? By how much? | reduce X by 30%, improve X with [specific feature] |
| At the forefront of | AI's version of 'we are leaders'. Unprovable. | remove or replace with a specific differentiator claim |
| Ensure / Ensuring | Passive-sounding guarantee language. Reads as corporate hedge. | track, verify, confirm, check — be specific about the mechanism |
| It is worth noting that | AI filler transition. Adds zero meaning — just delays the point. | Delete. State the point directly. |
| In order to | AI padding for 'to'. Two extra words, zero extra meaning. | Replace with 'to' every time. |
| Utilize / Utilise | Formal AI synonym for 'use'. Unnecessarily complex. | use — always |
| Furthermore / Moreover / Additionally | AI transition words that create a listicle feel in prose. | And, also, on top of this — or restructure the paragraph |
| Tailored to your needs / unique needs | AI's default personalisation phrase. Used on every competitor's site. | built for [specific team type], configurable for [specific workflow] |
| That being said | AI pivot phrase that signals a contradiction the writer isn't committing to | But, However — or restructure to avoid the hedge |
| It's important to note | AI filler — if it's important, just say it directly | Delete. State the important thing without the setup. |
| Dive deep into / Deep dive | Overused tech-blog idiom. Signals AI content farms. | explore, walk through, break down, examine |
| With that in mind | AI transition filler — adds no semantic content | Delete or restructure. Connect ideas directly. |
| Take your business to the next level | Vague aspiration — the oldest cliché in SaaS copywriting | specific outcome: 'cut payroll processing time by 60%' |
