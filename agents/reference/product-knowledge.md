# Time Champ — Knowledge Base & Writer's Guide

## What is Time Champ?

Time Champ is a **workforce intelligence platform** for employee monitoring, time tracking, and productivity that transforms raw work activity into actionable insights. Designed for agencies, BPOs, construction firms, architecture & engineering teams, freelancers, and any business managing remote, hybrid, or field teams.

**Core Value Proposition:** Give leaders visibility into how work happens — monitoring employees, activities, tracking time, productivity, attendance, and behaviour — so they can improve planning, reduce friction, and support teams proactively.

## Feature Modules — Source of Truth

### 1. ⏱ Time Tracking
Captures work hours automatically or manually across projects, tasks, and clients.
- Automatic time capture (no manual effort required)
- One-click task timer — start/stop with a single click
- Manual time logging and bulk log editing
- Offline time capture with automatic sync when reconnected
- Billable vs. non-billable hour classification at task level
- Task-based time tracking with descriptions
- Project & task-level time allocation reports
- Overtime tracking and automatic calculation
- Break scheduling with automatic reminders
- Timesheet auto-generation from tracked time
- Payroll-ready timesheet export

---

### 2. 📊 Productivity & Activity Monitoring
Reveals how employees actually spend their work time — apps, websites, and focus levels.
- Real-time activity monitoring (apps, websites, tasks)
- App & website usage analytics with time-spent breakdowns
- Productivity classification engine — label apps as productive, non-productive, or neutral
- Category-based usage insights (group apps by function)
- Percentage-based usage analysis
- Visual heatmaps — colour-coded by productive, away, and working time
- Activity intensity graphs (keystroke and mouse patterns)
- Idle time detection with configurable alert thresholds
- Timeline view — colour-coded hour-by-hour breakdown of each employee's day
- Work-life balance overview (focus time, breaks, overutilisation)
- Attrition risk insights (early disengagement signal detection)
- Team vs. individual productivity comparison

---

### 3. 🖥 Screen & Visual Oversight
Visual proof of work through screenshots and screen recordings for QA, compliance, and issue resolution.
- Automated periodic screenshots (configurable frequency)
- Manual screenshot capture on demand
- Screenshot blur to protect sensitive/private information
- Live screen viewing (Office TV) — see all employee screens in one dashboard
- Multi-monitor support
- Screen recordings with anomaly detection (activity spikes trigger recordings)
- Instant access to past recordings
- Offline recording with history sync
- Encrypted, secure data storage for all recordings
- Role-based access control — only authorised users can view/download
- Stealth mode — monitoring runs quietly in the background
- Effortless recording export (for audits, compliance, training)
- Non-intrusive design — respects workplace privacy

### 4. 📍 GPS & Field Operations
Tracks field employee locations, verifies job-site attendance, and manages territory coverage.
- Real-time GPS location tracking via mobile devices
- Geo-verified clock-ins and clock-outs
- Smart geofencing — restrict clock-ins to approved job sites, trigger alerts for zone violations
- Route and movement history tracking
- Visit management — log client visits with GPS-stamped time and outcomes
- Customer mapping and territory visibility (interactive map)
- Location-based expense logging with image proof
- Automated timesheet generation from GPS-tracked time
- Automated field reports (attendance, travel, visits, routes, spending)
- Comprehensive attendance monitoring with location backing
- Field expense management
- Reports for location history, time spent at client locations

Converts tracked work hours into accurate client invoices and financial records.

- Customisable billing rates — hourly, fixed fee, or role-based per client
- Set budgets by hours, pay rates, or bill rates
- Real-time budget alerts (notified when approaching limits)
- Budget vs. actuals reporting
- Automated timesheet-to-invoice conversion
- Automated payroll record generation from approved timesheets
- Client-level billing transparency reports

### 9. 🔒 Data Loss Prevention (DLP)
Monitors and protects sensitive data by tracking file activity, USB usage, and unauthorised web access.
- Website access violation monitoring with detailed logs
- Real-time USB insertion monitoring — block unauthorised/external USB devices
- Export USB and violation logs in XLSX, CSV, or PDF formats
- Comprehensive file monitoring (creation, modification, deletion with paths and timestamps)
- Upload/download violation alerts with domain and timestamp records
- Visual analytics graphs for violation summaries
- Advanced real-time compliance reports for audits
- Role-based access control and permissions management
- Suspicious activity detection with instant alerts for repeat violations
- Behaviour monitoring across website, file, and USB activity

Records and monitors audio for QA, compliance, and performance improvement.

- One-click audio recording

### 11. ⚠️ Alerts & Notifications
Real-time, configurable alerts for operational anomalies so managers can act immediately.

- Late logins
- Away time approval requests
- Idle time and long inactivity
- Productivity drops
- Non-productive and unauthorised app usage
- Over-utilisation and burnout risk
- Sensitive file and restricted access
- USB device connection
- Geofencing and zone boundary violations
- Daily activity summary reports
